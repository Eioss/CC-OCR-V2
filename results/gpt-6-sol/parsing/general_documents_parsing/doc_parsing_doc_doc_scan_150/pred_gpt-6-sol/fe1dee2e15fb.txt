\item \textbf{(3)}

Let \(H\) and \(h\) be the heights of the 2 candles.

Burnt amount at any time:
\begin{align*}
H:\quad \frac{120}{180}\times H&=\frac{2}{3}H\\
h:\quad \frac{120}{300}\times h&=\frac{2}{5}h
\end{align*}
Remaining:
\begin{align*}
H:\quad H-\frac{2}{3}H&=\frac{1}{3}H\\
h:\quad h-\frac{2}{5}h&=\frac{3}{5}h
\end{align*}
The ratio after 2 h is \(1:1\).
\[
\frac{\frac{1}{3}H}{\frac{3}{5}h}=\frac{1}{1}
\]
Cross multiply:
\[
\frac{1}{3}H=\frac{3}{5}h
\]
Cross multiply:
\begin{align*}
5H&=9h\\
\frac{H}{h}&=\frac{9}{5}
\end{align*}
\(H:h=9:5\)

\[
\frac{1-\frac{n}{180}}{1-\frac{n}{240}}=\frac{1}{2}
\]
Cross multiply:
\begin{align*}
2-\frac{n}{90}&=1-\frac{n}{240}\\
\frac{n}{90}-\frac{n}{240}&=2-1\\
\frac{8n-3n}{720}&=1\\
5n&=720\\
n&=144\ \text{min}=2\ \text{h}\ 24\ \text{min}
\end{align*}
