## Trigonometry

### 1) Trigonometry Ratios

In the right triangle shown, \(AB=\text{Opp}\), \(BC=\text{Adj}\), and \(AC=\text{Hyp}\).

\[
\begin{array}{lll}
\sin\theta=\dfrac{\mathrm{Opp}}{\mathrm{Hyp}}&
\cos\theta=\dfrac{\mathrm{Adj}}{\mathrm{Hyp}}&
\tan\theta=\dfrac{\mathrm{Opp}}{\mathrm{Adj}}\\[6pt]
\cot\theta=\dfrac{\mathrm{Adj}}{\mathrm{Opp}}&
\sec\theta=\dfrac{\mathrm{Hyp}}{\mathrm{Adj}}&
\cosec\theta=\dfrac{\mathrm{Hyp}}{\mathrm{Opp}}
\end{array}
\]

### 2) Imp. Results

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{i)}\ \sin\theta\cdot\cosec\theta=1&
\text{ii)}\ \cos\theta\cdot\sec\theta=1\\
\text{iii)}\ \tan\theta\cdot\cot\theta=1&
\text{iv)}\ \tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\\[5pt]
\text{v)}\ \cot\theta=\dfrac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}&
\text{vi)}\ \sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1\\
\text{vii)}\ \sec^2\theta=1+\tan^2\theta&
\text{viii)}\ \cosec^2\theta=1+\cot^2\theta\\
\text{ix)}\ \pi^\circ=180^\circ&
\text{x)}\ \text{length of arc }s=r\theta\\
\text{xi)}\ \text{Area of sector}=\dfrac12r^2\theta
\end{array}
\]

### 5) Allied - Angles

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\sin(-\alpha)=-\sin\alpha&
\sin(90^\circ-\alpha)=\cos\alpha\\
\cos(-\alpha)=\cos\alpha&
\cos(90^\circ-\alpha)=\sin\alpha\\
\tan(-\alpha)=-\tan\alpha&
\tan(90^\circ-\alpha)=\cot\alpha\\
\cot(-\alpha)=-\cot\alpha&
\cot(90^\circ-\alpha)=\tan\alpha\\
\sec(-\alpha)=\sec\alpha&
\sec(90^\circ-\alpha)=\cosec\alpha\\
\cosec(-\alpha)=-\cosec\alpha&
\cosec(90^\circ-\alpha)=\sec\alpha
\end{array}
\]

### 5) Compound Angles

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sin(A+B)&=\sin A\cos B+\cos A\sin B,\\
\sin(A-B)&=\sin A\cos B-\cos A\sin B,\\
\cos(A+B)&=\cos A\cos B-\sin A\sin B,\\
\cos(A-B)&=\cos A\cos B+\sin A\sin B,\\
\tan(A+B)&=\frac{\tan A+\tan B}{1-\tan A\tan B},\\
\tan(A-B)&=\frac{\tan A-\tan B}{1+\tan A\tan B}.
\end{aligned}
\]

### 6) Multiple Angle and Sub Multiple Angle

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sin2A&=2\sin A\cos A,\\
\cos2A&=\cos^2A-\sin^2A=1-2\sin^2A=2\cos^2A-1,\\
\tan2A&=\frac{2\tan A}{1-\tan^2A},\\
\sin A&=2\sin\frac A2\cos\frac A2,\\
\cos A&=\cos^2\frac A2-\sin^2\frac A2\\
&=1-2\sin^2\frac A2\\
&=2\cos^2\frac A2-1\\
&=\frac{1-\tan^2(A/2)}{1+\tan^2(A/2)},\\
\sin A&=\frac{2\tan(A/2)}{1+\tan^2(A/2)},\\
\tan A&=\frac{2\tan(A/2)}{1-\tan^2(A/2)},\\
1-\cos A&=2\sin^2\frac A2.
\end{aligned}
\]

### 7) Triple Angle Formulae

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sin3\theta&=3\sin\theta-4\sin^3\theta,&
\cos3\theta&=4\cos^3\theta-3\cos\theta,\\
\tan3\theta&=\frac{3\tan\theta-\tan^3\theta}{1-3\tan^2\theta},\\
\cos^3\theta&=\frac{\cos3\theta+3\cos\theta}{4},&
\sin^3\theta&=\frac{3\sin\theta-\sin3\theta}{4}.
\end{aligned}
\]

### 8) Factorization Formulae

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sin C+\sin D&=2\sin\frac{C+D}{2}\cos\frac{C-D}{2},\\
\sin C-\sin D&=2\cos\frac{C+D}{2}\sin\frac{C-D}{2},\\
\cos C+\cos D&=2\cos\frac{C+D}{2}\cos\frac{C-D}{2}.
\end{aligned}
\]

### 9) Defactorization Formulae

\[
\begin{aligned}
2\sin A\cos B&=\sin(A+B)+\sin(A-B),\\
2\cos A\sin B&=\sin(A+B)-\sin(A-B),\\
2\cos A\cos B&=\cos(A+B)+\cos(A-B),\\
2\sin A\sin B&=\cos(A-B)-\cos(A+B).
\end{aligned}
\]
