1) The range of the function \(\dfrac{1}{1-2\sin x}\) is

   a) \((-\infty,-1)\cup\left(\dfrac13,\infty\right)\)  
   b) \(\left(-1,\dfrac13\right)\)  
   c) \(\left[-1,\dfrac13\right]\)  
   d) \((-\infty,-1]\cup\left[\dfrac13,\infty\right)\)

2) The function \(f:[0,2\pi]\to[-1,1]\) defined by \(f(x)=\sin x\) is

   a) one-to-one \(\qquad\) b) onto \(\qquad\) c) bijection \(\qquad\) d) cannot be defined

3) The solution set of the inequality \(|x-1|\geq|x-3|\) is

   a) \([0,2]\) \(\qquad\) b) \([2,\infty)\) \(\qquad\) c) \((0,2)\) \(\qquad\) d) \((-\infty,2)\)

4) If \(3\) is the logarithm of \(343\), then the base is

   a) \(5\) \(\qquad\) b) \(7\) \(\qquad\) c) \(6\) \(\qquad\) d) \(9\)

5) In a \(\triangle ABC\), \(\tan\left(\dfrac A2\right)=\)

   a) \(\sqrt{\dfrac{(s-b)(s-c)}{bc}}\)  
   b) \(\sqrt{\dfrac{s(s-a)}{bc}}\)  
   c) \(\sqrt{\dfrac{(s-b)(s-c)}{s(s-a)}}\)  
   d) \(\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}\)

6) There are \(10\) points in a plane and \(4\) of them are collinear. Then the number of straight lines joining any two points is

   a) \(45\) \(\qquad\) b) \(40\) \(\qquad\) c) \(39\) \(\qquad\) d) \(38\)

7) The \(n^{\text{th}}\) term of the sequence \(1,2,4,7,11,\ldots\) is

   a) \(n^3+3n^2+2n\)  
   b) \(n^3-3n^2+3n\)  
   c) \(\dfrac{n(n+1)(n+2)}{3}\)  
   d) \(\dfrac{n^2-n+2}{2}\)

8) The slopes of the line which makes an angle \(45^\circ\) with the line \(3x-y=-5\) are

   a) \(1,-1\) \(\qquad\) b) \(\dfrac12,-2\) \(\qquad\) c) \(1,\dfrac12\) \(\qquad\) d) \(2,-\dfrac12\)
