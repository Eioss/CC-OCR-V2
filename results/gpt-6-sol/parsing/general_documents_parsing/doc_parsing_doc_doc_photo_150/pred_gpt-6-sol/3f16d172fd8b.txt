\begin{enumerate}
\item[48.] Pre-Ur III, dated (Umma).

Baked clay envelope, still intact and well preserved. Light brown. \(42 \times 42 \times 26\) mm. Obv. 3 ll.: one on top, one in center and one at bottom. Rev. 2 ll.: one on top and one in center. Covered with seal impression.

Šubati document: food provision.

Month: iti ur (Umma calendar, IX).

Date formula: mu 4 kam úš.

\hspace*{2em}Month name: \textit{CCTE}, p. 177; d.f.: unidentified: Schneider, 39, 6, f; \textit{CCTE}, p. 175 (I 28).

\hspace*{2em}Seal impression: Lugal-ga-mu/dumu Ur-\textsuperscript{d}Šará.

\item[49.] Ur III, dated (Umma).

Baked clay tablet. Light reddish brown. Complete and well preserved. \(22 \times 20 \times 11\) mm. Obv. 5 ll. rev. 2 ll.

Mu-DU document: asses.

Month: RI (Umma calendar, V).

\item[50.] Ur III, dated (Umma ?).

Baked clay tablet. Light reddish brown. \(25 \times 24 \times 12\) mm. Complete and fairly well preserved, except for a notch on obverse. Obv. 6 ll., rev. 4 ll.

Income record: Livestock (ki-x).

Date formula: Shulgi, 37.

\hspace*{2em}Obv. 1: ⌜udu⌝ bar-rug-ga cf. \textit{CCTE}, note 94; \textit{SETU}, 130: 181; obv. 3-4: máš šu-nir \textsuperscript{d}Nin-ur₄-ra cf. \textit{SETU}, 130 (from Umma) ll. 188–189.

\item[51.] Ur III, dated (Umma ?).

Baked clay tablet. Reddish brown. \(45 \times 42 \times 12\) mm. Complete and well preserved. Obv. 6 ll., rev. 3 ll. Covered with a seal impression.

Endorsed assignment of day labourers (kišib; ugula).

Date formula: Šu-Sîn, 7.

\hspace*{2em}Cf. parallel, \textit{CCTE}, KK, 11; obv. 3: šu-ur-ra cf. \textit{CCTE}, D 24, a.

\hspace*{2em}Seal impression: name of scribe is Šà-nin-gá dumu lugal-la: (cf. \textit{CCTE}, KK 11: 11; E 21: 8).

\item[52.] Ur III, dated (Puzriš-Dagān).

Baked clay tablet. Reddish brown. Complete and well preserved. \(32 \times 30 \times 14\) mm. Obv. 5 ll., rev. 8 ll.

Balanced account (níg-ŠID-ak) document: grain.

Months: Maš-kù-kù and šeš-da-kú (Ur calendar, I-II).

Date formula: Shulgi, 37 (Schneider 34, Bd).

\hspace*{2em}Puzriš-Dagān: A. Goetze, Ṣelluš-Dagān cf. \textit{JCS} 17 (1963), pp. 34–35.

\item[53.] Ur III, dated (Puzriš-Dagān).

Baked clay tablet. Light brown. \(30 \times 32 \times 15\) mm. Obv. 6 ll., rev. 6 ll., Chipped at lower left corner.

Ì-díb document: grain-fed oxen.

Month: ezen An-na (Ur calendar, X).

Date formula: Shulgi, 46 (Schneider 43, Bd).

Photo: Pl. 41.

\item[54.] Ur III, dated (Puzriš-Dagān).

Baked clay tablet. Bleached white. Complete. \(31 \times 27 \times 13\) mm. Obv. 6 ll., rev. 7 ll.

Šubati document: killed livestock.

Month: šeš-da-kú (Ur calendar, II).

Date formula: Shulgi, 47 (Schneider 44 A, a).

\hspace*{2em}Rev. 2: Ur-nigin-gar, receiver of killed animals from Shulgi 44 to AMAR-Sîn 3, cf. \textit{SETU}, p. 223 (W. W. Hallo, \textit{HUCA} 29 (1959), pp. 80–81); cf. also our Nos. 56, 57.
\end{enumerate}
