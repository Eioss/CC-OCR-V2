DICOTYLEDONEÆ. AGLAIA.

10. A. Wallichii Hiern in Hook. Flor. Brit. Ind. pars 3, p. 555; foliis breviter petiolatis, 2-3-jugis; foliolis oppositis, breviter petiolulatis, anguste ellipticis vel lanceolato-oblongis, basi æquali acutis, apice obtusiuscule cuspidatis, utrinque glabris, sed ad nervum centralem adpresse lepidotis; paniculis breviter pedunculatis, folia superantibus æquantibusve, laxe pyramido-ramosis, adpresse lepidotis, floribus breviter pedicellatis, subglobosis; calyce profunde obtuse dentato, extus parce stellato-lepidoto; petalis glabris, obovatis; tubo petala dimidia paullo superante subgloboso-urceolato, integro, extus glabro, intus inferne puberulo; antheris 5, parvis, ovario brevi stellato-lepidoto. Sapindus lepidotus Wall. Cat.

Rami glabri, leves, argillacei. Folia 12 cent. longa; foliola rigidula, opaca, in sicco argillacea, subæqualia, circiter 8 cent. longa, vix 2 cent. lata, nervis secundariis subpatulis, utrinque circiter 12. Paniculæ subangulosæ, adpresse lepidotæ, circiter 1 mill. crassæ. Petiolus 5 mill. longus, semiteres, adpresse lepidotus. Paniculæ pedunculis circiter 25 mill. longis, ramique graciles, leviter sulcati. Petala 5, obtusa, 1 mill. excedentia. Antheræ ovato-acutæ, minutæ.

In Sima (Wallich, n. 8036 in herb. Kew).
β. brevipetala: paniculis quam folia paullo brevioribus. Griffith, n. 1051 in herb. Kew.

11. A. andamanica (Kurz Journ. As. Soc. v. 44, p. 200); foliis longe petiolatis, 2-jugis; foliolis alternis suboppositisve oppositisve, ovatis vel oblongo-ellipticis, basi æquali cuneato-acutis, apice cuspidatis, utrinque glabris; paniculis æquantibus folia.
