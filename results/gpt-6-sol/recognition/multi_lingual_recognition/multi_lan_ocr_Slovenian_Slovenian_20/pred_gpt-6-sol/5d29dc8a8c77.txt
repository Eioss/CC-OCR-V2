‘MENOMASWELU POLAYA E TSHEGA’ – FS Khumou

POTSO 9: DIPOTSO TSE DIKHUTSHWANE
‘MOTHO’ – A Mogorosi le ba bangwe

9.1 Go tlhoka botshepegi le mete ga motho. (2)

9.2 Maikutlo a kutlobotlhoko – ka ntlha ya bonkgate ke tlhame jwa motho. Motho ga a tshepagale, ga a ikwise e se naga. (2)

9.3 Neeletsano – go golaganya mela e mebedi ntle le go dirisa lekopanyi. (2)

9.4 Tshwantshanyo – Go tshwantshanya dilo tse pedi tse di tshwanang ka bokao jaaka ba tshwantshanya mokgwa o ba itayang ka ona le wa sethunya. (2)

9.5 Motho ga a ikwise e se naga/motho o fetogafeloga bonologa a ikanyege.
