\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\Lambda^{3}(u\cdot\nabla\theta)\Lambda^{3}\theta\,dx

&=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}[\Lambda,u_i]\partial_i\theta\Lambda^{5}\theta\,dx

&=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}[\Lambda,u_i]\partial_i\theta\Lambda^{5}\theta\,dx
+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}[\Lambda^{2},u_i]\Lambda\partial_i\theta\Lambda^{3}\theta\,dx\\
&\quad+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}u_i\Lambda^{3}\partial_i\theta\Lambda^{3}\theta\,dx\\

+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}[\Lambda^{2},u_i]\Lambda\partial_i\theta\Lambda^{3}\theta\,dx\\

+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}[\Lambda^{2},u_i]\Lambda\partial_i\theta\Lambda^{3}\theta\,dx\\
