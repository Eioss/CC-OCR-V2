For each of the following formulae, choose the sentence that provides the best English translation of
the formula according to the following symbolization key:

\begin{tabular}{|c|l|}
\hline
$B$ & Bob is gathering eggs. \\
\hline
$C$ & Bob has fed the chickens. \\
\hline
$D$ & Bob has fed the ducks. \\
\hline
$F$ & Bob is chasing the fox away. \\
\hline
$P$ & The ducks are playing in the pond. \\
\hline
$Q$ & The ducks are quacking loudly. \\
\hline
$S$ & The chickens are playing in the pond. \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\textbf{Question 10}

\[
(F \rightarrow (Q \mathbin{\&} \neg S))
\]

\begin{flushright}
Select one answer.\\
2 points
\end{flushright}

\noindent\textbf{A.} If the ducks are quacking loudly and the chickens aren't playing
in the pond, then Bob is chasing the fox away.

\noindent\textbf{B.} The ducks are quacking loudly and the chickens aren't playing in the pond unless Bob is
chasing the fox away.

\noindent\textbf{C.} The ducks are quacking loudly and the chickens aren't playing in the pond if Bob is
chasing the fox away.

\noindent\textbf{D.} The ducks are quacking loudly and the chickens aren't playing in the pond only if Bob is
chasing the fox away.

\textbf{Question 11}

\[
((P \mathbin{\&} Q) \mathbin{\&} S)
\]

\begin{flushright}
Select one answer.\\
2 points
\end{flushright}

\noindent\textbf{A.} The ducks are quacking loudly and playing in the pond, and the
chickens are also playing in the pond.
