\textbf{Enrichment Worksheet}

\textbf{Formulas For a Line}

You can think of the slope-intercept form of an equation for a
line, $y = mx + b$, as a formula for the line given $m$ and $b$.

\textbf{Example:} Write the formula for a line given two points $(x_{1}, y_{1})$
and $(x_{2}, x_{2})$.

Use the idea that the slope of a line is the same
between any two points.
\[
\frac{y - y_{1}}{x - x_{1}} = \frac{y_{2} - y_{1}}{x_{2} - x_{1}}
\]

Multiply each side by $(x - x_{1})$.
\[
y - y_{1} = \frac{y_{2} - y_{1}}{x_{2} - x_{1}}(x - x_{1})
\qquad
\begin{array}{l}
\text{Two-point}\\
\text{formula}
\end{array}
\]

If you know two points of a line, you can write an equation for the
line directly using this formula.
