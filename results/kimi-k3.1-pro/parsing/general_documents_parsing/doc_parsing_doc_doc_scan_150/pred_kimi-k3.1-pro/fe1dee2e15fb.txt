\qno{9.}{3}
Let $H$ and $h$ be the heights of the 2 candles.\\
Burnt amount at any time:
\[
\begin{array}{@{}l@{\quad}l@{}}
H: & \dfrac{120}{180}\times H=\dfrac{2}{3}H\\[6pt]
h: & \dfrac{120}{300}\times h=\dfrac{2}{5}h
\end{array}
\]
Remaining:
\[
\begin{array}{@{}l@{\quad}l@{}}
H: & H-\dfrac{2}{3}H=\dfrac{1}{3}H\\[6pt]
h: & h-\dfrac{2}{5}h=\dfrac{3}{5}h
\end{array}
\]

The ratio after 2 h is $1:1$.
\[
\frac{\frac{1}{3}H}{\frac{3}{5}h}=\frac{1}{1}
\]
Cross multiply:
\[
\frac{1}{3}H=\frac{3}{5}h
\]
Cross multiply:
\[
\begin{aligned}
5H&=9h\\
\frac{H}{h}&=\frac{9}{5}\\
H:h&=9:5
\end{aligned}
\]

\qno{10.}{1}
Height after $n$ min:
\[
\begin{array}{@{}l@{\quad}l@{}}
1^{\text{st}}: & \dfrac{n}{180}\\[6pt]
2^{\text{nd}}: & \dfrac{n}{240}
\end{array}
\]
Height remaining:
\[
\begin{array}{@{}l@{\quad}l@{}}
1^{\text{st}}: & 1-\dfrac{n}{180}\\[6pt]
2^{\text{nd}}: & 1-\dfrac{n}{240}
\end{array}
\]
The ratio became $1:2$.
\[
\frac{1-\frac{n}{180}}{1-\frac{n}{240}}=\frac{1}{2}
\]
Cross multiply:
\[
\begin{aligned}
2-\frac{n}{90}&=1-\frac{n}{240}\\[4pt]
\frac{n}{90}-\frac{n}{240}&=2-1\\[4pt]
\frac{8n-3n}{720}&=1\\
5n&=720\\
n&=144\text{ min}=2\text{ h }24\text{ min}
\end{aligned}
\]
Working backwards: 14 36 h
