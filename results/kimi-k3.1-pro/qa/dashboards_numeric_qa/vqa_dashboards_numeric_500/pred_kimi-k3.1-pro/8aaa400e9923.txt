According to the infographic, early childhood education offers three main developmental benefits:

- **Social and emotional:** Children develop relationships with adults and peers. The consistent presence of these people provides a sense of security, which encourages greater participation and cooperation.
- **Cognitive:** A developmental curriculum provides age-appropriate learning. Children build on existing skills and increase their knowledge, while teachers help them reach important developmental milestones.
- **Language:** Language develops rapidly between ages 1 and 5. Routine interactions with teachers and peers help develop language skills, and more advanced skills emerge as children’s conversations and vocabulary progress.
