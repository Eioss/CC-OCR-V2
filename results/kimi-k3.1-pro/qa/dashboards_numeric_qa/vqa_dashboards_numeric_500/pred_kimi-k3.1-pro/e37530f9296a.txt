According to the infographic, the Bureau of Labor Statistics calculates the fatality rate as:

\[
\text{Fatality rate} =
\frac{\text{Number of fatal injuries}}
{\text{Total hours worked by all employees during the calendar year}}
\times 200{,}000{,}000
\]

The result is the number of fatalities **per 100,000 full-time workers or equivalent**. The 200 million hours represents 100,000 workers each working 40 hours per week for 50 weeks.
