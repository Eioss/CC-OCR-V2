The image lists two emergency benefits for working Canadians:

- **Emergency Care Benefit:** **$900 bi-weekly for up to 15 weeks** for people who must stay home due to COVID-19 and do not have paid sick leave. This includes those who cannot access Employment Insurance (EI) because they are sick or are caring for a sick child or another sick person.
- **Emergency Support Benefit:** Up to **$5 billion in total funding** to support workers who are not eligible for EI and are facing unemployment or reduced hours. This is a program funding amount, not an individual payment.
