The table summarizes **class performance after a course on Food and Nutrition**, measured as the **percentage of answers agreeing with the course’s teachings** on a 50-question test.

It compares the **1975 and 1976 classes** across three categories:
- Established economic or scientific facts
- Confirmed scientific findings still rejected by consumer activists
- Issues pitting professional scientists’ opinions against consumer activists’ charges

Overall agreement with the course teachings was **84% for the 1975 class** and **81% for the 1976 class**.
