This is a **Discipline Review Letter** from the **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** to **Takeda Global Research & Development Center, Inc.**

It concerns **NDA 21-073/S-026**, a supplemental new drug application for **Actos® (pioglitazone HCl) tablets**. The FDA summarizes its clinical review and explains proposed labeling changes, particularly regarding how results from the **PROactive trial** should be presented.

It is **regulatory review correspondence, not an approval letter**. The stamp indicates Takeda received it on **February 19, 2007**.
