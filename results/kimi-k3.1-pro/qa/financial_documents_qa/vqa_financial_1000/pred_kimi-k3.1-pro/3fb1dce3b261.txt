The document is an **audit agenda for DePuy Orthopaedics, Inc. in Warsaw, Indiana**, for a **Production & Process Controls audit scheduled for 13–17 July 2009**.

Its purpose is to:
- Assess compliance with **21 CFR 820** and **ISO 13485:2003** in selected areas of the Quality Management System.
- Evaluate whether the production and process controls subsystem has been effectively implemented.
- Review the effectiveness of corrective actions identified in previous internal audits, where appropriate.

It is **not intended as a full quality-system assessment**, although other elements may be examined if identified during the audit, and the company may request particular areas of focus.
