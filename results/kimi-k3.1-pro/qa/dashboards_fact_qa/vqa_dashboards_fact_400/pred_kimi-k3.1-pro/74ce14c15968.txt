Ban Ki-moon’s **Education First** initiative, launched through the United Nations, has three main priorities:

1. **Put every child in school** — remove barriers such as poverty, hunger, gender inequality, and lack of classrooms.
2. **Improve the quality of learning** — provide well-trained teachers, better learning materials, and stronger literacy and numeracy skills.
3. **Foster global citizenship** — promote peace, tolerance, respect for diversity, community participation, and environmental responsibility.

The infographic also emphasizes **closing the education financing gap** by increasing investment and using resources effectively.
