\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\textwidth}
    \textbf{\Large \colorbox{sectionred}{\textcolor{white}{5}} \hspace{0.2cm} PRESENT PERFECT}
    \vspace{0.3cm}

    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.5}
    \begin{tabular}{|>{\bfseries}l|p{5.5cm}|}
        \hline
        Form & have or has + Past Participle \\
        \hline
        Use & For actions which started in the past and continued up to the present. \\
        \hline
        Signal words & just, already, yet, for, since, so far, recently, ever... \\
        \hline
    \end{tabular}
    \vspace{0.2cm}

    {\footnotesize NB. We use Present Perfect Continuous (have/has+ been + verb +ing) to give more emphasis on the duration of the action. However, some verbs can't be used in the continuous form such as own, smell, need, belong, cost, agree, contain..., etc.}
    \vspace{0.3cm}

    \textbf{\colorbox{sectionred}{\textcolor{white}{\small EXERCISE \quad 1}} \underline{Find the signal words for present perfect.}}
    \vspace{0.2cm}

    1. Which is a signal word for present perfect? \\
    still -- usually -- since

    2. Which is \textbf{not} a signal word for present perfect? \\
    since -- before -- for
\end{minipage}
