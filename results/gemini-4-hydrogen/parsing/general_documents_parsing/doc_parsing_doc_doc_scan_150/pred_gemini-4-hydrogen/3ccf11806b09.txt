\noindent Smartphone Camera Flash

\columnbreak

\noindent{\large\sffamily\color{awinicblue}\textbf{GENERAL DESCRIPTION}}
\vspace{4pt}

\noindent The AW36526 is a inductorless triple channel flash \& Indicator 2in1 LED driver that provides a high level of adjustability within a ultra-small solution size. The AW36526 includes two current sources and a current sink which is controlled independently by $\text{I}^2\text{C}$-compatible interface. The AW36526 provides IVFM protection mode to prevent system reset or shutdown under low battery condition.

\vspace{8pt}
\noindent The AW36526 is controlled via an $\text{I}^2\text{C}$-compatible interface. The main features of the AW36526 include: flash / torch / IR / Indicator current, flash timeout duration, IVFM and breath features. The AW36526 also provides hardware flash pin (STROBE) to control flash event.

\vspace{8pt}
\noindent The device operates over a $-40^\circ\text{C}$ to $+85^\circ\text{C}$ ambient temperature range.

\vspace{8pt}
\noindent The AW36526 is available in small 0.4mm pitch 1.6mm$\times$1.2mm FCQFN-10L package.
