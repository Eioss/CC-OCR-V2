This is a **Discipline Review Letter** from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), under the Department of Health & Human Services. 

It is addressed to Takeda Global Research & Development Center, Inc. regarding their supplemental New Drug Application (NDA 21-073/S-026) for **Actos® (pioglitazone HCl)** tablets, and it outlines the FDA's review and rationale for changes to the proposed drug labeling.
