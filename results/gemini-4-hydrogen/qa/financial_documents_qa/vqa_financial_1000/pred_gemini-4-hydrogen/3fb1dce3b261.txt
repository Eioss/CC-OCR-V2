This document is an **Audit Agenda** for **DePuy Orthopaedics, Inc.** in Warsaw, IN, scheduled for **13–17 July 2009**. 

Specifically, it outlines the objective and scope for a **Production & Process Controls** audit:

* **Audit Objective:** To assess compliance with **21CFR820** and **ISO 13485:2003** for selected areas of the Quality Management System.
* **Audit Scope:** 
  * Evaluating whether the quality management subsystem for Production and Process Controls has been effectively implemented.
  * Reviewing the effectiveness of items previously identified for corrective actions through internal audits.
  * Clarifying that it is not a full assessment of the entire Quality System, though the company may choose specific areas within the scope for particular focus.
