Based on the infographic, the figure is **22.5%** (though note that this specifically represents the percentage of **businesses** that expect **10% or more of their workforce** to continue working remotely once the pandemic is over, rather than the percentage of individual workers). 

For context on the other numbers:
* **16.6%** is the proportion of businesses with 10%+ of their workforce working remotely prior to February 1, 2020.
* **32.6%** is the proportion of businesses with 10%+ of their workforce working remotely on May 29, 2020.
