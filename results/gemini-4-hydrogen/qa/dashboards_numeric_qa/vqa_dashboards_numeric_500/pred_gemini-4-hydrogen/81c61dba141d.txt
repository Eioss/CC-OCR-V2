Australia has **6 states** (and 2 mainland territories). 

As shown at the bottom of the image, the **6 states** are:
* **NSW** (New South Wales)
* **VIC** (Victoria)
* **QLD** (Queensland)
* **WA** (Western Australia)
* **SA** (South Australia)
* **TAS** (Tasmania)

The other two listed—**NT** (Northern Territory) and **ACT** (Australian Capital Territory)—are territories, not states.
