Based on the infographic, the **long-term benefits** of early childhood education are divided into two main areas:

### 1. Student Success
* **Higher graduation rates:** Students are **29% more likely** to graduate from high school.
* **Fewer repeated grades:** Students are **44% less likely** to repeat a grade.
* **Better test performance:** Students **pass standardized tests more frequently**.

### 2. Adult Responsibility
* **Better employment:** Adults age 40 who had early childhood education are **more likely to be employed**.
* **Higher income:** These 40-year-olds also have a **33% higher average income**.
* **Lower crime rates:** 18-year-olds *without* early childhood education are 70% more likely to be arrested for a violent crime (meaning those with early childhood education are significantly less likely to be arrested for violent crimes).
