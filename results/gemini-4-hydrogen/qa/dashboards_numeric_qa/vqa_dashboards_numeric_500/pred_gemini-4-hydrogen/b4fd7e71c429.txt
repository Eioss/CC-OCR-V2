Based on the "Incidence by Age" chart in the infographic, **82%** of cancer incidences in women occur in those over the age of 50. 

This is calculated by adding the percentages for the age groups over 50:
* **50 to 64 yrs:** 27%
* **65 to 74 yrs:** 25%
* **Over 74 yrs:** 30%

*(27% + 25% + 30% = 82%, or 100% - 18% for those under 50)*
