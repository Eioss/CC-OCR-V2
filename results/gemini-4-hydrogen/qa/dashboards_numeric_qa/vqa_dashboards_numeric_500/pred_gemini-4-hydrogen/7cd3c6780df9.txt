Based on the infographic, it depends on what type of "new things" you are referring to:

* **Trying new products/items:** **Generation Z** is more likely to experiment and purchase items they would not usually buy online (**61%** of Gen Z compared to **52%** of Millennials).
* **Trying new online retailers:** **Millennials** are slightly more likely to try out new online retailers (**35%** of Millennials compared to **32%** of Gen Z).
