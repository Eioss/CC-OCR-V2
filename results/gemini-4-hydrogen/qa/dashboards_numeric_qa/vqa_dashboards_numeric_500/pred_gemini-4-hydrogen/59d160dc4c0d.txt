According to the provided infographic from il bureau, party candidates for president are officially chosen at the **National Party Conventions**. 

As detailed under the "How Do The Candidates Win?" section of the graphic, the process happens in two main steps:
1. The electorate first votes for presidential nominees at **Caucuses and State Primaries**.
2. Party delegates from each state then attend the **National Party Conventions** to officially select their party's presidential candidate.
