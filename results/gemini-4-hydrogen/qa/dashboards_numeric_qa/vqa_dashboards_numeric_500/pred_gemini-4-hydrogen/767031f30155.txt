Based on the image, under the **Working Canadians** section, there are two emergency benefits provided:

1. **Emergency Care Benefit**
   * **Amount:** Up to $900 bi-weekly for up to 15 weeks.
   * **Details:** For those who must stay home due to COVID-19 and do not have paid sick leave. This includes individuals who cannot access EI and are sick, or those taking care of a child or someone who is sick.

2. **Emergency Support Benefit**
   * **Amount:** Up to $5 billion in total support.
   * **Details:** For workers who are not eligible for EI and are facing unemployment and reduced hours.
