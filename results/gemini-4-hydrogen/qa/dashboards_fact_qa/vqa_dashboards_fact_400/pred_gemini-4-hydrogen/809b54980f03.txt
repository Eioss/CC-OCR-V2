Based on the infographic, it depends on whether the food is unopened or opened:

* **Unopened:** **Canned dog food** has a longer shelf life (**18–24 months** from the date packaged) compared to dry dog food (**12–15 months**).
* **Once opened:** **Dry dog food** keeps much longer (**90 days** in a cool, dry place) compared to canned dog food (**3 days** refrigerated).
