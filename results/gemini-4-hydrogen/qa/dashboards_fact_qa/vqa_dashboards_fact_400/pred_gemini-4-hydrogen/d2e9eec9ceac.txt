Based on the infographic (under the **User Base** section), **"1/3 of all mobile phone users will access Instagram at least once a month in 2016."**

Subtracting this from the total (100%):
* $1 - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{3} \approx \mathbf{66.67\%}$ (or about **67%**)

*(Note: The infographic specifies "mobile phone users" rather than "smartphone users," and "at least once a month.")*
