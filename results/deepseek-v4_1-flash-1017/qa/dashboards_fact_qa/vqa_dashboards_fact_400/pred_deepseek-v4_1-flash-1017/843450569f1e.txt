Based on the infographic under the section **"THE GIFT VALUATION GAP"**:

*   The **Gift Price** (actual value) for the iPad is **$499**.
*   The **Value to Recipient** (perceived value) is **$409**.

To find the difference:
$499 - $409 = **$90**

The recipient perceives the iPad to be **$90** less valuable than its actual price.
