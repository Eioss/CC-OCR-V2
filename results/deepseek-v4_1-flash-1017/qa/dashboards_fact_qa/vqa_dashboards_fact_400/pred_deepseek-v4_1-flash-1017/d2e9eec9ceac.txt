Based on the infographic in the "User Base" section, the stat states:

**"1/3 of all mobile phone users will access Instagram at least once a month in 2016"**

To find the percentage of users who are **not** using it, you subtract that fraction from the whole:
1 (or 100%) - 1/3 = **2/3**

Converting 2/3 to a percentage:
**66.7%**

So, approximately **67%** of all mobile phone users are not using Instagram about once a month in 2016.
