Based on the infographic, the initiatives of Ban Ki-moon are part of the **Education First** campaign, which is described as "The United Nations Secretary-General's initiative."

The infographic outlines five main goals or pillars for this initiative:

1.  **Put every child in school:** This includes eliminating cost barriers, closing the gender gap, and building 4 million more classrooms.
2.  **Improve the quality of learning:** This focuses on training 2 million more teachers, providing better learning materials, and increasing early-childhood education programs.
3.  **Foster global citizenship:** The goal is to promote peace, tolerance, and environmental stewardship through education.
4.  **Close the financial gap:** This involves generating additional funding and increasing global investment in education to $24 billion per year.
5.  **Reduce poverty and hunger:** As stated in the quote at the top, putting "Education First" is seen as a way to reduce poverty, end wasted potential, and look forward to stronger societies.
