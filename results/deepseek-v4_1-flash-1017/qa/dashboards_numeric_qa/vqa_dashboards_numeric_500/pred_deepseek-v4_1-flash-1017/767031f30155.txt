Based on the image provided, the emergency benefits for working Canadians under the COVID-19 Economic Response Plan are:

1.  **Emergency Care Benefit:** This provides benefits for those who must stay home due to COVID-19 and don't have paid sick leave. It covers individuals who can't access EI and are sick, or those taking care of a child or someone who is sick. The benefit provides **$900 bi-weekly for up to 15 weeks**.

2.  **Emergency Support Benefit:** This benefit provides up to **$5 billion** in support to workers who are not eligible for EI and who are facing unemployment and reduced hours.

*   **$27 Billion** in direct support for Canadian Workers and Businesses.
