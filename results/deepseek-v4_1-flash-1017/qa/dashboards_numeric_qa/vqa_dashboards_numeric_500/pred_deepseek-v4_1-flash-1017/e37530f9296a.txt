Based on the footnote at the bottom of the infographic, the fatality rate is calculated as follows:

**The Formula:**
The rate is calculated by taking the **number of fatal injuries divided by the number of hours worked** by all employees during the calendar year, and then **multiplying that result by 200,000,000**.

**The Definition:**
This calculation results in a rate that represents the number of fatalities per **100,000 full-time workers** (or equivalent). The data is based on the Bureau of Labor Statistics' formula.
