Based on the "TYPE OF ENTREPRENEURS" section in the bottom right of the infographic, the two types are:

1.  **Opportunity-Driven:** These are entrepreneurs in markets with strong enabling conditions (like New Zealand, Canada, UK, etc.) that foster more opportunity-driven entrepreneurs.
2.  **Necessity-Driven:** These are entrepreneurs in markets with fewer supporting conditions (like Ghana, Brazil, Bangladesh, etc.) that have more necessity-driven entrepreneurs.
