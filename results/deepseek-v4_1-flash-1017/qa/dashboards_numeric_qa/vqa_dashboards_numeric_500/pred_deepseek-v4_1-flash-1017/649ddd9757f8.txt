Based on the final chart in the infographic titled "If a driverless car hits a person, who is at fault?", the majority of people believe the **driverless car manufacturer** is at fault.

Specifically, **73.72%** of respondents chose "The driverless car manufacturer," which is represented by the large orange section of the donut chart. This is significantly higher than the other options:
*   Those inside the car: 13.76%
