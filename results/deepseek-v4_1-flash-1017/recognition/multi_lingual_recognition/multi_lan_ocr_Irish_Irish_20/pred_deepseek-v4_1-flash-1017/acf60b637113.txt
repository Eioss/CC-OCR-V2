SECTION B: PRÓS

Ceist (b)
Cén sort duine é Tomás, dar leat? Déan cur síos gearr air agus inis
cén fath ar thaitin nó nár thaitin sé leat. (Is leor dhá fháth)

Freagra

Is cosúil gur duine an-dáiríre é Tomás. Bhí sé ag obair go dian ar an
bhfeirm i gcónaí. Ní raibh sé sásta fanacht ina shuí gan rud éigin a
dhéanamh. Chomh maith leis sin, bhí sé an-chróga agus thug sé
comhairle dá mhac ó am go ham. Ar an láimh eile, bhí sé rud beag
balbh agus ní raibh sé in ann a chuid mothúchán a chur in iúl dá mhac.
Mar sin, is duine é a raibh meas agam air ach níor thaitin a chuid
bealaí cumarsáide liom.

Ceist (iii)
Cén sort duine é Muirís, dar leat? Déan cur síos gairid air agus inis
cén fáth ar thaitin (nó nár thaitin) sé leat. (Is leor dhá fháth)

Freagra
Is mór an trua, dar liom, Muirís. Tá sé cliste. Bhí sé ábalta an phointí a bhí
sa phictiúr a thuiscint agus an chiall cheart a bhaint as an scéal. Níor thaitin a bheith
ag obair ar an bhfeirm leis. Ba mhaith leis a bheith saor chun a chuid ama a chaitheamh
ag léamh nó ag scríobh nó ag breathnú ar an teilifís. B'fhéidir gurbh fhearr leis a bheith
ag obair in oifig nó in ionad éigin eile ina mbeadh sé ag plé le daoine seachas a bheith
ag obair leis na hainmhithe. Ar an iomlán, ba mhaith liom a bheith ina chara leis mar
is duine é atá cineálta agus tuisceanach.

Le Buddy Jenkinson

Colúr an Scéil
................................................................................

This is an unusual story about a vet. She is called out one day to look at a
prize cock, called Oisín, who has won many competitions and who usually
likes to mate with all the hens. However, Oisín has not been in form lately
and has had no energy. Oisín is owned by Sally who is very proud of him
and boasts about him often.

The locals laugh at her and have nicknamed her husband Coilichín – the
little rooster – and jeer at him. When the vet examines the shed where
Oisín lives, she notices that the gaps in the wall have been blocked up and
that Oisín has been given seeds to eat – seeds that would make him drowsy.
The vet suspects that Sally's husband has tried to harm Oisín because he is
sick and tired of all the jeering.
