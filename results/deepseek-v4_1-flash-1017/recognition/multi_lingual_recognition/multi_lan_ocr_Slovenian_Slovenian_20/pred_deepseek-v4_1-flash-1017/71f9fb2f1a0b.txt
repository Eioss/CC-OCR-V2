3.58  Bolo! mo merafeng ya tholego bo tshwanetse go tlhalogannya gela go tswa mo
ponalong ya tumelo ya seso, mme gape le ka kanaano ya gore ditumelo tsa bodumedi
di tshameka karolo e e botlhokwa mo kgokgong o bolo! bo tswang ka ona. Oupona o
tlhagisitse gore 'maitemogelo a semowa a Setorika ke a a ona 'motshepi' kgotsa
lefelo le bo itshetlegileng mo maitemogelong a letsatsi le letsatsi a motho wa
botho ka tsenelelo e kgolo ya gore bodumedi, seso, le setshaba di amana segolo. Go ya
ka Ellis le Ter Haar,

Go bopaki ka bokhara jwa gore Mafofrika a le mantsi a a iponela ba
tswelela go itshola ditumelo tse di ntshitsweng mo dikosomololng tsa
seso tseo ba di dirisang mo ditirong tsa bona tsa letsatsi le letsatsi, le fa
ba dula kwa diporofeng go le tirela botlhelelo, go tswa mo ditirong tsa
ditirelo tsa baagi kgotsa lefapha la sesweng la ekonomi.¹¹

Polelo eno e bontsha gore dikakanyo tsa seso, tsa mmele di santse di tshameka karolo e
kgolo mo matshelong a Mafofrika a sesweng.

3.59  Meleae e teng e e tlhalosang ka moo lephata la sedumedi le tshobegeseng ntiha ya
boloi. Ditshogo di tswa mo Dikerekeng tsa Tholego tsa Aforika (ATRs), dikereke tsa
borongwa, dikereke tsa Pentekosele, le badiratsi ba sasso ka katlego. Mo diAIC go na
le tumelo e kgolo mo nngwe teng go ba gosula. Bosula joo bo tswa jwaaka boloi, bakanye
ena ka ya bosula karolo ya puo e e ratang mo thutobomodimo, e bile e ka nna
gantsi mo ditlhong le mekgwa ya tlhaeletsano. Kgatelolo mo boruting e nogo
sodimosetso go dirisa maano otlhe a a tlamilweng ke kereke, go tshitshetsa kgathalang
le dilamorago tsa bosula jo. Thata e kgathang le botlhele. Fa motho a santswe a
amogele - ka ntlha ya dilamalo tsa gagwe - go dikelomelo go dirwa tse di
marara le ditlamorago tsa go tlhapiwa le go busetsa batho mo bolatong jwa bosetlho.
Kgato ya go alafa ga e samagane lela le motho ka esi nngwe gape le ikokobo e motho a
nnang mo go yona.

¹¹ Ellis S and Ter Haar G, Religion and politics in sub-Saharan Africa (1998) at 177.
