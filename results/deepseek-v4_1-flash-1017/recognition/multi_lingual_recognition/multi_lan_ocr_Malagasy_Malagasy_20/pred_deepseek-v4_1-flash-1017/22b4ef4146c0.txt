TOKO FAHA-7
Olo Maty ma
Mbala Hivilomiy?
Ino ahaizantsika fa olo maty
mbala hivilomiy?
Olo karakory ma hivelomño olo maty?
Olo karakory ma hivilomiy Zañahary?
OHATRA izio misy olo fatatra ndreky farany ratsy faha-
hy mariñana anao. Efa hainao fa maro, olo nívonený.
Anao mamango milomay fa izy mbala avy io. Farany
anao lany hevitry. Tio fo, misy lelahy araiky navy hana-
votro anao. Izy matanijaka mandilatra olo matiñana anao
2 Misy fahavalo araiky manilña atsika jiaby. Tsy ino
izio fa fahafatesana. Atsika tsy mahavita misakana izio
tsy ho avy. Efa maro koa olo haintsika nazony. Fa Je-
hovah mahery mandilatra fahafatesana. Izy tena tsara
fahahy, fotony izy efa nanano raha hahavotana atsika,
baka atsika tsy hofoaty. Ary i korintianina 15:26 mi-
volana fa: "Fahafatesana, to fahavalo farany hiofany
Zañahary, tena mampaparavoravo vaoao zey!"
3 Atsika koa fa maharety olo haintsika naty, atsika
tena malahelo. Nahatsara izy, Jehovah efa nivolana fa
olo naty aby ijy mbala hivilomiy. (Isaia 26:19) Baka
amy zery mahavy atsika mahantera fa iro to mbala ho
veloño.

hatao karakory fahavalo io?
