\rowcolor{headerpink} \textbf{Use} & For actions which started in the past and continued up to the present. \\ \hline

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \small NB. We use Present Perfect Continuous (have/has+ been + verb +ing) to give more emphasis on the duration of the action. However, some verbs can't be used in the continuous form such as own, smell, need, belong, cost, agree, contain..., etc.
