Based on the newspaper article provided, Lazlo Toth was arrested for **"industrial espionage."**

The article states:

> "Almost a year after his arrest in Yugoslavia for 'industrial espionage,' Lazlo Toth, a Thornton resident, has been pardoned..."

It further explains that he was charged with taking several photographs of a beet-sugar plant in Vrbas, a Belgrade suburb, while vacationing in Yugoslavia. He was the Director of Research and Development for the Great Western Sugar Co. in Loveland, Colorado.

He was sentenced to seven years after a closed-door trial but was later pardoned by the Yugoslav collective presidency.
