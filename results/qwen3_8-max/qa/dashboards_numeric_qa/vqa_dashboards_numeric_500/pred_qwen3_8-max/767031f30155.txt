Based on the image provided, the emergency benefits for working Canadians under Canada's Economic Response Plan are:

1.  **Emergency Care Benefit:**
    *   Provides up to **$900 bi-weekly** for up to **15 weeks**.
    *   For individuals who must stay home due to COVID-19 and don't have paid sick leave. This includes those who can't access EI, are sick, or are caring for a sick child or someone else who is sick.

2.  **Emergency Support Benefit:**
    *   Provides up to **$5 billion** in total support.
    *   For workers who are not eligible for EI and are facing unemployment or reduced hours.

Additionally, the plan includes broader support:
*   **$27 Billion** in direct support for Canadian Workers and Businesses.
*   **$55 Billion** in support for business liquidity through tax deferrals.
