Based on the infographic, under the section "1 IN 6 HEARING LOSS", it states:

"1 in 6 Australians are affected by hearing loss."

This means that out of every 6 Australians, 1 is affected. Therefore, the number not affected is:

6 - 1 = **5**

So, **5 out of 6** Australians are not affected by hearing loss.
