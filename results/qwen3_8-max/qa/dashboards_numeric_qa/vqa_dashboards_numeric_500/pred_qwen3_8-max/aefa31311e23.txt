Based on the infographic provided, the majority of millennials choose online viewing for the following reasons:

*   **Immediate access (56%)**: This is the top reason cited. Over half of millennials prefer the ability to watch content instantly whenever they want.
*   **Multitasking (49%)**: Nearly half appreciate the ability to do other things while watching, which online platforms often facilitate better than traditional TV.
*   **Convenience of viewing location (44%)**: A significant portion values the flexibility to watch content anywhere — on a phone, tablet, or laptop — rather than being tied to a living room TV.

These factors highlight that millennials prioritize **control, flexibility, and convenience** in their media consumption habits, which online viewing platforms are uniquely positioned to provide.
