Based on the infographic, under the section "HOW MANY PEOPLE ARE RECEIVING FOOD ASSISTANCE?", it states that less than half of food insecure Australians (46%) have sought assistance from a charity.

The reasons given for this include:
*   42% feel others need help more than they do
*   **36% feel Embarrassed**
*   33% feel Ashamed

Therefore, **36%** of food insecure Australians who haven't sought assistance feel embarrassed about doing so.
