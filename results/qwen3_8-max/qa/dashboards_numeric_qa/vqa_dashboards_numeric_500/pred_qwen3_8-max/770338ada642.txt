Based on the provided map and its key, the **Eastern Wild Turkey** (represented by the teal/blue color) is found in the largest continuous area of the country.

This range covers:
- The entire Eastern United States, from Maine down to Florida (excluding the southern tip where Osceola turkeys are).
- Extending westward through the Midwest, including states like Missouri, Wisconsin, Michigan, and parts of Texas and Oklahoma.

The other subspecies have more limited or fragmented ranges:
- **Osceola Wild Turkey**: Confined mostly to central and southern Florida.
- **Rio Grande Wild Turkey**: Found in a band from Texas through Oklahoma, Kansas, and into Nebraska.
- **Hybrid Turkey**: Appears as scattered green patches, primarily in the Great Plains region.
- **Merriam’s Wild Turkey**: Concentrated in the mountainous western states (Rockies, Southwest).

Therefore, the **Eastern Wild Turkey** occupies the most extensive and major area across the U.S. according to this map.
