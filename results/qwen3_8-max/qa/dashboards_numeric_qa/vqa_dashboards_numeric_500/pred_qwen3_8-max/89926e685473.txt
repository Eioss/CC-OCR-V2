Based on the infographic, in the section on the left side, it states:

"51 billion plastic bottles are used every year, **only 1 in 5 are recycled**"

This means that out of every 5 bottles, 1 is recycled. Therefore, the number of bottles **not** recycled is:

5 - 1 = **4**

So, **4** out of 5 bottles are not recycled.
