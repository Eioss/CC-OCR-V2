Based on the factsheet provided, for social distancing during outdoor activity (including bootcamps), there should be **4 square metres of space per person**.

This is explicitly stated in the section:
> "Outdoor activity, including bootcamps, can be conducted in small groups... with social distancing (ensuring 4 square metres of space per person and maintaining a 1.5 metre distance from others)."

The same 4 square metres per person rule also applies to indoor yoga or Pilates classes within a private residence.

So, the required area between persons for social distancing is **4 square metres per person**.
