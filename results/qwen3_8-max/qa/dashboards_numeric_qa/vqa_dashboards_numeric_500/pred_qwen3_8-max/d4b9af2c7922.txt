Based on the infographic provided:

*   There are over **6 million** disabled people of working age in the UK.
*   Only about **3.2 million** are currently in employment.

To find the number of unemployed (or not in employment) disabled people of working age, you subtract the employed from the total:

6 million - 3.2 million = **2.8 million**

Therefore, approximately **2.8 million** disabled people of working age in the UK are not currently in employment.
