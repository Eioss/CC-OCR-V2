Based on the infographic provided, the long-term benefits of early childhood education are categorized into two main areas: **Student Success** and **Adult Responsibility**.

Here is a breakdown of those benefits:

**Student Success**
*   **Higher Graduation Rates:** Students with early childhood education are **29% more likely to graduate from high school**.
*   **Less Grade Repetition:** They are **44% less likely to repeat a grade**.
*   **Better Test Performance:** They pass standardized tests more frequently.

**Adult Responsibility**
*   **Higher Employment:** Adults at age 40 who had early childhood education are **more likely to be employed**.
*   **Higher Income:** These same 40-year-olds have a **33% higher average income**.
*   **Lower Crime Rates:** 18-year-olds *without* early childhood education are **70% more likely to be arrested for a violent crime** (implying that early education significantly reduces this risk).
