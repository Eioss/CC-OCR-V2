Based on the infographic provided, under the section for **ANZAC BISCUITS**, it states:

> **Bread substitute**
> The original biscuits used in WWI soldier rations were hard and savoury.

Therefore, the biscuits used in WWI soldier rations were the original, hard, and savoury versions of what later became known as Anzac biscuits. They served as a bread substitute.

*(Note: The modern sweet Anzac biscuit recipe was first published in 1917, during the war, but the ration biscuits themselves were different — harder and less sweet.)*
