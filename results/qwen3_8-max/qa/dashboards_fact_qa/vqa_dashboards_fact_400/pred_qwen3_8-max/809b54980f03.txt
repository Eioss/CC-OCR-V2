Based on the infographic provided, **canned dog food has a longer shelf life** than dry dog food when unopened.

Here’s the breakdown from the “BEST IF USED BY: STORING DOG FOOD” section:

- **Canned Dog Food (Unopened):**
  - Shelf Life: **18–24 months** from date packaged.

- **Dry Dog Food (Unopened):**
  - Shelf Life: **12–15 months** from date packaged.

✅ Therefore, **canned dog food lasts longer** — up to 24 months compared to 15 months for dry food.

*(Note: Once opened, storage conditions and duration differ significantly — canned food must be refrigerated and used within 3 days, while dry food can be stored in a cool, dry place for up to 90 days.)*
