Based on the provided infographic from the Department of Health & Social Care, the two main types of coronavirus (COVID-19) tests are:

1.  **Swab test** (also known as a PCR or antigen test): This test answers the question "**Do I have it?**" It detects if you currently have an active infection by taking a swab sample from your throat and nose.

2.  **Antibody test** (also known as a serology test): This test answers the question "**Have I had it?**" It detects if you have previously been infected with the virus by looking for antibodies in your blood. The infographic further divides this into:
    *   **Laboratory test**: Blood drawn by a medical professional.
    *   **Home test**: A 'finger-prick' test taken at home (though the note says these were not accurate enough to be offered at the time this graphic was published).

So, the two fundamental categories are the **Swab test** (for current infection) and the **Antibody test** (for past infection).
