Based on the infographic provided, under the section **"SEARCH ENGINE BASICS"**, it states:

> "All modern search engines use the same basic approach to organizing websites"

The three approaches listed are:

1.  **CRAWLING**: Search engines follow links to browse the 60 trillion (and growing) pages that make up the web.
2.  **SORTING**: Results get sorted by a variety of factors (more on this in a second!).
3.  **INDEXING**: Sorted pages get stored in an index so search engines can match queries with results faster.

Therefore, the three basic approaches for organizing websites (as described in the context of search engines) are **Crawling, Sorting, and Indexing**.
