Based on the infographic:

*   **79%** of learning materials were accessed via Apps by smartphone.
*   **17%** installed the App on **both** tablets and smartphones.

To find the percentage who used **only** smartphones, we subtract those who used both from the total smartphone users:

79% (smartphone users) - 17% (used both) = **62%**

Therefore, **62%** of people used only apps in smartphones.

*(Note: The 21% figure refers to usage on tablets, not exclusively tablets. The 17% overlap is key to calculating "only" smartphone users.)*
