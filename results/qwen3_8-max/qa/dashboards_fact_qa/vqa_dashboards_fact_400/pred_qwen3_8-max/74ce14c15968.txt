Based on the provided infographic, the initiatives of Ban Ki-moon under the "Education First" campaign are centered around three core pillars:

1.  **Put every child in school**
    *   Eliminate cost barriers to attending and completing school.
    *   Identify and help children who need nutritional, health, and social support.
    *   Close the gender gap at all education levels.
    *   Build 4 million more classrooms, especially in rural and conflict-affected areas.
    *   Double or more education’s percentage of humanitarian aid.

2.  **Improve the quality of learning**
    *   Train 2 million more well-trained teachers with effective student performance measures.
    *   Improve traditional and digital learning materials in students’ home language.
    *   Increase early-childhood education programs by 45% to prepare children for primary school.
    *   Ensure a better match between schooling and needed workforce skills, and promote more adult learning.

3.  **Foster global citizenship**
    *   Promote peace, tolerance, and respect for diversity across cultures through education.
    *   Cultivate a sense of community, active participation, and environmental stewardship.
    *   Train teachers to use innovative, participatory, and non-formal teaching and learning methods.

Additionally, the initiative calls for closing the financial gap by:
*   Generating additional funding through sustained global advocacy.
*   Increasing global investment in poor countries’ school systems by $24 billion per year.
*   Investing at least 5–6% of countries’ national GDP in education.
*   Ensuring effective and accountable use of resources aligned with national education plans.

These initiatives are presented as part of the United Nations Secretary-General’s broader “Education First” initiative, with quotes from Ban Ki-moon emphasizing that education reduces poverty, lifts people out of poverty, fosters global interconnectedness, and is the single best investment for building prosperous, healthy, and equitable societies.
