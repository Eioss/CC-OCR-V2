Based on the infographic:

*   **21%** of learning materials were accessed via apps on tablets.
*   **17%** installed the app on **both** tablets and smartphones.

To find the percentage who used **only** tablets, we subtract those who used both from the total tablet users:

21% (total tablet users) - 17% (used both) = **4%**

Therefore, **4%** of people used only apps on tablets.
