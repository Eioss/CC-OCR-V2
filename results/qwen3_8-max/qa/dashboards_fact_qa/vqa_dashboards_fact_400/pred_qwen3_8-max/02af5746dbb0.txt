Based on the provided infographic, the **Swab test** ("Do I have it?") is the one that indicates if a person:

*   needs to continue to self isolate
*   can return to work
*   needs to be re-tested

This information is found in the bottom-left section under "What does it mean?" for the Swab test column.

The Antibody tests (Laboratory and Home) are described as telling someone whether they *have had* the virus, but not about current infection status or immunity levels that would determine isolation or return-to-work decisions.
