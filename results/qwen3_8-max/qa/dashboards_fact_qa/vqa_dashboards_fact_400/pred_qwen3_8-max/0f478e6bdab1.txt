Based on the infographic, under the "FACTS ABOUT INFERTILITY" section, it states:

> **10% OF WOMEN**  
> Percentage of women in the United States between the ages of 15-44 considered infertile (6.1 million)

If 10% are considered *infertile*, then the percentage considered *fertile* would be:

**100% - 10% = 90%**

✅ So, **90%** of women in the U.S. between the ages of 15–44 are considered fertile.

*(Note: This is a simplification — fertility exists on a spectrum, and “fertile” here likely means “not classified as infertile” according to the definition given: not conceiving after one year of unprotected sex.)*
