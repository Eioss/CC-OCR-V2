Based on the infographic, in the section titled "THINKING OUTSIDE THE BAG (OR CAN): SUPPLEMENTING YOUR DOG'S DIET", it lists the components of Ian Billinghurst's proposed "Biologically Appropriate Raw Food" diet.

The foods mentioned are:
1.  Muscle Meat (on bone)
2.  Bones (whole or ground)
3.  Organ Meats (liver, kidney)
4.  Raw Eggs
5.  Veggies (broccoli, spinach, celery)
6.  Apples or other fruits
7.  Some dairy, such as yogurt

Therefore, there are **7** types of food mentioned for this diet.
