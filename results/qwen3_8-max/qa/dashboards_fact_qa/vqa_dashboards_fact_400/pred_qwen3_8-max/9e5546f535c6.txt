Based on the provided infographic from the Department of Health & Social Care, antibody testing (which answers the question "Have I had it?") can be done in two ways:

1.  **Laboratory test**: This involves a blood test taken by a medical professional.
2.  **Home test**: This involves a 'finger-prick' test taken at home.

The infographic notes that while laboratory tests are being offered to specific groups (NHS and social care staff, NHS hospital inpatients, and care home residents), home tests are not currently being offered because they are not accurate enough yet.
