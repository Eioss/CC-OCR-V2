Based on the infographic, under the "USER BASE" section, it states:

"**1/3** OF ALL MOBILE PHONE USERS will access Instagram AT LEAST ONCE A MONTH IN 2016"

If 1/3 of users *do* access it at least once a month, then the remaining fraction who do *not* is:

1 - 1/3 = **2/3**

To express this as a percentage:

(2/3) * 100% ≈ **66.67%**

Therefore, approximately **66.67%** of all smartphone users are not using Instagram about once a month in 2016.

*(Note: The infographic says "mobile phone users," which is generally synonymous with "smartphone users" in this context.)*
