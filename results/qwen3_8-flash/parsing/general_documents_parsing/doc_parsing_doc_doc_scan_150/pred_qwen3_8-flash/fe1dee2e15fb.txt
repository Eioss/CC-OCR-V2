    \item[(3)] Let H and h be the heights of the 2 candles.
    
    Burnt amount at any time:
    \[
    \text{H: } \frac{120}{180} \times \text{H} = \frac{2}{3}\text{H}
    \]
    \[
    \text{h: } \frac{120}{300} \times \text{h} = \frac{2}{5}\text{h}
    \]
    Remaining:
    \[
    \text{H: } \text{H} - \frac{2}{3}\text{H} = \frac{1}{3}\text{H}
    \]
    \[
    \text{h: } \text{h} - \frac{2}{5}\text{h} = \frac{3}{5}\text{h}
    \]
    The ratio after 2 h is $1 : 1$.
    \[
    \frac{\frac{1}{3}\text{H}}{\frac{3}{5}\text{h}} = \frac{1}{1}
    \]
    Cross multiply:
    \[
    \frac{1}{3}\text{H} = \frac{3}{5}\text{h}
    \]
    Cross multiply:
    \[
    5\text{H} = 9\text{h}
    \]
    \[
    \frac{\text{H}}{\text{h}} = \frac{9}{5}
    \]
    $\text{H} : \text{h} = 9 : 5$

    \item[(1)] Height after $n$ min:
    \[
    1^\text{st}: \frac{n}{180}
    \]
    \[
    2^\text{nd}: \frac{n}{240}
    \]

    \[

    \[

    \[

    \]

    \]

    \]

    \]
    \[
