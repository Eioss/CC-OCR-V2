For each of the following formulae, choose the sentence that provides the best English translation of the formula according to the following symbolization key:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|}
\hline
$B$ & Bob is gathering eggs. \\ \hline
$C$ & Bob has fed the chickens. \\ \hline
$D$ & Bob has fed the ducks. \\ \hline
$F$ & Bob is chasing the fox away. \\ \hline
$P$ & The ducks are playing in the pond. \\ \hline
$Q$ & The ducks are quacking loudly. \\ \hline
$S$ & The chickens are playing in the pond. \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Question 10}

$(F \to (Q \mathbin{\&} \neg S))$ \hfill Select one answer. \\
\hfill 2 points

\begin{itemize}
    \item[A.] $\bigcirc$ If the ducks are quacking loudly and the chickens aren't playing in the pond, then Bob is chasing the fox away.
    \item[B.] $\bigcirc$ The ducks are quacking loudly and the chickens aren't playing in the pond unless Bob is chasing the fox away.
    \item[C.] $\bigcirc$ The ducks are quacking loudly and the chickens aren't playing in the pond if Bob is chasing the fox away.
    \item[D.] $\bigcirc$ The ducks are quacking loudly and the chickens aren't playing in the pond only if Bob is chasing the fox away.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{1cm}

\noindent\textbf{Question 11}

$((P \mathbin{\&} Q) \mathbin{\&} S)$ \hfill Select one answer. \\
\hfill 2 points

\begin{itemize}
    \item[A.] $\bigcirc$ The ducks are quacking loudly and playing in the pond, and the chickens are also playing in the pond.
\end{itemize}
