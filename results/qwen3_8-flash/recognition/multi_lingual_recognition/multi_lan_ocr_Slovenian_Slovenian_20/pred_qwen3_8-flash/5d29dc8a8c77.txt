POTSO 8: DIPOTSO TSE DIKHUTSHWANE
'MENOMASWEU POLAYA E TSHEGA' – FS Khunou
8.1
Bolwetse jo bo bolayang batho bo sa bontshe.
(2)
8.2
Maikutlo a kutlobotlhoko – Ka ntlha ya selo se se bolaileng ntsalaa mmoki.
(2)
8.3
Tshwantshiso – gore mmuisi a tlhaloganye bonolo ka go tshwantshanya dilo tse pedi go utlwisa bogale jwa menomasweu a a go bolayang a tshega nao.
(2)
8.4

(2)
8.5

POTSO 9: DIPOTSO TSE DIKHUTSHWANE
'MOTHO' – A.Mogorosi-le-ba bangwe
9.1
Go tlhoka botshepegi le nnete ga motho.
(2)
9.2
Maikutlo a kutlobotlhoko – ka ntlha ya bonkgate ke tlhaname jwa motho/ Motho ga a tshepagale, ga a itsiwe e se naga.
(2)
9.3
Neeletsano – go golaganya mela e mebedi ntle le go dirisa lekopanyi.
(2)
9.4
Tshwantshanyo – Go tshwantshanya mokgwa o ba itayang ka ona le wa sethunja.
Motho ga a itsiwe e se naga/motho o fetogafetoga bonolo/ga a ikanyege.
(Tshwantshanyo – Go tshwantshanya dilo tse pedi tse di tshwanang ka bokao jaaka ba tshwantshanya mokgwa o ba itayang ka ona le wa sethunja.
