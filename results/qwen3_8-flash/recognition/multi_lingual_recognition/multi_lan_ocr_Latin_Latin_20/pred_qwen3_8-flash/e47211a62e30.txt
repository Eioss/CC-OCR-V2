Ind. pars 3, p. 535), foliis breviter petiolatis, 2-3-jugis; foliolis oppositis, breviter petiolulatis, anguste ellipticis vel lanceolato-oblongis, basi æquali acutis, apice obtusiuscule cuspidatis, utrinque glabris, sed ad nervos contiuem adpresse lepidotis; paniculis breviter pedunculatis, laxa pyramidato-ramosis, adpresse folia superantibus æquantibusve, subglobosis; calyce profunde lepidotis; floribus breviter pedicellatis; petalis glabris, obovatis; obtusidentato, extus parce stellato-lepidoto; tubo breve subgloboso-urceolato, integro, stello-lepidoto. Panicula pedunculus circiter 25 mill. longus, rami ramulique graciles, leviter sulcati. Petala 5, obtusa, 1 mill. excedentia. Antheræ ovato-acutæ, minutæ.
