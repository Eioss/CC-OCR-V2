This is a **Discipline Review Letter** from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), part of the Department of Health & Human Services.

As indicated by the title at the top right:  
> **DISCIPLINE REVIEW LETTER**

It was sent to Takeda Global Research & Development Center, Inc., regarding their supplemental New Drug Application (NDA) for Actos® (pioglitazone HCl) tablets. The letter details the FDA’s review of clinical data related to labeling revisions — specifically concerning the PROactive trial results and how they should be presented in the drug’s label (e.g., moving information about failed primary endpoints to the Adverse Reactions section).

The document also includes a “RECEIVED” stamp dated February 19, 2007, indicating when it was processed internally by Takeda’s Regulatory Affairs Department.
