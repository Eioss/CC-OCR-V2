This is clearly printed at the top of the form, just below the identifier “SH147, 635”. The form includes fields for:

- Mode of travel (with carrier and class of service)
- Agent’s valuation of ticket
- From / To locations
- Time and date of departure and arrival
- Remarks

It also notes on the right margin: *“THIS STUB IS FOR TRAVELER’S USE”*, indicating it’s likely a receipt or record kept by the traveler after purchasing a ticket — possibly from an airline or railroad company in 1950 (as suggested by the dates filled in).

So, to directly answer your question:  
> **What type of statement is given here?**  
✅ **A Statement of Travel**

*(Note: While sometimes called a “travel stub” or “ticket stub,” the official title on the form itself is “STATEMENT OF TRAVEL.”)*
