This form is known as a **Universal Credit Card Charge Form**.

It was used by **Eastern Air Lines** (as indicated by the logo and text) to process credit card charges for tickets or related services. The form includes fields for:

- Passenger name
- Routing information
- Fare details
- Credit card number, expiration date, and approval code
- Contractor invoice copy designation
- Payment terms (e.g., extended payment options)

The top of the form clearly states:  
> **“UNIVERSAL CREDIT CARD CHARGE FORM”**

Additionally, it notes that this is “Page 7” and references standard company policies regarding payment — typical for airline ticketing forms from the era (likely 1970s–80s).

---

✅ So, in summary:  
**Form Name:** *Universal Credit Card Charge Form*  
**Issuer:** Eastern Air Lines  
**Purpose:** To authorize and record credit card payments for air travel tickets/services
