This is a **RJRT/Research & Development Documentation Approval Form**.

It’s used to formally approve and document the publication or presentation of research-related materials (in this case, an abstract) from R&D departments within RJ Reynolds Tobacco Company. The form includes fields for:

- Author name and title
- Division
- Date and mail address
- Type of documentation (e.g., Abstract — marked with “X”)
- Document title
- Co-authors
- Purpose of document (publication/presentation details)
- Signatures from author, manager, and director to confirm non-proprietariness and quality

The specific instance shown relates to an abstract titled *“Mainstream Particulate Phase Comparison of a Reference Cigarette and a Cigarette That Heats Rather Than Burns Tobacco”*, intended for presentation at the American Chemical Society Southeast Regional Meeting in Atlanta, GA, on November 11, 1988.

So, in summary:  
> **Type of form**: Internal corporate R&D documentation approval form for scientific publications/presentations.
