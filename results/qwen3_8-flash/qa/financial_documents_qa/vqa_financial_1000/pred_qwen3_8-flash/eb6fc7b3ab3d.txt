According to Frank L. Iber, M.D., **alcohol** is the only nutrient antinutrient.

This is stated directly in the text on the page:

> *“Alcohol is the only nutrient antinutrient. It is a food that causes a very special kind of malnutrition.”*

The term “nutrient antinutrient” refers to something that, while potentially providing calories or being consumed as part of the diet (like alcohol), actively interferes with the body’s ability to absorb, utilize, or retain essential nutrients — leading to malnutrition despite caloric intake. In this case, alcohol fits that definition uniquely according to Iber.
