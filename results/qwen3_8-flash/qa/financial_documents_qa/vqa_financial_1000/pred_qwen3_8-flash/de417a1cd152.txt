This is a **Facsimile Transmission** cover sheet.

It was used to send documents via fax (facsimile) and includes fields for:
- Date
- Recipient (“For the Attention of”)
- Sender’s Name
- Number of pages being sent
- Optional message space

The document is from **Imperial Tobacco Limited, Technical Services**, dated **30/11/98**, addressed to **Jim Swauger**, sent by **Ian Bailey**, with **4 pages** to follow.

Such forms were common in offices before email became widespread, serving as transmittal notes for faxed materials.
