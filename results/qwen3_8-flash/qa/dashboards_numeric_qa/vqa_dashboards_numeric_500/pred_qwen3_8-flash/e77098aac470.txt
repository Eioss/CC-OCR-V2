Based on the infographic **“Mobile Internet Costs Around The World”**, we are looking for the **best country to get a 4G LTE smartphone** — not necessarily just cheapest, but best value or performance in terms of cost and speed.

Let’s analyze the options given: **India, America (USA), Japan, Australia**

---

### 1. **United States (America)**

### 3. **Australia**

### 4. **India**

## ✅ Conclusion:

Among **India, America, Japan, Australia**, the **best country to get a 4G LTE smartphone** based on **cost, speed, and value** is:

> **🇯 Japan**

- Low monthly cost (~$10–15 for 2GB),
- Actual 4G LTE networks,
- High speeds (37.5 Mbps via DOCOMO),
- Best value plans for heavy users ($54 unlimited).

---

**Answer: Japan**
