According to the infographic, **2 in 5 people with disability are 65 years or older**.

This means that among all Australians who have a disability, **40% (or 2 out of every 5)** are seniors aged 65 and above.

Additionally, the graphic notes:  
> *“The likelihood of living with disability increases with age.”*

So while it doesn’t give an exact percentage chance for *any* senior citizen having a disability, it clearly states that **disability is more common in older age groups**, and **seniors make up a significant portion (40%) of the total disabled population** in Australia.

Also shown elsewhere in the image:
- **1.8 million Australians aged 65+** have some form of disability — which aligns with the “2 in 5” statistic when compared to the total ~4 million people with disability in Australia.

✅ In summary:  
**Among those with disability, 2 in 5 (40%) are 65+.**  
And **1.8 million seniors (65+) live with disability** — highlighting how strongly age correlates with increased risk.
