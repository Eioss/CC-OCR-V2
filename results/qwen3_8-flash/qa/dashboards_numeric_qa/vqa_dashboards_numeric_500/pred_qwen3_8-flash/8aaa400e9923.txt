Based on the infographic provided, the **developmental benefits** of early childhood education are categorized into three main areas:

1.  **Social and Emotional:**
    *   Students are surrounded by adults and peers, allowing them to **develop relationships**.
    *   The constant presence of these people offers a **sense of security**.
    *   This feeling of security leads to **greater participation and cooperation**.

2.  **Cognitive:**
    *   A developmental curriculum provides **age-appropriate learning**.
    *   Students build on existing skills, which helps in **increasing their knowledge**.
    *   Teachers help ensure students reach important **developmental milestones**.

3.  **Language:**
    *   Between ages 1 and 5, **language develops quickly**.
    *   Routine interactions with teachers and peers help **develop language skills**.
    *   As conversations and vocabulary progress, **more advanced skills emerge**.
