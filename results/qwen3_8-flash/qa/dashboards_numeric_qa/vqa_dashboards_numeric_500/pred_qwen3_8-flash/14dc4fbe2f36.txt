Based on the infographic provided, here is the information regarding consumers trying new things while shopping online:

1.  **Overall Consumers:** The section titled "Consumers are experimenting, purchasing items they would not usually purchase online" states that **39%** of respondents said the lockdown encouraged them to try and buy goods online they had not considered before.
2.  **Millennials:** The same section notes that this figure increased to **52%** for Millennials. This is also shown in the bar chart where the "Millennials" bar is at 52%.

To find the increase from overall consumers to millennials:

*   Percentage for Millennials: 52%
*   Percentage for Overall: 39%
*   Increase = 52% - 39% = **13%**

Therefore, the increase in percentage from overall consumers when it comes to millennials trying new things while shopping online is **13 percentage points**.
