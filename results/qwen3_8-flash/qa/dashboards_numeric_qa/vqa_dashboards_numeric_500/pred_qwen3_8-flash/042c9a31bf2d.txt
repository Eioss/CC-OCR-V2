Based on the provided infographic, the **long-term benefits** of early childhood education are categorized into two main areas:

---

### 1. **Student Success**
- Students with early childhood education are **29% more likely to graduate from high school**.
- They are **44% less likely to repeat a grade**.
- They **pass standardized tests more frequently**.

---

### 2. **Adult Responsibility**
- Adults age 40 who had early childhood education are **more likely to be employed**.
- These same adults have a **33% higher average income**.
- Conversely, **18-year-olds *without* early childhood education are 70% more likely to be arrested for a violent crime**.

---

These long-term outcomes suggest that early childhood education contributes not only to academic achievement but also to future economic stability and reduced involvement in criminal behavior.

*(Source: Provided by Campbell Christian Schools, citing data from earlychildhoodalliance.com, kueeducation.com, and highscoe.org)*
