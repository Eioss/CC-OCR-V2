According to the infographic, the three main reasons for deforestation are:

1. **Agriculture** – Clearing forests to make way for crops and farming (illustrated with a tractor).
2. **Logging** – Cutting down trees for timber and wood products (shown with a truck carrying logs).
3. **Cattle Grazing** – Converting forest land into pasture for livestock (depicted with cows grazing).

These activities are presented as the primary drivers behind the large-scale clearing of Earth’s forests.
