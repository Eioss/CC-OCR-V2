Based on the "Foodbank Hunger Report 2017" infographic, under the section **"HOW MANY PEOPLE ARE RECEIVING FOOD ASSISTANCE?"**, there is a subsection titled **"This is because they feel:"**.

In this section, three reasons are listed for why less than half of food insecure Australians seek assistance from charity:
*   **42%**: Others need help more than they do
*   **36%**: Embarrassed
*   **33%**: Ashamed

Therefore, **33%** of food insecure Australians are ashamed of seeking assistance from a charity.
