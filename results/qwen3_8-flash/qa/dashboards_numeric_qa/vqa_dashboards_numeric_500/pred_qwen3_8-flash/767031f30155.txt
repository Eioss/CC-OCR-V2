Based on the infographic provided, under the section **“WORKING CANADIANS”**, two emergency benefits are highlighted as part of **Canada’s Economic Response Plan to COVID-19**:

---

### 1. **Emergency Care Benefit**
- **Amount**: Up to **$900 bi-weekly**
- **Duration**: For up to **15 weeks**
- **Eligibility**: 
  - Individuals who must stay home due to COVID-19 and don’t have paid sick leave.
  - Includes those who can’t access Employment Insurance (EI) because they are:
    - Sick themselves, or
    - Taking care of a child or someone who is sick.

---

### 2. **Emergency Support Benefit**
- **Total Funding**: **$5 billion** allocated for this benefit
- **Purpose**: To support workers who:
  - Are **not eligible for EI**, and
  - Are facing **unemployment or reduced hours** due to the pandemic.

*(Note: The exact weekly amount or duration for the Emergency Support Benefit is not specified in this graphic — only the total funding envelope of $5 billion is shown.)*

---

These benefits were designed to provide direct financial relief to Canadian workers impacted by the economic disruptions caused by the pandemic.
