Based on the infographic, the candidate for president is officially chosen at the **National Party Conventions**.

Here's how the process works according to the "THE SYSTEM" section:

1.  **Caucuses and State Primaries:** Voters in each state participate in caucuses or primaries to select delegates who support specific presidential nominees.
2.  **Party Delegates:** These delegates are then sent from each state to their party’s national convention.
3.  **National Party Convention:** At this convention, the delegates officially vote to choose the party’s candidate for president.

So, while voters express preference through caucuses/primaries, the formal selection happens at the National Party Convention by the delegates.
