According to the **August 2010** survey shown in the infographic, **35%** of all respondents said that Islam is more likely than other religions to encourage violence.

This is indicated by the black segment of the donut chart for August 2010, which corresponds to the legend:  
> **“IS MORE LIKELY THAN OTHERS TO ENCOURAGE VIOLENCE”** (black circle)

The orange segment (42%) represents those who said it *doesn’t* encourage violence more than others, and the gray segment (24%) represents “Other/Don’t Know”.

✅ So, the answer is: **35%**
