Based on the infographic titled **“UNIQUE WAYS PINTEREST CAN HELP YOUR BUSINESS”**, there are **5 distinct ways** Pinterest can help businesses:

1. **Creates Backlinks To Your Site**  
   → When users pin and repin your content, it generates backlinks that improve SEO.

2. **Boosts Your Social Signals**  
   → Pins act as social signals (likes, shares, saves) that search engines like Google factor into rankings.

3. **Opportunity for New Content**  
   → Repinning others’ content or creating new pins gives you fresh material to engage audiences and satisfy search engines’ appetite for new content.

4. **Boosts Your Keyword Targeting**  
   → Using keywords in pin descriptions helps align with what people are searching for — and since search engines now scan Pinterest, this boosts visibility and ranking.

5. **SEO Image Boost**  
   → Optimizing images with keyword-rich names and full descriptions leverages image search algorithms, giving your business more exposure without being overly promotional.

---

✅ So, the answer is: **5 unique ways**.
