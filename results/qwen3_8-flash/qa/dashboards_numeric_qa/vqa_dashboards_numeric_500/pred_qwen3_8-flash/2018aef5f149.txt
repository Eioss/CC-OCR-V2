Based on the provided factsheet, for social distancing during outdoor activity (including bootcamps), you must ensure:

*   **4 square metres of space per person**, and
*   Maintain a **1.5 metre distance from others**.

This information is found in the section with the icon of two people jumping, under the heading “What does this mean for community sport and active recreation in WA?”

Additionally, the same 4 square metres per person rule applies to indoor yoga or Pilates classes within a private residence, as stated in the section with the rolled mat icon.

So, while both area (4 sq m/person) and linear distance (1.5 m between persons) are specified, the question asks for *area*, which is **4 square metres per person**.
