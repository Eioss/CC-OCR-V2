Based on the infographic, under the section **“If a driverless car hits a person, who is at fault?”**, the data shows:

- **73.72%** believe **the driverless car manufacturer** is at fault (orange segment).
- **13.76%** believe **those inside the car** are at fault (yellow segment).
- **12.52%** believe **the pedestrian** is at fault (teal segment).

### Conclusion:
**Most people (over 73%) think the driverless car manufacturer should be held responsible if a driverless car hits a person.**

This suggests that public opinion leans toward holding the technology provider — not the user or victim — accountable for accidents involving autonomous vehicles.
