Looking at the infographic, we can find the section that mentions:

> “Facebook images that were posted together as a photo album post received **12.9 times** more clicks than single photo posts.”

Below this text, there is a visual representation of cursor arrows (pointing hands) arranged in rows to symbolize clicks.

Let’s count them:

- First row: 5 cursors
- Second row: 5 cursors
- Third row: 3 cursors + the number “12.9 times” next to it

So total cursors = 5 + 5 + 3 = **13**
