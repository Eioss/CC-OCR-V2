Based on the infographic:

> **“DISABILITY: 1 IN 5 PEOPLE”**  
> *“Over 4 million people in Australia have some form of disability. That’s 1 in 5 people.”*

This means that **out of every 5 people**, **1 has a disability**.

Therefore, the number of people **not disabled** out of 5 is:

> **5 - 1 = 4**

✅ **Answer: 4 people are not disabled (out of every 5 people).**
