According to the timeline graphic from *The Lancet* (based on early Wuhan data), **fever was present in 98% of patients**.

Therefore, the percentage of corona patients who are **not** getting fever as a symptom is:

> **100% - 98% = 2%**

---

✅ **Answer: 2%**

*(Note: This reflects findings from a small number of early cases published on February 15, 2020. More recent studies may show different percentages as more data became available.)*
