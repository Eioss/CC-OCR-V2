According to the infographic, under the "INCLUSION & PARTICIPATION" section, it states:

> **Women make up only 22% of Parliamentarian seats, and 8% of the world’s executives.**

This means that **8%** of the world’s executives are women.

To find the percentage who are **not** women, we subtract from 100%:

**100% - 8% = 92%**

---

✅ **Answer: 92% of the world's executives are not women.**
