The reason stated on the **red card** (labeled **04**) is:

> **LACK OF ACTIONABLE DATA**

It asks:
> *Do your providers have access to data at the point of care that is accurate and easily actionable?*

This highlights a key sign that it’s time to reassess your Population Health Strategy — if clinicians don’t have timely, reliable, and usable data when they need it most (at the point of care), their ability to make informed decisions and improve patient outcomes is compromised.
