Based on the provided infographic, the three inventions introduced at the **1904 St. Louis World's Fair** were:

1.  **The Ice Cream Cone**: It made its debut as the first edible, walk-away ice cream container.
2.  **Electricity**: The Palace of Electricity was a main attraction, showcasing the development of electricity for the masses, with Thomas Edison overseeing its presentation.
3.  **The Hot Dog**: According to the graphic, Bavarian sausage seller Anton Feuchtwanger ran out of gloves (used by fair-goers to eat sausages) and asked his brother-in-law to create a sausage-sized long bun, leading to the birth of the hot dog.

*(Note: While X-rays were displayed in 1901 and incubators in 1909, only these three are explicitly linked to the 1904 St. Louis event in this infographic.)*
