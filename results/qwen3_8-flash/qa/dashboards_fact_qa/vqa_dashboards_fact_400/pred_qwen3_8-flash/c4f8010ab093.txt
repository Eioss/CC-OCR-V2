In the **Decentralised** section of the infographic, there are **4 steps** of app processing shown:

1. The app broadcasts a random ID and collects other apps’ IDs + interaction data (time/distance).
2. If a user reports being unwell, they upload their ID to a central database.
3. Users’ phones regularly download the most up-to-date version of this database and look for IDs they’ve interacted with.
4. If they see any ID(s) they have interacted with, their app calculates whether or not to inform them that they have been in contact with an infected person.

✅ So, the answer is: **4 steps**.
