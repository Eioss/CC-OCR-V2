Based on the infographic provided, under the **"USER BASE"** section, there is a statistic that states:

> **1/3 of all mobile phone users will access Instagram at least once a month in 2016**

This means that **one-third (1/3)** of smartphone users *are* using Instagram about once a month.

To find the percentage who are **not** using it about once a month, we subtract this from the total:

- Total = 1 (or 100%)
- Using Instagram monthly = 1/3
- Not using Instagram monthly = 1 - 1/3 = **2/3**

Converting 2/3 to a percentage:

> **(2 ÷ 3) × 100 ≈ 66.67%**

---

✅ **Answer: Approximately 66.67% of all smartphone users are NOT using Instagram about once a month in 2016.**
