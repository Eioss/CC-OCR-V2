The **3 R’s** in the context of waste management and sustainability, as shown in the infographic from *Flowcrete*, are:

1. **REDUCE**  
   → Eliminate the generation of waste at the source by delivering only what is required to site.

2. **REUSE**  
   → Make use of excess or unused materials in their original state on other sites.

3. **RECYCLE**  
   → Modify excess products to create new, usable products.

These principles aim to minimize environmental impact — especially relevant for industries like construction that generate significant waste (as highlighted elsewhere in the infographic). The 3 R’s form a hierarchy: reducing waste first is most preferred, followed by reusing, then recycling.
