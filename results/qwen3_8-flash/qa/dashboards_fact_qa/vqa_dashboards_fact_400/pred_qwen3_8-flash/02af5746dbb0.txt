### Why not the Antibody test?

The Antibody test (“Have I had it?”) tells you if you’ve been infected in the past and may have developed some immunity — but **it does NOT tell you if you are currently infectious or need to isolate**. The infographic explicitly states for the antibody test:

> “Can tell someone whether they have had the virus or not, but not about any level of protection or immunity from getting the virus again. Social distancing measures must still be followed.”

So while it helps scientists understand spread, it doesn’t guide individual isolation or return-to-work decisions during active infection.
