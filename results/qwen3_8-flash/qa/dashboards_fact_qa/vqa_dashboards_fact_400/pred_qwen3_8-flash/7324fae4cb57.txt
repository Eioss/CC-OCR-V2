Looking at the infographic, under the section titled **“EUROPEAN INTERNET HABITS”**, there is a bar chart that states:

> **21% INTERNET USERS WHO ARE EUROPEAN**

This means that **21%** of all internet users globally are from Europe.

To find the percentage of internet users who are **not** Europeans, we subtract this from 100%:

> **100% - 21% = 79%**

---

✅ **Answer: 79% of internet users are not Europeans.**
