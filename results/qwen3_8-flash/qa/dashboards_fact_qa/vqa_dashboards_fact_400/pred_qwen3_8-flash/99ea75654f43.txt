Based on the provided infographic, the **Laboratory test** (which is a type of Antibody test) is offered based on a clinician’s advice.

Here's how we know:

1.  The "Antibody test" section has two sub-types: "Laboratory test" and "Home test".
2.  Under the heading **"Can I get tested now?"**, the description for the **Laboratory test** column states:
    > "This test is being offered to NHS and social care staff, and to NHS hospital inpatients and care home residents **based on their clinician’s advice**."

The other tests have different criteria:
*   **Swab test**: Available to anyone experiencing symptoms via the NHS website or priority testing for essential workers.
*   **Home test**: Not currently offered by the Government because they are not accurate enough yet.

Therefore, the **Laboratory antibody test** is the one specifically mentioned as being offered based on a clinician’s advice.
