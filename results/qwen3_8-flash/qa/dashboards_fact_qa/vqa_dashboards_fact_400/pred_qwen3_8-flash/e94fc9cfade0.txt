Based on the infographic provided, under the section titled **"SEARCH ENGINE BASICS"**, it states:

> "All modern search engines use the same basic approach to organizing websites"

The three approaches shown with icons and descriptions are:

1.  **CRAWLING**  
    *Search engines follow links to browse the 60 trillion (and growing) pages that make up the web.*

2.  **SORTING**  
    *Results get sorted by a variety of factors (more on this in a second!)*

3.  **INDEXING**  
    *Sorted pages get stored in an index so search engines can match queries with results faster.*

---

✅ Therefore, the three basic approaches for organizing a network (specifically, how search engines organize websites) are:  
**Crawling, Sorting, and Indexing.**
