Based on the infographic provided, **infrared** touch screens are more durable than resistive ones.

Here’s the comparison from the “DURABILITY” row:

- **Resistive**:  
  > *Fair Durability. Screens are vulnerable to scratches and other minor damage. The screen will wear over time and need replacing.*

- **Infrared**:  
  > *Good Durability. Glass shatters upon major impact only.*

### Why?
- Resistive screens use a flexible top layer (often polyester film) that can be scratched or worn down with repeated use — especially if pressed with fingernails or styluses.
- Infrared screens typically use glass as the surface material (as shown in the “SCREEN MATERIAL” row), which is harder and more scratch-resistant. While glass can shatter under heavy impact, it doesn’t degrade from normal touching like resistive layers do.

✅ So, **infrared is more durable for everyday use**, though both rely on glass surfaces for protection against impacts.

*(Note: Capacitive also has “Good Durability,” similar to infrared, but your question specifically compares resistive vs. infrared.)*
