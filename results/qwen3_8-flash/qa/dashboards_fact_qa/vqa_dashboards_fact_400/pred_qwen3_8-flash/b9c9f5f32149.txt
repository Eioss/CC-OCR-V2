Based on the provided infographic from the UK Department of Health & Social Care, the two main types of coronavirus (COVID-19) tests are:

1.  **Swab Test**  
    *   **Purpose:** Answers the question “Do I have it?” — detects current infection.
    *   **How it works:** A sample is taken from the throat and nose using a swab.
    *   **Who can get tested now:** Anyone experiencing symptoms can ask for a test via the NHS website; priority testing is available to essential workers.
    *   **What it means:** Indicates if a person needs to continue self-isolating, can return to work, or needs to be re-tested.

2.  **Antibody Test**  
    *   **Purpose:** Answers the question “Have I had it?” — detects past infection by looking for antibodies in the blood.
    *   **Sub-types shown:**
        *   **Laboratory test:** Blood drawn by a medical professional.
        *   **Home test:** ‘Finger-prick’ test done at home.
    *   **Who can get tested now:** Currently offered only to NHS/social care staff and hospital inpatients/care home residents based on clinician’s advice. Home tests are not yet accurate enough for general public use.
    *   **What it means:** Tells someone whether they’ve had the virus before, but does *not* indicate immunity or protection against future infection. Also helps scientists and government understand spread patterns.

---

✅ So, while there are sub-types (like lab vs. home antibody tests), the **two fundamental categories** presented are:

> **1. Swab Test (for active infection)**  
> **2. Antibody Test (for past infection)**

These serve different purposes and are used in different contexts during the pandemic response.
