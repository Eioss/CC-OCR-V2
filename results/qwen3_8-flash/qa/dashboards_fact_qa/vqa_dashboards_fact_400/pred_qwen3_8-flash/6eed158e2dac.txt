Based on the "BEST IF USED BY: STORING DOG FOOD" section of the infographic, we can compare the storage times for opened canned and dry dog food.

-   **Canned Dog Food (left side):**
    -   Once opened, keeps for: **3 DAYS** (refrigerated)

-   **Dry Dog Food (right side):**
    -   Once opened, keeps for: **90 DAYS** (store in a cool, dry place)

---

Comparing the two durations, $90$ days is significantly longer than $3$ days.

Therefore, once opened, **dry dog food** can be stored for a longer time.
