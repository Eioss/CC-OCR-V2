According to the infographic, **18.7%** of LGBT people seek help from family when they suffer violence.

This is stated in the pink circle at the bottom left:
> “65.2% LGBT people seek help from friends when they suffer violence and only **18.7%** seek help from family; 29.8% of LGBT have chosen not to seek help when suffering violence”

So, while a majority turn to friends, less than one-fifth rely on family for support after experiencing violence.
