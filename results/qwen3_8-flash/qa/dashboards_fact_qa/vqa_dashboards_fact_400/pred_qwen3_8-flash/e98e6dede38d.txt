Based on the infographic titled **“INTERNET BANKING IN SOUTH AFRICA”**, under the section **“DEVICES USED”**, we can see the following percentages for devices used to access internet banking:

- **Personal Laptop or Computer**: 97%
- **Cellphone or Smartphone App**: **26%**
- Public Computer: 14%
- Tablet: 10%

The survey was conducted by Columinate, with data collected from **21 February to 15 March 2012**, among **1353 internet banking users**.

---

✅ Therefore, according to the 2012 survey, **26%** of consumers in South Africa do internet banking using a **smartphone app**.
