Based on the infographic provided, under the section **"BEST IF USED BY: STORING DOG FOOD"**, there is a comparison between canned food and dry kibble.

For **canned dog food** (represented by the gold can icon on the left):

*   **SHELF LIFE (UNOPENED):** 18-24 MONTHS FROM DATE PACKAGED.
*   **ONCE OPENED, STORE AT:** <40° F
*   **ONCE OPENED, KEEPS FOR:** 3 DAYS (REFRIGERATED)

Therefore, the shelf life for **unopened** canned dog food is **18 to 24 months from the date it was packaged**. Once opened, it should be refrigerated and used within **3 days**.
