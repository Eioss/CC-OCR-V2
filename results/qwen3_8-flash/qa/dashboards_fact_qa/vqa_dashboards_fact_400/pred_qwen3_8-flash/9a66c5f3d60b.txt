Looking at the infographic titled **“2020 This Is What Happens In An Internet Minute”**, we can see that in **60 seconds (1 minute)**, **2.5 million images are viewed**.

To find out how many images are viewed in **2 minutes**, we simply double that number:

> **2.5 million × 2 = 5 million**

✅ **Answer: 5 million images**
