Based on the infographic provided, **there is no direct percentage given for people who used *only* apps on smartphones**.

However, we can infer it from the data presented in the section about device usage:

> - **79%** of learning materials accessed via Apps by smartphone  
> - **21%** on their tablets  
> - **17%** installed the App on **both** tablets and smartphones

This suggests that the 79% includes those who used smartphones (possibly also tablets), and the 17% are a subset of that 79% who used both devices.

So, to find the percentage who used **only** smartphones:

→ Subtract those who used **both** from those who used **smartphones**:

**79% (used smartphone) – 17% (used both) = 62%**

---

✅ Therefore, approximately **62%** of users accessed learning materials via apps **only on smartphones**.

*(Note: This assumes the 79% represents all users who ever used the app on a smartphone — including those who also used tablets — which is consistent with how such statistics are typically reported.)*
