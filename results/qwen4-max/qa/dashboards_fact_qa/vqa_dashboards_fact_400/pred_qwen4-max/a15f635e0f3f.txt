Looking at the infographic:

- In **2003**, 57.2% of academic leaders believed online education was the same or even superior to face-to-face learning.
- "Just two years later" (i.e., **2005**), that number had increased to **77%**.

The difference: 77% − 57.2% = **19.8%**

So **19.8%** more academic leaders in 2005 found online education the same or superior compared to 2003.
