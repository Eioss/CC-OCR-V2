The reason stated on the red card (labeled **04**) is:

**LACK OF ACTIONABLE DATA**

It asks:  
*“Do your providers have access to data at the point of care that is accurate and easily actionable?”*

This highlights a key issue in population health strategy — if providers can’t get timely, relevant, and usable data when they need it (at the point of care), it hinders effective decision-making and patient management.
