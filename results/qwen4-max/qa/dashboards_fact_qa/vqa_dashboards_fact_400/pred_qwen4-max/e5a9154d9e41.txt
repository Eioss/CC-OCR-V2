Based on the "DURABILITY" section of the infographic:

- **Resistive** screens are described as having **"Fair Durability."** They are vulnerable to scratches and minor damage, and the screen will wear over time, often needing replacement.
- **Infrared** screens are described as having **"Good Durability."** The glass only shatters upon major impact.

Therefore, according to this chart, **infrared** touchscreens are more durable than resistive ones.
