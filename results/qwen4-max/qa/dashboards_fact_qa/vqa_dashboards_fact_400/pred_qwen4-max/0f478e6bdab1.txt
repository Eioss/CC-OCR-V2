90% of women in the U.S. between the ages of 15–44 are considered **fertile**.

This is inferred from the infographic, which states that **10% of women** in that age group (6.1 million) are considered **infertile**. Therefore:

> 100% – 10% = **90% fertile**

Note: The term “fertile” here refers to women who are *not classified as infertile* according to the standard definition — meaning they have not failed to conceive after at least one year of unprotected sex (primary infertility) or have not experienced secondary infertility. It does not guarantee immediate conception, but rather indicates they do not meet the clinical threshold for infertility.
