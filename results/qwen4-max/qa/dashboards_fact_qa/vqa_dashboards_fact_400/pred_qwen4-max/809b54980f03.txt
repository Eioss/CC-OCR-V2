Based on the "BEST IF USED BY: STORING DOG FOOD" section of the infographic:

- **Canned dog food** (the can illustration): shelf life unopened is **18–24 months** from date packaged.
- **Dry dog food** (the bag illustration): shelf life unopened is **12–15 months** from date packaged.

Therefore, **canned dog food has a longer shelf life** than dry dog food.
