The infographic does not provide a number for appliances that suck up electricity when **not plugged in** — and in fact, that scenario is physically impossible. Appliances must be plugged in to draw any electricity.

What the infographic *does* describe are **“Vampire” appliances** — devices that consume electricity **while plugged in, even when switched off**. It lists 10 such categories:

- Flat Screen TVs
- Gaming Console
- Computers
- DVRs / Cable Boxes
- DVD / VCR
- Space Heater
- Phones
- Printer
- Router / Modem
- Kitchen Appliances

✅ So, the correct answer to the *intended* question (vampire appliances that suck power while plugged in but turned off) is **10 categories**.

But strictly speaking, **zero** appliances suck up electricity when *not plugged in*.
