Looking at the "Thinking Outside the Bag (or Can)" section, the BARF diet lists these foods:

1. Muscle meat (on bone)
2. Bones (whole or ground)
3. Organ meats (liver, kidney)
4. Raw eggs
5. Veggies (broccoli, spinach, celery)
6. Apples or other fruits
7. Some dairy, such as yogurt

**There are 7 food categories mentioned** in the biologically appropriate raw food diet.
