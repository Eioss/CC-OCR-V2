The **Swab test** indicates whether a person needs to continue to self-isolate, can return to work, or needs to be re-tested.

This is shown in the “What does it mean?” section under the **Swab test** column (black box on the left), which lists:
- needs to continue to self isolate
- can return to work
- needs to be re-tested

In contrast, the Antibody test (green boxes) tells you if you’ve *had* the virus, but not about current infection status or immunity — so it doesn’t determine isolation or return-to-work decisions.

✅ Therefore, the correct answer is: **Swab test**.
