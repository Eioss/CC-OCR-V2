Looking at the "THE IMPACT OF HAPPINESS" section, thriving (happy) employees show:
- 16% better overall job performance
- 46% more satisfied with their jobs (high satisfaction)
- 32% more committed to the organization (high commitment)
- 125% less burnout than their peers (low exhaustion)

The largest indicator is **low exhaustion** (125% less burnout), which exceeds both high satisfaction (46%) and high commitment (32%).
