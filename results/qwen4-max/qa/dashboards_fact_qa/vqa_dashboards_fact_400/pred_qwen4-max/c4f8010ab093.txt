The decentralized section of the infographic shows **4 steps** of app processing.

They are labeled as:
1. The app broadcasts a random ID and collects IDs from other apps.
2. If a user reports they are unwell, they can upload their ID to a central database.
3. Users’ phones regularly download the latest database version and check for matching IDs.
4. If a match is found, the app calculates risk and informs the user if they’ve been in contact with an infected person.

✅ So, there are **4 steps** shown in the decentralized section.
