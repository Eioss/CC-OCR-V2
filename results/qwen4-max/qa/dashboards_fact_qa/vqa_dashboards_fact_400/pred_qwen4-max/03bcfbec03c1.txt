The **Laboratory test** involves blood testing by a medical professional.

According to the infographic, under the "Antibody test" category (which answers "Have I had it?"), there are two types:
- **Laboratory test** – "Blood test taken by a medical professional"
- **Home test** – "'Finger-prick' test taken at home"

So the laboratory antibody test is the one performed by a medical professional using a blood sample.
