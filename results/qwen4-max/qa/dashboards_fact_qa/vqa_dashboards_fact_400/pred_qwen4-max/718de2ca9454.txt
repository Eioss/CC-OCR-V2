To find how many **more** Indian tourists are expected this year compared to 2012, we subtract the 2012 number from the current year’s expected number:

- Expected Indian visitors **this year**: **25,000**
- Indian visitors in **2012**: **18,000**

**Calculation:**

25,000 – 18,000 = **7,000**

✅ So, **7,000 more** Indian tourists are expected this year compared to 2012.
