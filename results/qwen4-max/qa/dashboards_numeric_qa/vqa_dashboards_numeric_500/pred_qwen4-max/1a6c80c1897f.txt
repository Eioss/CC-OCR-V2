The infographic compares **China** and the **United States (US)** regarding their reported cases of **Coronavirus Disease 2019 (COVID-19)** and **Influenza** as of February 2020.

Specifically:
- **China**: Data as of **February 21, 2020** — 75,569 reported cases (99% in mainland China), 2,239 deaths.
- **US**: 
  - *COVID-19*: As of **February 24, 2020** — 14 diagnosed cases through healthcare system, 39 among repatriated citizens, 0 deaths, 0 critically ill, no evidence of community transmission.
  - *Influenza*: As of **February 15, 2020** — At least 29 million illnesses, 13 million physician visits, 280,000 hospitalizations, 16,000 deaths (including 105 pediatric deaths).

The comparison highlights that while global concern was focused on the emerging COVID-19 outbreak in China, influenza posed a much larger public health burden in the US at that time — especially for children — and urges focus on flu prevention (vaccination, hand hygiene, avoiding sick contacts) rather than mask-wearing in public for general protection against either virus.

✅ So, the countries being compared are: **China and the United States**.
