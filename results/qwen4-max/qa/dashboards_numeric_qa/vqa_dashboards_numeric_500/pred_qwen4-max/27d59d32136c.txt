Looking at the **INIHAW (Barbecued)** section, the items made from chicken parts are:

1. **Isaw** – chicken intestines
2. **Betamax** – dried pork/chicken blood
3. **Adidas** – chicken feet
4. **Helmet** – chicken heads

That gives **4** barbecue items prepared using various parts of a chicken.

(The other two — Pork BBQ and Pig skin — come from pork, not chicken.)
