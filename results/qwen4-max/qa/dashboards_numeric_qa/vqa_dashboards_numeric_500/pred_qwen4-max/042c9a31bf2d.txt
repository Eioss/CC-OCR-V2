Based on the infographic, the **long-term benefits** of early childhood education fall into two categories:

**Student success**
- Students are **29% more likely to graduate from high school**.
- Students are **44% less likely to repeat a grade**.
- Students **pass standardized tests more frequently**.

**Adult responsibility**
- Adults age 40 who had early childhood education are **more likely to be employed**.
- These 40-year-olds have a **33% higher average income**.
- 18-year-olds *without* early childhood education are **70% more likely to be arrested for a violent crime** (i.e., early education reduces this risk).

In short, early childhood education leads to better academic outcomes and greater employment, income, and social responsibility later in life.
