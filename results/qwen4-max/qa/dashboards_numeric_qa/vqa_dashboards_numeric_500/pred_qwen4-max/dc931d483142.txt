**Solar panels / photovoltaic (PV)**

In the "Engagement with Emerging Technology" section, under the column *"Report being 'somewhat' or 'very' familiar,"* solar panels/photovoltaic (PV) is the only concept shown with all three party symbols — Democrats (donkey), Republicans (elephant), and Independents (I) — accompanied by the note *"We're all on the same page!"* This indicates that familiarity with solar panels/PV was shared across all three political groups.
