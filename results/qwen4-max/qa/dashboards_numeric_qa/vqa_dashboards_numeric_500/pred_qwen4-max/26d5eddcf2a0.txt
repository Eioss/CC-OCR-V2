According to the 2013 survey shown in the infographic, under the section **“Was the online program worth the financial investment?”**, the responses are:

- **65%** — Yes, completely  
- **31%** — Somewhat  
- **4%** — No, not at all  

The question asks for the percentage of students who **do not agree** that the program was worth the financial investment. This corresponds to the **“No, not at all”** category.

> ✅ **Answer: 4%**

This is the only group that explicitly disagrees with the statement. The “Somewhat” group indicates partial agreement, so they are not counted as disagreeing.
