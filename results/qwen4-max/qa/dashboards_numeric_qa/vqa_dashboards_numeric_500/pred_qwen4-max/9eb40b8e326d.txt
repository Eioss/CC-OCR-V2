This means spam decreased by **20 percentage points** from 2010 to 2013.

> ⚠️ Note: This is a **20 percentage point decrease**, not a 20% *relative* decrease. If you were calculating relative percent decrease (i.e., what percent of the original 89% was lost), it would be:
>
> `(20 / 89) × 100 ≈ 22.47%`
>
> But since the question asks “how much percentage... decreased”, and given the context of comparing two percentages over time in an infographic like this, it’s standard to report the **absolute difference in percentage points** — which is **20**.
