**65%**

According to the rainbow-style chart on the right ("Internet usage in over 50s"), the age bands map to the percentages as follows:

- 50–55 year olds → 85%
- 55–59 year olds → 90%
- 60–64 year olds → 82%
- 65–69 year olds → 76%
- **70–74 year olds → 65%**
- 75+ year olds → 41%

So Australians aged 70–74 had a 65% internet usage rate in the 2011 survey.
