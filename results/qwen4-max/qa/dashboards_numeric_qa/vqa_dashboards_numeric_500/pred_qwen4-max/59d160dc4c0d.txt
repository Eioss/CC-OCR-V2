The candidate for president is officially chosen at the **National Party Conventions**.

As explained in the infographic under “HOW DO THE CANDIDATES WIN?”:

> The electorate votes for presidential nominees at Caucuses and State Primaries. Then party delegates from each state go to the National Party Conventions to officially choose a party candidate for president.

So, while voters express their preference in primaries and caucuses, it’s the **delegates** who formally select the nominee at the **National Party Convention**.
