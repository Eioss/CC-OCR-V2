Looking at the map, the teal/blue color (Eastern Wild Turkey) covers the entire eastern half of the United States, stretching from the Midwest across to the Atlantic coast and down through the Southeast. This is by far the largest contiguous colored region on the map.

In comparison:
- **Red** (Rio Grande) covers parts of Texas, Oklahoma, and the Southwest.
- **Green** (Hybrid) covers a band through the central plains.
- **Orange** (Osceola) is limited to Florida.

**Blue** covers the major area of the map.
