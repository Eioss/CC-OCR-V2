Based on the map, the **Eastern Wild Turkey** (shown in teal/blue) is found across the largest area of the country. Its range covers most of the eastern half of the United States — stretching from the Northeast and New England down through the Southeast (including Florida's panhandle), across the Midwest, and into parts of the central states. This is by far the most extensive colored region on the map.

For comparison, the other types occupy much smaller ranges:
- **Merriam's Wild Turkey** (purple) – scattered across the mountainous West
- **Rio Grande Wild Turkey** (red) – central/southern plains (Texas, Oklahoma, etc.)
- **Hybrid Turkey** (green) – a narrow band in the central plains
- **Osceola Wild Turkey** (orange) – limited to southern Florida

So the Eastern Wild Turkey dominates the map in terms of geographic coverage.
