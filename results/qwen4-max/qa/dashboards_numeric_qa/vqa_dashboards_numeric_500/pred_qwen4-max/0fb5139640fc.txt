To find the number of reported cases **not from Chinese Mainland**, we use the data provided for **COVID-19 in China as of February 21, 2020**:

- **Total reported cases**: 75,569  
- **Cases in Chinese mainland**: 75,467 (which is 99% of total)

So, subtracting:

> **75,569 – 75,467 = 102**

✅ Therefore, the number of reported cases **not from Chinese Mainland** is **102**.

*(Note: These likely include cases in other parts of China such as Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan — though the infographic doesn’t specify.)*
