Based on the infographic, the emergency benefits provided to working Canadians under Canada's Economic Response Plan are:

1. **Emergency Care Benefit**
   - For those who must stay home due to COVID-19 and don't have paid sick leave (including those who are sick, can't access EI, or are caring for a sick person or child).
   - Provides **up to $900 bi-weekly for 15 weeks**.

2. **Emergency Support Benefit**
   - For workers who are not eligible for EI and who are facing unemployment and reduced hours.
   - Backed by **up to $5 billion** in support.

These fall under the broader "$27 Billion – Direct support for Canadian Workers and Businesses" portion of the plan.
