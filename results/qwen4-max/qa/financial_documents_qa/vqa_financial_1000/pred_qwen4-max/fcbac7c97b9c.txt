The **Howard W. Blakeslee Awards** are granted for distinguished media communication regarding cardiovascular diseases.

According to the document, recipients included JoAnn Ellison Rodgers of the *Baltimore News American* (for a four-part newspaper series on heart disease in women), the CBS-TV series *Good Times* (for the episode "The Check Up"), and Andy Guthrie of WKYC-TV News, Cleveland (for the report "The Ambulance Crisis: Who Will Come for You?").
