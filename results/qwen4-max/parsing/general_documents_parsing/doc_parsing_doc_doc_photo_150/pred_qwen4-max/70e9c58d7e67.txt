48.

The Discriminant

The formula for finding the discriminant is derived from the quadratic equation.

Discriminant formula: $b^{2}-4ac$

When:

* $b^{2}-4ac>0$
    * there are two unequal real solutions.
    * if the discriminant is a perfect square, the solutions are rational.
    * if the discriminant is not a perfect square, the solutions are irrational

* $b^{2}-4ac=0$
    * there is one solution that is a real number

* $b^{2}-4ac<0$
    * there are no real solutions
    * there are two imaginary solutions
    * the solutions are complex conjugates
