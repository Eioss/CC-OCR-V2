The image lists two emergency benefits for working Canadians:

- **Emergency Care Benefit:** Up to **$900 biweekly for 15 weeks** for people who must stay home due to COVID-19 and don’t have paid sick leave, including those who are sick or caring for a sick person or child.
- **Emergency Support Benefit:** Up to **$5 billion in support** for workers who are not eligible for Employment Insurance (EI) and face unemployment or reduced hours.
