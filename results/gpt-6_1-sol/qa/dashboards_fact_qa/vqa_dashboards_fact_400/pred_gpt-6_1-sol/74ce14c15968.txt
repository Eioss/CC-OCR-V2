Ban Ki-moon’s **“Education First” initiative**, launched as United Nations Secretary-General, focused on three priorities:

1. **Put every child in school** — remove barriers to education and reach children affected by poverty, conflict, and other disadvantages.
2. **Improve the quality of learning** — support well-trained teachers, better learning materials, and strong literacy and numeracy skills.
3. **Foster global citizenship** — promote peace, tolerance, inclusion, and environmental responsibility through education.

The initiative also called for **closing the education financing gap** through greater investment and more effective use of resources.
