The document is an **audit agenda for DePuy Orthopaedics’ Production and Process Controls**, scheduled for **July 13–17, 2009, in Warsaw, Indiana**.

Its objective is to assess compliance with **21 CFR 820** and **ISO 13485:2003** in selected areas of the company’s quality management system. The audit will evaluate whether production and process controls are effectively implemented and, where appropriate, review the effectiveness of corrective actions identified through internal audits.

It is a targeted audit, not a full assessment of the entire quality system. The company may also identify specific areas of concern for particular focus.
