The table compares the performance of the **1975 and 1976 classes after taking a course on food and nutrition**, measured by the percentage of test answers agreeing with the course’s teachings.

It groups the 50 questions into three categories: established economic or scientific facts, confirmed scientific findings rejected by consumer activists, and issues involving scientists’ opinions versus consumer activists’ claims.
