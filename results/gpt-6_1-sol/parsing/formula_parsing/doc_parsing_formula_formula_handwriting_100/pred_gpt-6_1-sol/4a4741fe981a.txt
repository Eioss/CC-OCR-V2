\left|\mathbb{E}\left[F_t^{BA}(s_t,a_t)\mid\mathcal{F}_t\right]\right|

\mid\mathcal{F}_t\right]\right|\\

\min\left\{Q_t^B(s_{t+1},b^*),Q_t^A(s_{t+1},a^*)\right\}
-\min\left\{Q_t^A(s_{t+1},b^*),Q_t^B(s_{t+1},b^*)\right\}
\mid\mathcal{F}_t\right]\\

Q_t^B(s_{t+1},b^*)<Q_t^A(s_{t+1},b^*),\mathcal{F}_t\right]\\
&\quad-\gamma P\left(Q_t^B(s_{t+1},b^*)\geq Q_t^A(s_{t+1},b^*)\mid\mathcal{F}_t\right)
\mathbb{E}\left[Q_t^A(s_{t+1},b^*)\mid
Q_t^B(s_{t+1},b^*)\geq Q_t^A(s_{t+1},b^*),\mathcal{F}_t\right]\\
