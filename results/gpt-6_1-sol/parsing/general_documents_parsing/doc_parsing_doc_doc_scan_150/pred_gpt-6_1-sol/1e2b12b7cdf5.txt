\section*{5 \quad PRESENT PERFECT}

\begin{tabular}{|>{\bfseries}p{3cm}|p{10cm}|}
\hline
Form & have or has + Past Participle \\
\hline
Use & For actions which started in the past and continued up to the present. \\
\hline
Signal words & just, already, yet, for, since, so far, recently, ever... \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\medskip

NB. We use Present Perfect Continuous (have/has+ been + verb +ing) to give
more emphasis on the duration of the action. However, some verbs can't be
used in the continuous form such as own, smell, need, belong, cost, agree,
contain..., etc.

\subsection*{EXERCISE 1 \quad Find the signal words for present perfect.}

\item Which is \textbf{not} a signal word for present perfect?\\
since -- before -- for
\end{enumerate}
