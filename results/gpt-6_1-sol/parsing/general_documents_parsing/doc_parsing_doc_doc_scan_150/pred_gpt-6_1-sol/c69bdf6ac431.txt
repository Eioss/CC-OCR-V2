\section*{III. CALCULATIONS:}

Calculating the volume of the given examined bodies based on the formula:
\[
V_b = a \cdot b \cdot c
\]

$V_b$ -- volume of examined bodies $[\mathrm{cm}^3]$,
$a$ -- width of examined bodies $[\mathrm{cm}]$,
$b$ -- length of examined bodies $[\mathrm{cm}]$,
$c$ -- height of examined bodies $[\mathrm{cm}]$.

\[
V_b = 2{,}5\,[\mathrm{cm}] \cdot 4{,}2\,[\mathrm{cm}]
\cdot 1{,}5\,[\mathrm{cm}]
= 15{,}75\,[\mathrm{cm}^3]
\]

Calculating the material density of the given examined bodies based on the formula:
\[
\rho_b = \frac{m_b}{V_b}
\]

$\rho_b$ -- material density of given body $[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]$,
$m_b$ -- mass of given body $[\mathrm{g}]$,
$V_b$ -- volume of examined body.

\[
\rho_b\,(\text{ebonite})
= \frac{22{,}7}{15{,}75}\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 1{,}44127\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 1441{,}27\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3]
\]

\[
\rho_b\,(\text{bakelite})
= \frac{19{,}8}{15{,}75}\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 1{,}25714\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 1257{,}14\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3]
\]

\[
\rho_b\,(\text{aluminium (101)})
= \frac{42{,}6}{15{,}75}\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 2{,}70476\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 2704{,}76\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3]
\]

Calculating the material density using areometer based on the formula:
\[
\rho_b' = \frac{m_1-m_2}{m_3-m_2}\,\rho_w
\]

$\rho_b'$ -- material density of given body using areometer
$[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]$,
$m_n$ -- values of mass from experiment $[\mathrm{g}]$,
$\rho_w$ -- density of distilled water $=1\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]$.

\[
\begin{aligned}
\rho_b'\,(\text{ebonite})
&= \frac{110-87}{103-87}\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{g}]
\cdot 1\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3] \\
&= \frac{23}{16}\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 1{,}4375\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 1437{,}5\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3].
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
\rho_b'\,(\text{bakelite})
&= \frac{110-90}{105-90}\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{g}]
\cdot 1\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3] \\
&= \frac{20}{15}\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 1{,}33333\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 1333{,}33\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3].
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
\rho_b'\,(\text{aluminium (101)})
&= \frac{110-67}{83-67}\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{g}]
\cdot 1\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3] \\
&= \frac{43}{16}\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 2{,}6875\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 2687{,}5\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3].
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
\rho_b'\,(\text{aluminium (111)})
&= \frac{110-86}{94-86}\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{g}]
\cdot 1\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3] \\
&= \frac{24}{8}\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 3\,[\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 3000\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3].
\end{aligned}
\]

Comparing calculated density values with table density values:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\rho_b\,(\text{ebonite})-\rho_{\text{table}}\,(\text{ebonite})
&= 1441{,}27\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3]

= 22{,}48\,[\%]

\]

\[
\begin{aligned}

&= 42{,}86\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3]
= 3{,}35\,[\%]
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
\rho_b'\,(\text{bakelite})-\rho_{\text{table}}\,(\text{bakelite})
&= 1333{,}33\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3]
-1300\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3] \\
&= 33{,}33\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3]
= 2{,}53\,[\%]
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}

= 0{,}17\,[\%]
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
\rho_b'\,(\text{aluminium (101)})-\rho_{\text{table}}\,(\text{aluminium})
&= 2687{,}5\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3]
-2700\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3] \\

= 0{,}46\,[\%]
\end{aligned}

\begin{aligned}

&= 3000\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3]
-2700\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3] \\
&= 300\,[\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3]
= 10{,}53\,[\%]
\end{aligned}
\]
