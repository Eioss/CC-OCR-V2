\sectiontitle{GENERAL DESCRIPTION}

The AW36526 is a inductorless triple channel flash \& Indicator 2in1
LED driver that provides a high level of adjustability within a ultra-small
solution size. The AW36526 includes two current sources and a current sink
which is controlled independently by \(\mathrm{I}^{2}\mathrm{C}\)-compatible
interface. The AW36526 provides IVFM protection mode to prevent system
reset or shutdown under low battery condition.

\bigskip

The AW36526 is controlled via an \(\mathrm{I}^{2}\mathrm{C}\)-compatible
interface. The main features of the AW36526 include: flash / torch / IR /
Indicator current, flash timeout duration, IVFM and breath features. The
AW36526 also provides hardware flash pin (STROBE) to control flash event.

\bigskip

The device operates over a \(-40^{\circ}\mathrm{C}\) to
\(+85^{\circ}\mathrm{C}\) ambient temperature range.

\bigskip

The AW36526 is available in small 0.4mm pitch
\(1.6\mathrm{mm}\times1.2\mathrm{mm}\) FCQFN-10L package.
