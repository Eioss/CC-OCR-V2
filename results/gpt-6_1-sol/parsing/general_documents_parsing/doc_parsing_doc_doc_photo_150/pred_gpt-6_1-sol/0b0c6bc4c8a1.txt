\bigskip
\noindent
\textbf{SECTION A: READING SKILLS}
\hfill \textbf{(12+10=22 Marks)}
\hfill \textbf{50 Periods}

\begin{itemize}
    \item The section will have two unseen passages with the maximum word limit of 750 words. The passages can have continuous and non-continuous text drawn from the themes in prescribed books. Please refer to the Main Course Book (MCB) for the types of non-continuous texts. (For example --Unit 1 has lists, tables, cues, message, telephonic conversation etc.).

    \item Objective Type Questions (including Multiple Choice Questions). Very Short Answer Type Questions (one word/ one phrase / one sentence) and Short Answer Type Questions (30-40 words each) will be asked to test interpretation, analysis, inference, evaluation and vocabulary in context.
\end{itemize}

\bigskip
\noindent
\textbf{SECTION B: WRITING SKILLS}
\hfill \textbf{22 Marks}
\hfill \textbf{50 Periods}

\medskip
\noindent
This section will have a variety of short and long writing tasks.

\begin{itemize}
    \item Email to school authorities (Application for leave/ change of subject/ change of section/ bus-timings or similar topics) in maximum 50 words
    \hfill \textbf{3 Marks}

    \item Factual Description of a person/object in maximum 100 words
    \hfill \textbf{4 Marks}

    \item One out of two formal letters, in maximum 120 words, thematically aligned to topics in MCB.
    \hfill \textbf{7 Marks}

    \item One out of two articles based on verbal cues, in maximum 150 words, thematically aligned to MCB topics.
    \hfill \textbf{8 Marks}
\end{itemize}

\bigskip
\noindent
\textbf{SECTION C: GRAMMAR}
\hfill \textbf{10 Marks}
\hfill \textbf{20 periods}

\medskip
\noindent
Grammar items will be taught and assessed over a period of time.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Tenses
    \item Modals
    \item Subject -- verb concord
    \item Reported speech
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Commands and requests
        \item Statements
        \item Questions
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
