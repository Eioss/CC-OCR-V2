The infographic groups the **long-term benefits** of early childhood education into two categories:

**Student Success**
- Students are **29% more likely to graduate** from high school.
- Students are **44% less likely to repeat a grade**.
- Students **pass standardized tests more frequently**.

**Adult Responsibility**
- Adults age 40 who had early childhood education are **more likely to be employed**.
- These 40-year-olds also have a **33% higher average income**.
- 18-year-olds **without** early childhood education are **70% more likely to be arrested for a violent crime**.

Overall, early childhood education is linked to better academic performance, stronger employment and earnings in adulthood, and lower involvement in violent crime.
