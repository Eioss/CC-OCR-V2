**Asia** is the most populous continent. According to the infographic's "Where do we all live?" pie chart, it is home to **60%** of the world's population, far more than Africa (14%), Europe (11%), North America (8%), South America (5.3%), and Australia (0.3%).

Note that the chart shows population share, not density (people per area). Asia is also the most densely populated continent by that measure, at roughly 150 people per km².
