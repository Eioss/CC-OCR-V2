At pre-match, the two highest values are:

1. **Preparedness**, which peaks at **100**, its maximum for the whole week.
2. **Fitness**, which remains high at about **80**.

By contrast, **fatigue** drops to about **10** and **training load** to **0**. This is the goal of the 2-day taper: reduce load so fatigue dissipates while fitness is maintained, leaving players as prepared as possible for the match.
