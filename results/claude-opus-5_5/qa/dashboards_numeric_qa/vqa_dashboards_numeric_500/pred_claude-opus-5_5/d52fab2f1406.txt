**South Korea** had the highest average broadband speed in Asia in Q2 2016, at **27 Mbps**, according to the infographic, which cites Akamai's State of the Internet Report.

It was well ahead of the next-fastest:
- Japan: 19.5 Mbps
- Singapore: 17.2 Mbps
- Hong Kong: 17.1 Mbps
- Taiwan: 15.6 Mbps

The infographic also notes that South Korea led the entire world in broadband speed, followed by Norway and Hong Kong.
