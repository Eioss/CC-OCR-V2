Australia has **six states**:

1. **NSW**: New South Wales
2. **VIC**: Victoria
3. **QLD**: Queensland
4. **WA**: Western Australia
5. **SA**: South Australia
6. **TAS**: Tasmania

The image shows eight regions, but two of them are **territories**, not states:

- **NT**: Northern Territory
- **ACT**: Australian Capital Territory, which contains the capital, Canberra

So Australia has 6 states and 2 mainland territories, for 8 major jurisdictions in total. It also has several smaller external territories, such as Christmas Island and Norfolk Island.
