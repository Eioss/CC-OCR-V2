According to the infographic, you should sanitize your hands for **at least 20 seconds**.

The poster recommends these steps:

1. **Use a sanitizer with 60% or higher alcohol content.**
2. **Apply the product** to the palm of one hand.
3. **Rub your hands together.**
4. **Cover all surfaces** of your hands and fingers, and keep rubbing until your hands feel dry (about 20 seconds).

The same **20-second** guideline applies to washing your hands with soap and running water, which the poster also shows.
