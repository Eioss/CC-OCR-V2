**Females.** According to the infographic, the annual payroll of female-owned businesses grew **36.52%** from 2007 to 2015, compared to **16.54%** for male-owned businesses.

Note that these figures measure payroll **growth**, not total payroll. Since men own more than 75% of American businesses, male-owned firms likely still have a larger total payroll. Female-owned firms simply increased theirs at more than twice the rate.
