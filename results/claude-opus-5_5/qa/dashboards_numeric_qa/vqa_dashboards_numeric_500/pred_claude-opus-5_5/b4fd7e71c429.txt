**82%**

The infographic breaks down cancer incidence in women by age:
- 50 to 64 years: 27%
- 65 to 74 years: 25%
- Over 74 years: 30%

27% + 25% + 30% = **82%**, which matches 100% minus the 18% of cases that occur under age 50.

Note that this figure means 82% of cancers in Irish women occur in those aged 50 and over. It does not mean 82% of women over 50 get cancer.
