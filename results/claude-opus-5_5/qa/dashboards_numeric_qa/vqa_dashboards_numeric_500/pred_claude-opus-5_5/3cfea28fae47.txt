**Hong Kong**, at **9.5 Mbps**, is second only to South Korea (11.1 Mbps).

Two caveats:
- **New Zealand** is listed at 9.8 Mbps, but it is not in Asia, so I excluded it.
- **Hong Kong** is a special administrative region rather than an independent country. If you count only sovereign countries, **Taiwan** (9.3 Mbps) is next, though its status is also disputed. Among universally recognized countries, it would be the **Philippines** (8.5 Mbps).
