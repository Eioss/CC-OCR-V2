The infographic doesn't give **monetary amounts** for fire damage or theft claims. It shows each category's **share of all home insurance claims** in the UK:

- **Fire damage:** 10% of claims
- **Theft:** 14% of claims
- **Combined:** 24%, nearly a quarter of all claims

The only claim amount mentioned is for burst pipes (water damage), which average **£25,000**. The actual amount a homeowner can claim for fire or theft depends on their policy's coverage limits.
