According to the infographic, Pinterest can help businesses in **5 ways**:

1. **Creates Backlinks to Your Site**: Every time a pin is uploaded or repinned, a new link back to your website is created.
2. **Boosts Your Social Signals**: Pins generate social signals, which Google and other search engines factor into rankings.
3. **Opportunity for New Content**: Posting fresh, original content appeals to your audience and is rewarded by search engines.
4. **Boosts Your Keyword Targeting**: Search engines scan Pinterest, so well-used keywords can raise your page ranking.
5. **SEO Image Boost**: Search engines index images, so using keyword-rich file names, full descriptions, and your business name helps your SEO.
