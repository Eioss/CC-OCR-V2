According to the infographic, **senior citizens (aged 65 and over) have a 50.7% chance of having a disability**, roughly **1 in 2 people**. That is about **1.8 million** older Australians.

For comparison, the rate for people under 65 is just 12.5% (1 in 8), which reflects the infographic's point that the likelihood of living with disability increases with age.
