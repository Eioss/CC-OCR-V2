The infographic shows that **20%** of those polled agreed that *"Jews' loyalty to Israel makes them less loyal to Britain than other British people."* So it is Jews' loyalty to **Britain** that is being questioned, based on a perceived loyalty to **Israel**.

This reflects the "dual loyalty" accusation, a long-standing antisemitic stereotype that Jews cannot be fully loyal citizens of their own countries. The infographic presents it as one of several prejudiced attitudes found in the survey.
