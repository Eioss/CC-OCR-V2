According to this infographic, **fever occurred in 98% of patients**, so about **2% did not have a fever**.

Keep in mind that this figure comes from a small number of early hospitalized cases in Wuhan (the Huang et al. study in *The Lancet* included 41 patients). Later research with larger and broader groups, including milder cases, found that a much larger share of COVID-19 patients never develop a fever, so the 2% figure likely underestimates how common fever-free infections are.
