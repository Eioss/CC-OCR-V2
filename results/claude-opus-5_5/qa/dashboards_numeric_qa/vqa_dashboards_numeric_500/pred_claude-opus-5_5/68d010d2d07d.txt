According to the infographic, **prescription drugs** cause the most overdose deaths in the US. In 2014, out of **47,055 total overdose deaths**:

- **Prescription drugs:** ~25,000+ deaths (more than half)
- **Heroin:** ~11,000 deaths
- **Cocaine:** ~5,500 deaths

The infographic also notes a **200% increase in overdose deaths involving opioids** (prescription painkillers or heroin) between 2000 and 2014, which shows opioids are the core of the problem.

Note that these figures are from 2014. Since then, illicitly made synthetic opioids, especially **fentanyl**, have overtaken prescription drugs as the leading cause of US overdose deaths.
