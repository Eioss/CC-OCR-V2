The image shows two emergency benefits for working Canadians under **Canada's COVID-19 Economic Response Plan**:

### 1. Emergency Care Benefit
- **Amount:** Up to **$900 bi-weekly** for up to **15 weeks**
- **Who it's for:** People who must stay home due to COVID-19 and don't have paid sick leave, including:
  - Those who can't access Employment Insurance (EI) and are sick
  - Those caring for a child or someone who is sick

### 2. Emergency Support Benefit
- **Amount:** Up to **$5 billion** in total support
- **Who it's for:** Workers who are **not eligible for EI** and are facing unemployment or reduced hours

### Broader Context
These benefits are part of a larger package that includes:
- **$27 billion** in direct support for Canadian workers and businesses
- **$55 billion** in business liquidity support through tax deferrals

**Note:** This graphic reflects the plan as first announced in March 2020. Shortly afterward, these two benefits were combined into the **Canada Emergency Response Benefit (CERB)**, which offered $2,000 per four-week period. CERB was later replaced by EI changes and other recovery benefits.
