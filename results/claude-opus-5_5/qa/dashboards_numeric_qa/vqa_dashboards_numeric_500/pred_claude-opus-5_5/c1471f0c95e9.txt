According to the timeline, the first event after **hospital admission (day 7)** is **shortness of breath**, which appears at a median of about **day 8** after symptom onset.

It is followed by ARDS (acute respiratory distress syndrome) around day 9, then intensive care unit admission around day 10–11.

These are median times from a small group of early cases in Wuhan, published February 2020, so they may not reflect later data.
