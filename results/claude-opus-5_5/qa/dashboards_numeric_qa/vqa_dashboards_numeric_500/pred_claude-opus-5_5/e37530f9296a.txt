According to the infographic's footnote, the fatality rate is expressed **per 100,000 full-time workers (or equivalent)**, using the Bureau of Labor Statistics' formula:

**Fatality rate = (Number of fatal injuries ÷ Total hours worked by all employees during the calendar year) × 200,000,000**

The 200,000,000 figure represents the hours that 100,000 full-time workers would work in a year (100,000 workers × 40 hours/week × 50 weeks). Using hours worked rather than headcount accounts for part-time workers and makes rates comparable across occupations.

For example, logging workers' rate of 135.9 means about 136 deaths for every 100,000 full-time-equivalent workers in that occupation.
