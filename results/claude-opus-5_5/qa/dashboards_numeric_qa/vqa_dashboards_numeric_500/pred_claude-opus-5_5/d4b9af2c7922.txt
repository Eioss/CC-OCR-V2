**About 2.8 million.** The infographic says there are over 6 million disabled people of working age in the UK, and only about 3.2 million are employed (6 million − 3.2 million ≈ 2.8 million).

Strictly, this figure counts everyone *not in employment*. That includes people who are economically inactive (not looking for work), not just those officially classed as "unemployed."
