According to this graphic, **56%** of patients did not experience myalgia or fatigue (100% − 44% = 56%).

A few caveats:

- **The 44% combines two symptoms.** It counts patients with myalgia (muscle pain) *or* fatigue, so the share of patients without fatigue specifically could be higher than 56%.
- **The sample is small and early.** As the banner notes, the data comes from a small number of early cases in Wuhan, China (published February 2020). Later research with larger populations and newer variants may show different rates.
