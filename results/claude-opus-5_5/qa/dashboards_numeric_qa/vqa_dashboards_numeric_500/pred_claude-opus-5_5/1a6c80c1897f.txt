The infographic compares February 2020 COVID-19 reports from **China** and the **United States**:

- **China (as of February 21, 2020):** 75,569 reported cases, 75,467 (99%) of them in mainland China, and 2,239 deaths.
- **United States (as of February 24, 2020):** 14 cases diagnosed through the US health care system, 39 cases among repatriated US citizens, 0 deaths, 0 critically ill patients, and no evidence of community-wide transmission.

It also sets these against **US influenza** data (as of February 15, 2020) to argue that the flu posed the greater public health threat in the US at the time.
