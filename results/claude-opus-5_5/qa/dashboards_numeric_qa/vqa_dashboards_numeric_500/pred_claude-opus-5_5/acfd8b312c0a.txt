The **0–14 age group** is the one clearly shrinking. Its share of the world population peaks at about 35% around 1965–1970, then falls steadily to roughly 17–18% by 2050.

The 15–59 group also dips slightly after 2017, from about 67% to about 62%. Meanwhile, the 60+ group grows from about 8% in 1955 to a projected 21.3% by 2050.

Note that the chart shows each group's **percentage share** of the population, not absolute numbers.
