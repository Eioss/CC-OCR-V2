According to the infographic, cigarette smoke contains **4,000 chemicals**, including **70 cancer-causing chemicals** such as radioactive polonium.

Two notes: the infographic labels it "Polonium-201," but the radioactive element in tobacco smoke is actually **Polonium-210**. Also, more recent estimates from sources like the U.S. Surgeon General and the National Cancer Institute put the total at **over 7,000 chemicals**, with at least 69–70 known to cause cancer.
