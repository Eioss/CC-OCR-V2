This document is an **Audit Agenda** for a **Production & Process Controls Audit** at **DePuy Orthopaedics, Inc.** in Warsaw, Indiana, scheduled for **13–17 July 2009**. It is page 2 of 5, marked "Confidential," and labeled as a protected document subject to a protective order.

**Audit Objective:**
- To assess compliance with **21 CFR 820** (the FDA Quality System Regulation for medical devices) and **ISO 13485:2003** (the international quality management standard for medical devices) for selected areas of the Quality Management System.

**Audit Scope:**
- Evaluate whether the quality management subsystem for Production and Process Controls has been effectively implemented.
- Review items previously identified for corrective action through internal audits, to check whether those actions were effective.
- The audit is **not** a full assessment of the Quality System. Other elements are out of scope unless issues surface during the assessment of the subsystems above.
- The company may identify areas within scope of special interest or concern for particular focus.

In short, it outlines the purpose and boundaries of a targeted regulatory compliance audit of a medical device manufacturer's production and process controls.
