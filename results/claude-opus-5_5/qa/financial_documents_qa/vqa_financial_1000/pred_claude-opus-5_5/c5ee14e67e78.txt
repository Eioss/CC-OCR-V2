**Table 1** shows **class performance recorded after students took a course on Food and Nutrition**. It reports the **percentage of students agreeing with the course teachings** for the **1975 and 1976 classes**, grouped by question type:

| Question Category | 1975 Class | 1976 Class |
|---|---|---|
| 17 questions on established economic or scientific facts | 88% | 86% |
| 17 questions on confirmed scientific findings still rejected by consumer activists | 82% | 78% |
| 16 questions pitting professional scientists' opinions against consumer activists' charges | 83% | 80% |
| **Entire test (50 questions)** | **84%** | **81%** |

In short, the table measures how well students in both years accepted the course's teachings, particularly on issues where scientific findings conflicted with consumer activists' claims. Agreement was slightly higher in the 1975 class across all categories.
