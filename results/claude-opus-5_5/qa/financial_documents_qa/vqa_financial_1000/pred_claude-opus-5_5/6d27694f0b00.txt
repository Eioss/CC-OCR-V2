For barnyard grass in the **N.C. Colorado District**, the primary recommendation is a **Roneet preplant**. The **option** is:

**Post with Betanal plus Betanal 475 with surfactant\*\* when the grass has 2 leaves or smaller.**

According to the footnote, the surfactant (\*\*) is Citowett Plus, mixed at 1 oz. to 10 gal.
