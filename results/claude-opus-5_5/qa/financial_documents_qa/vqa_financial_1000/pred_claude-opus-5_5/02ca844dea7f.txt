The stated objective is **"To provide a value added service to epilepsy centers."**

According to the description, this "service" is a quarterly newsletter personalized with each epilepsy center's name and logo, covering epilepsy news, legislation changes, and treatment advances. However, Parke-Davis retains editorial control and brands the newsletter with its drugs Neurontin and Cerebyx, so it also functions as a marketing tool. Sales reps (TMs) deliver it to physicians and staff they don't normally call on, such as neurosurgeons and anaesthesiologists.
