According to the infographic, **Anzac biscuits** were used in WWI soldier rations. The original versions served as a **bread substitute** and were **hard and savoury**, unlike the sweet oat, coconut and syrup biscuits we know today.

Historically, those ration biscuits were a type of hardtack, nicknamed "Anzac tiles" or "Anzac wafers." The sweet Anzac biscuit is generally linked to biscuits baked at home and sent to the troops, with the first known recipe published in 1917.
