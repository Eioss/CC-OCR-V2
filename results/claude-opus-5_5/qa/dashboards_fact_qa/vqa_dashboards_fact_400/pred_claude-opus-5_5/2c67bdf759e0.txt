The **Battle of Blore Heath** took place in **1459**, as shown in the infographic. The exact date was **23 September 1459**. It was one of the early battles of the **Wars of the Roses**, fought in Staffordshire, England.

According to the infographic:
- **Lancastrians:** commanded by Baron Audley and Baron Dudley, with 8,000–14,000 men
- **Yorkists:** commanded by the Earl of Salisbury, with 3,000–5,000 men
- **Outcome:** a Yorkist victory. The Yorkists feigned a retreat to lure the Lancastrian cavalry into a charge, and Baron Audley was killed. About 500 Lancastrians deserted to the Yorkists.
- **Casualties:** about 2,000 Lancastrians and 1,000 Yorkists
