According to the infographic, **close readers** ask "What happens in the text?" It's one of the "10 Questions All Close Readers Ask," shown in the speech bubbles under the street lamp on the right side.

The other questions in that section are:
- What is the text about?
- Who is the audience for the text?
- Who is speaking in the text?
- What words do I notice?
- What is the mood or feeling of the text?
- How does this part relate to what happens before and after?
- What is not being said?
- What did the author mean by ___?
- Why did the author write the text?
