According to the infographic, the two Thomas Edison inventions introduced at World Expos were:

1. **The Phonograph** at the 1878 Paris World Expo
2. **Electricity** at the 1904 St. Louis World's Fair, where Edison oversaw its presentation in the Palace of Electricity

Note that Edison did not actually invent electricity, which is a natural phenomenon. His real contributions were practical electrical technologies such as the long-lasting incandescent light bulb and electric power distribution systems.
