The **home antibody test**, the "finger-prick" test taken at home, is the one described as inaccurate. The infographic says these tests "are currently not accurate enough," so the Government is not offering them yet but plans to once more accurate tests are available.

The **laboratory antibody test**, a blood test taken by a medical professional, is the one being offered to NHS and social care staff, hospital inpatients, and care home residents.
