All four **Long Routes** are **loops**:

- **Ring of the Reeks Cycle**, MacGillycuddy Reeks, Co. Kerry (83 km / 52 miles)
- **Burren Cycling Route, Long Loop**, Co. Clare (150 km / 94 miles)
- **Inishowen Scenic Cycle**, Co. Donegal (160 km / 100 miles)
- **Ring of Kerry Scenic Cycle**, Co. Kerry (200 km / 125 miles)
