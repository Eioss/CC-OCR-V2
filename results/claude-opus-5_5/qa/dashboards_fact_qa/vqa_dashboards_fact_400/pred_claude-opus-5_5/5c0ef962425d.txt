According to the infographic, the three inventions featured at the **1904 St. Louis World's Fair** were:

1. **The Ice Cream Cone**: A waffle rolled by hand into a cone shape, debuting as the first edible, walk-away ice cream container. It was so popular that an industrial cone-rolling machine was developed soon afterward.

2. **Electricity**: The Palace of Electricity was a main attraction, celebrating the development of electricity for the masses, with Thomas Edison overseeing its presentation to the public.

3. **The Hot Dog**: Bavarian sausage seller Anton Feuchtwanger ran out of the gloves he gave customers to hold his sausages, so he had his brother-in-law create a sausage-sized long bun.
