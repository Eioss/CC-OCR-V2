The infographic doesn't show that car accidents cause suicides. They are **separate causes of death**.

The chart lists the **leading causes of death for 15–24 year olds** in Australia each year:

- **Suicide:** 23.3%
- **Car accidents:** 14%
- **Drugs and poisoning:** 4.2%
- **Motorcycle accidents:** 4.2%
- **Pedestrian accidents:** 2.9%
- **Cancer:** 2.4%

So **14% of deaths** among young people are from car accidents, while **23.3%** are from suicide. That's why the infographic says suicide accounts for roughly "one in four deaths" among young people, making it the leading cause of death in that age group.
