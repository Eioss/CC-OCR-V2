**The sun** is the renewable energy source.

In the infographic, it appears under the "Green Energy" branch with the tip "use the sun, open windows." Sunlight is naturally replenished and won't run out on any human timescale.

The other options aren't energy sources:

- **Bulbs, CFLs, and LEDs** are devices that *consume* electricity to make light.
- **CFLs and LEDs** are more energy-efficient than traditional incandescent bulbs, which is why the infographic suggests "switch to fluorescent." Efficiency reduces energy use, but it doesn't generate energy.
