**Carthage** had more infantry at the Battle of Zama. According to the infographic, Hannibal's Carthaginian army had **50,000 infantry**, compared to **34,000** for Scipio Africanus' Romans, a difference of 16,000.

Despite this, Rome won. Its cavalry, including 6,000 Numidian horsemen, drove the Carthaginian cavalry from the field, then returned to attack the Carthaginian lines from behind.
