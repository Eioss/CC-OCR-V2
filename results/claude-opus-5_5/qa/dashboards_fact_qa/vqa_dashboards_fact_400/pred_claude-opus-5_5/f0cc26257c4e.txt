Three stadiums were **started in 2007 and completed in 2009**:

1. **Cape Town Stadium** (Cape Town): capacity 68,000, cost $776.4m
2. **Nelson Mandela Bay** (Port Elizabeth): capacity 48,000, cost $281.1m
3. **Mbombela** (listed as Rustenburg): capacity 43,500, cost $147.2m

Moses Mabhida Stadium was also completed in 2009, but construction began in 2006. Peter Mokaba opened in 2010.
