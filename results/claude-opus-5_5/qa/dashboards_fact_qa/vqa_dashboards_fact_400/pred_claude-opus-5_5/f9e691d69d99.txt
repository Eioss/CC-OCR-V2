The infographic lists **1194–1184 BC** under Helen of Sparta, so it might seem to suggest she died in **1184 BC**. However, those dates are actually the traditional dates of the **Trojan War**, not Helen's lifespan.

Helen is a figure from Greek mythology, so there is no historical record of her birth or death. Ancient accounts of her fate also differ. In some she returns to Sparta with Menelaus and lives out her life there, while in others she is killed or made immortal.
