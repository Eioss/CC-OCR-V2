The infographic doesn't say that pedestrian accidents cause suicides. It lists **separate causes of death** among 15–24 year olds in Australia each year:

- **Suicide:** 23.3%
- **Car accidents:** 14%
- **Drugs and poisoning:** 4.2%
- **Motorcycle accidents:** 4.2%
- **Pedestrian accidents:** 2.9%
- **Cancer:** 2.4%

So **pedestrian accidents account for 2.9% of all deaths** in that age group, while suicide accounts for 23.3%, which is why the graphic says suicide is responsible for roughly "one in four deaths" among young people.

The right side of the infographic lists seven warning signs that someone may be suicidal, such as withdrawing from friends and family, talking about hopelessness, or self-harming. If you or someone you know is struggling, the graphic points to **Lifeline on 13 11 14** (Australia) or **headspace.org.au** for support.
