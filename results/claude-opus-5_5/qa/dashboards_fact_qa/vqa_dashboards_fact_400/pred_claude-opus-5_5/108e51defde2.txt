According to the infographic, the zipper was invented by **Whitcomb Judson**. It made its first public appearance at the **Chicago World's Fair in 1893**. Judson reportedly came up with the idea after seeing a stiff-backed friend struggling to do up his shoes.

Historically, Judson filed his patent for the device, called a "clasp locker," in 1891, and it was granted in 1893. The modern zipper design was later developed by Gideon Sundback around 1913.
