**None.** The infographic doesn't claim any appliance uses electricity when unplugged. It defines "vampire" appliances as devices that draw power **while plugged in, even though they're switched off**. Unplugging them stops the drain, which is why the infographic also advises you to "turn off & unplug appliances when not in use."

It lists **10** vampire appliances:

1. Flat Screen TVs
2. Gaming Consoles
3. Computers
4. DVRs / Cable Boxes
5. DVD / VCR players
6. Space Heaters
7. Phones
8. Printers
9. Routers / Modems
10. Kitchen Appliances
