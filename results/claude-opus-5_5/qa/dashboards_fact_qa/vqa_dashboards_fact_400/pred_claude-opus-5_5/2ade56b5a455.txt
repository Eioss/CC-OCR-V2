The infographic doesn't show what percentage of U.S. students live below the poverty line. The figure you're likely looking at is in the **"Access to Digital Tools by Socioeconomic Status"** chart, which shows the share of students who teachers say have access to digital tools:

- **Below poverty line/Low income, in school: 45%**
- Below poverty line/Low income, at home: 3%

So 45% describes **in-school access to digital tools** among low-income students. It is not the share of students who are below the poverty line.
