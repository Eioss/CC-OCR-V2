According to the infographic, the **Data and Analytics Manager** is the **"Data Science Team Leader."** Their primary role is to **manage a team of analysts and data scientists**.

**Mindset:** The infographic calls them the "Data Wizards' Cheerleader," meaning they support, motivate, and guide the team.

**Languages:** SQL, R, SAS, Python, Matlab, Java

**Key skills and talents:**
- Database systems (SQL and NoSQL based)
- Leadership and project management
- Interpersonal communication
- Data mining and predictive modeling

**Example employers:** Coursera, Slack, and Motorola Solutions

The role combines technical knowledge, so they understand and can contribute to the team's data work, with people and project management skills to coordinate the team and keep projects on track.
