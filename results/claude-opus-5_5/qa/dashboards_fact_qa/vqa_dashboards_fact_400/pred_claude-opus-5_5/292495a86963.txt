**The Coalition had far greater strength** during Operation Phantom Fury (2004), according to the infographic:

**Coalition** (commanders: U.S. Natonski/Mattis, U.K. Cowan)
- 10,500 U.S. troops
- 2,000 Iraqi troops
- 850 U.K. troops
- **Total: about 13,350 men**

**Insurgents** (commanders: Al-Janabi/Hadid)
- **Total: about 4,000 men**

The Coalition outnumbered the insurgents by more than **3 to 1**. It also had air power, bombing Fallujah for several weeks before the ground assault, and armored vehicles such as tanks.
