The infographic doesn't directly state how many people used *only* smartphones. It does say 17% installed the app on both tablets and smartphones. If you read the Venn diagram as overlapping groups of people, roughly 79% − 17% = **62%** would be smartphone-only users. However, the 79% and 21% figures add up to 100%, so they likely describe shares of access rather than groups of people, which makes that subtraction uncertain.
