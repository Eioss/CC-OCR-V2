According to the Department of Health & Social Care infographic, the two types of coronavirus (COVID-19) tests are:

1. **Swab test: "Do I have it?"**
   - A swab sample is taken from the throat and nose.
   - It shows whether a person **currently** has the virus, indicating whether they need to keep self-isolating, can return to work, or need to be re-tested.
   - Anyone with symptoms can request one through the NHS website, and essential workers get priority testing.

2. **Antibody test: "Have I had it?"**
   - It shows whether someone has **previously** had the virus, but not whether they are protected or immune from catching it again, so social distancing must still be followed.
   - It also helps scientists and the Government understand how the virus has spread.
   - It comes in two forms:
     - **Laboratory test:** A blood test taken by a medical professional, offered to NHS and social care staff, hospital inpatients, and care home residents based on clinical advice.
     - **Home test:** A "finger-prick" test done at home. At the time, these weren't accurate enough to be offered, though the Government planned to provide them once more reliable versions became available.
