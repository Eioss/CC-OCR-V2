According to this infographic, **online recruitment platforms** are the most popular job search method, used by **26%** of respondents. The other methods rank as follows:

- Personal research & contacts: 11%
- Recruitment agency: 9%
- Traditional media: 7%
- Social media: 6%

**A caveat:** These figures show how many people *use* each method, not how *successful* each one is at landing a job. The infographic also notes that **41% say they use all these methods**, which suggests that combining approaches is common and may be the most practical strategy.
