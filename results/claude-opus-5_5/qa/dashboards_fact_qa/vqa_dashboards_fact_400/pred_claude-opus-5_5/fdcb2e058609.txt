The infographic doesn't show suicides being caused by motorcycle accidents. It lists them as **separate causes of death** among 15–24 year olds in Australia:

- **Suicide:** 23.3% of deaths (about one in four)
- **Motorcycle accidents:** 4.2% of deaths

So motorcycle accidents account for **4.2% of all deaths** in this age group, not a percentage of suicides.

To compare them, suicide accounts for roughly **5.5 times as many deaths** as motorcycle accidents (23.3 ÷ 4.2 ≈ 5.5).
