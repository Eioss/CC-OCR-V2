**Canned dog food** has the longer shelf life. According to the infographic, unopened canned food lasts **18–24 months** from the date packaged, while unopened dry food lasts **12–15 months**.

Once opened, however, dry food lasts much longer. It keeps for **90 days** stored below 80°F in a cool, dry place, while opened canned food keeps for only **3 days** refrigerated below 40°F.
