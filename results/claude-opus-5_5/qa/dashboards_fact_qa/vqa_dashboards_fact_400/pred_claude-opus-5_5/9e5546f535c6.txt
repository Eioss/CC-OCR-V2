According to the infographic, antibody testing (which answers "Have I had it?") can be done in two ways:

1. **Laboratory test**: A blood test taken by a medical professional. At the time of the infographic, it was offered to NHS and social care staff, and to NHS hospital inpatients and care home residents based on their clinician's advice.

2. **Home test**: A "finger-prick" test taken at home. At the time, these tests were not considered accurate enough, so the Government was not offering them, though it planned to once more accurate tests became available.
