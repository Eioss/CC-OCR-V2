The infographic presents **Education First**, the UN Secretary-General's initiative under Ban Ki-moon. It has three main priorities:

1. **Put every child in school**
2. **Improve the quality of learning**
3. **Foster global citizenship**

It also adds a fourth goal, **closing the financial gap**, to fund these priorities.

**1. Put every child in school**
61 million primary-aged children are out of school. To change this, the initiative calls for:
- Eliminating cost barriers to attending and completing school
- Identifying and helping children who need nutritional, health and social support
- Closing the gender gap at all education levels
- Building 4 million more classrooms, especially in rural and conflict-affected areas
- Doubling (or more) education's share of humanitarian aid

**2. Improve the quality of learning**
250 million children cannot read, write or count well, even after at least four years in school. The initiative calls for:
- 2 million more well-trained teachers, with effective measures of student performance
- Better traditional and digital learning materials in students' home languages
- A 45% increase in early-childhood education programs
- A better match between schooling and needed workforce skills, plus more adult learning

**3. Foster global citizenship**
The initiative says education should:
- Promote peace, tolerance and respect for diversity across cultures
- Cultivate a sense of community, active participation and environmental stewardship
- Train teachers to use innovative, participatory and non-formal teaching methods

**4. Close the financial gap**
To pay for these goals, it calls for:
- Generating sufficient funding through sustained global advocacy
- Increasing global investment in poor countries' school systems by $24 billion per year
- Investing at least 5–6% of countries' national GDP in education
- Using resources effectively and accountably, in line with national education plans
