According to the chart, the **Surrender of Burgoyne** happened on **October 17**, as labeled directly above the illustration. Historically, this was **October 17, 1777**, when British General John Burgoyne surrendered his army to American General Horatio Gates after the Battles of Saratoga in New York. It was a major turning point in the American Revolutionary War because it helped convince France to formally ally with the United States.
