**The eastern part**, more precisely the central-eastern Midwest.

The "U.S. corn producing areas" map shows the major growing areas (dark green) concentrated in the Midwest Corn Belt, around Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Nebraska, and Missouri. The western U.S. has very little corn production. These corn areas fall inside the drought zone, where the main map shows severe to extreme (D2–D3) and even exceptional (D4) drought in states like Illinois, Indiana, Missouri, Kansas, and Nebraska.

This is why the expected corn harvest dropped from 14 billion bushels to 11 billion bushels.
