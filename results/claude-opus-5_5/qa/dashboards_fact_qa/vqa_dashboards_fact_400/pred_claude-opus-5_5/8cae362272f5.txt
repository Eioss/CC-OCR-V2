**The antibody test.**

In the "What does it mean?" section, the antibody test column (covering both the laboratory and home tests) says it helps "Scientists and the Government gain a better understanding of the spread of the virus." It can show whether someone has *had* the virus.

The swab test instead shows whether a person *currently* has the virus, which determines whether they need to self-isolate, can return to work, or need re-testing. An eye test isn't mentioned in the infographic.
