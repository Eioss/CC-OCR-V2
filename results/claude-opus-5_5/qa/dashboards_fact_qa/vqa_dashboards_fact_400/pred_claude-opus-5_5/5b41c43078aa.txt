The **"The Good News"** section highlights two good practices:

1. **Read to your children:** Introducing children to books and reading to them at home before they start school is a significant factor in their early educational success. *(National Commission on Reading, 1985)*

2. **Be a reading role model:** Children who know adults who read for pleasure take it for granted that reading is a valuable and worthwhile activity. *(Csikszentmihalyi, 1991)*
