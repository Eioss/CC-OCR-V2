**85%**, though the infographic doesn't mention cerebrospinal fluid. It says "Our brains are made up of **85% water**," under the Healthy Eating section. Cerebrospinal fluid is a separate fluid surrounding the brain and spinal cord, not the brain's overall water content.

The other figures refer to different things:
- **36%**: Brits who suffer from "Tech Hangovers"
- **58%**: how much plants can reduce depression/dejection

The 85% figure is also on the high side. Scientific sources usually put the brain's water content at about 75–80%.
