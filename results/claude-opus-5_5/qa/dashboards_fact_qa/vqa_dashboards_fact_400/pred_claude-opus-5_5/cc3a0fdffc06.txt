**The Burj Khalifa** is the tallest building shown. It's the stepped tower with the long spire on the left side of the image.

The Burj Al Arab doesn't appear to be in the illustration. It's known for its distinctive sail shape, and the building on the right is a straight rectangular tower with a red top. The only sail-like shape in the image is the small sailboat on the far left.

In reality, the Burj Khalifa stands about 828 m (2,717 ft), making it the world's tallest building, while the Burj Al Arab is about 321 m (1,053 ft).
