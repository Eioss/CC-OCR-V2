According to the infographic, **MySpace and Napster were both released in 1999**, the last year the Buffalo Bills made the NFL playoffs at the time it was made (around 2015).

However, that's only half right:

- **Napster** did launch in **June 1999**, created by Shawn Fanning and Sean Parker.
- **MySpace** actually launched in **August 2003**, about four years later. In 1999, the popular social site was Friendster's predecessor era (sites like SixDegrees), and Friendster itself didn't arrive until 2002.

So the infographic is accurate about Napster but wrong about MySpace.
