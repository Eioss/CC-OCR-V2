The **Lancastrians** had more strength in numbers at the Battle of Blore Heath (1459):

- **Lancastrians** (commanded by Baron Audley and Baron Dudley): **8,000–14,000 men**
- **Yorkists** (commanded by the Earl of Salisbury): **3,000–5,000 men**

However, the larger army didn't win. The Yorkists used tactics to offset their smaller numbers:
1. They set up defensively, with one flank against the woods and the other against an encircled wagon train.
2. They feigned defeat at their center to bait a Lancastrian cavalry charge, then slaughtered the attackers from their uphill position.
3. When the Lancastrians pushed across the stream, Baron Audley was killed.
4. During a second crossing attempt, 500 Lancastrians deserted to the Yorkists.

The infographic lists casualties of **2,000 Lancastrians** and **1,000 Yorkists**, which shows that the Yorkists' strategy overcame the Lancastrians' numerical advantage.
