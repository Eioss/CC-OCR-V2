According to the infographic, it's the **Ferris wheel**, which it describes as "now an amusement park fixture across the globe." (The infographic doesn't claim it's the *most* common fixture, only that it's found worldwide.)

It debuted as the largest attraction at the **1893 Chicago World's Fair**. Designed and built by George Washington Gale Ferris Jr., the original stood 80.4 metres tall and rotated on a 71-tonne, 45.5-foot axle.
