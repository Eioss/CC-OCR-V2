According to the infographic's timeline, the search technologies before 1995 were:

1. **Archie (1990)**: The first search engine, from before the World Wide Web. It didn't crawl sites but kept an index of public FTP servers so users could find and download files.
2. **Lycos (1994)**: Its index grew from 54,000 documents in 1994 to 60 million by 1996.
