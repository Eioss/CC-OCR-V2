According to the infographic, bones can be given to dogs in two forms: **whole or ground**. They're listed among the foods in Dr. Ian Billinghurst's "Biologically Appropriate Raw Food" diet.

Note that many veterinarians warn about bone risks. Cooked bones can splinter, and whole bones can cause choking or intestinal blockages, so it's worth checking with a vet before adding them to your dog's diet.
