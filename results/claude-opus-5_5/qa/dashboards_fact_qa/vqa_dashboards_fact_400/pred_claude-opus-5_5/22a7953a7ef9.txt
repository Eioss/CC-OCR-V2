According to the infographic, there are **two** dog food formulations, based on the two nutrient profiles in current AAFCO standards:

1. **Growth and reproduction**: for puppies and pregnant or nursing dogs
2. **Adult maintenance**: for adult dogs

The infographic also compares two forms of food, which differ in nutrient content:

- **Dry food** contains 8–22% fat, 18–32% protein, and 46–74% carbohydrates.
- **Canned food** contains 20–32% fat, 28–50% protein, and 18–57% carbohydrates.
