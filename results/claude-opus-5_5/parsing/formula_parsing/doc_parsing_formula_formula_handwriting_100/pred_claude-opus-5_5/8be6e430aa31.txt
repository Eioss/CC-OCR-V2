$$
\frac{\partial^2 \lambda_k}{\partial W_{pq}\,\partial W_{rs}} = \sum_{j \neq k}\left[\frac{\partial W_{kj}}{\partial W_{pq}}\frac{\partial \tau^{k}_{,j}}{\partial W_{rs}} + \frac{\partial W_{kj}}{\partial W_{rs}}\frac{\partial \tau^{k}_{,j}}{\partial W_{pq}}\right]
= \begin{cases}
\dfrac{1}{\lambda_k - \lambda_q}, & p = k,\ q \neq k,\ r = q,\ s = k; \\[2ex]
\dfrac{1}{\lambda_k - \lambda_s}, & r = k,\ s \neq k,\ p = s,\ q = k; \\[2ex]
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$
