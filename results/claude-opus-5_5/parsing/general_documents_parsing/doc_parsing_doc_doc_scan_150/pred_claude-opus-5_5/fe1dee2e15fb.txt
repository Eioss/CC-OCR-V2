\item \textbf{(3)}\\
Let H and h be the heights of the 2 candles.\\
Burnt amount at any time:\\
H: $\dfrac{120}{180} \times \text{H} = \dfrac{2}{3}\text{H}$\\[4pt]
h: $\dfrac{120}{300} \times \text{h} = \dfrac{2}{5}\text{h}$\\[4pt]
Remaining:\\
H: $\text{H} - \dfrac{2}{3}\text{H} = \dfrac{1}{3}\text{H}$\\[4pt]
h: $\text{h} - \dfrac{2}{5}\text{h} = \dfrac{3}{5}\text{h}$\\[4pt]
The ratio after 2 h is $1 : 1$.
\[
\frac{\frac{1}{3}\text{H}}{\frac{3}{5}\text{h}} = \frac{1}{1}
\]
Cross multiply:
\[
\frac{1}{3}\text{H} = \frac{3}{5}\text{h}
\]
Cross multiply:
\begin{align*}
5\text{H} &= 9\text{h}\\
\frac{\text{H}}{\text{h}} &= \frac{9}{5}
\end{align*}
H : h = 9 : 5

Height remaining:\\
\quad $1^{\text{st}}: 1 - \dfrac{n}{180}$\\[4pt]
\quad $2^{\text{nd}}: 1 - \dfrac{n}{240}$\\[4pt]

\[
\frac{1 - \frac{n}{180}}{1 - \frac{n}{240}} = \frac{1}{2}

\begin{align*}

\frac{8n - 3n}{720} &= 1\\
5n &= 720\\
n &= 144 \text{ min} = 2 \text{ h } 24 \text{ min}
\end{align*}
