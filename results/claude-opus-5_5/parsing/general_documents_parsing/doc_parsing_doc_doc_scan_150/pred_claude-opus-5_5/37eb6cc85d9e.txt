## Notes on the Transcription

- **Layout:** The two-column boxed structure of the original is reproduced with a bordered `tabular`. The Metric Prefixes section is separated from the variable definitions by a horizontal rule, and the definitions and footnote span both columns at the bottom.
- **Content:** All text, equations, table values, and the asterisk footnotes are kept exactly as shown, including the capital $S$ in the Standard Deviation formula and the lowercase $s$ in the Standard Error formula and the definitions.
- **Packages:** `amsmath` handles the equations and `multirow` handles the merged "*p* value" header cell in the Chi-Square table.
