{\rowcolors{1}{pinkbg}{pinkbg}

Form & am, is, or are + verb + ing \\ \hline
Use & actions happening now, at the moment of speaking. \\ \hline
Signal words & now, at the moment, still, Look! Listen!\ldots \\
\end{tabular}}

\begin{multicols}{2}

{\rowcolors{1}{pinkbg}{pinkbg}
\begin{tabular}{>{\bfseries}p{2.5cm}|p{5cm}}
Form & was or were + verb + ing \\ \hline
Use & For actions which were in progress in the past. \\ \hline

\end{tabular}}

\section*{5 \quad PRESENT PERFECT}

\begin{multicols}{2}

{\rowcolors{1}{pinkbg}{pinkbg}
\begin{tabular}{>{\bfseries}p{2.5cm}|p{5cm}}
Form & have or has + Past Participle \\ \hline
Use & For actions which started in the past and continued up to the present. \\ \hline
Signal words & just, already, yet, for, since, so far, recently, ever\ldots \\
\end{tabular}}

\medskip
{\small NB. We use Present Perfect Continuous (have/has+ been + verb +ing) to give more emphasis on the duration of the action. However, some verbs can't be used in the continuous form such as own, smell, need, belong, cost, agree, contain\ldots, etc.}

\exercise{1}{Find the signal words for present perfect.}
1. Which is a signal word for present perfect?\\
still -- usually -- since\\
2. Which is \textbf{not} a signal word for present perfect?\\
since -- before -- for

\columnbreak

\end{tabular}
