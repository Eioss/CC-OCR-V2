The authors introduce the Transformer, a sequence transduction
architecture that relies entirely on attention mechanisms and omits
recurrence and convolution. On WMT 2014 machine translation it reaches
28.4 BLEU (English--German) and 41.0 BLEU (English--French, single
model, after 3.5 days on eight GPUs). It also trains faster and
parallelizes better than prior models.
