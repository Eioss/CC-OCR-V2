Yasmin attempted to use the quadratic formula to determine the exact values of $x$ in
the equation $-2x^2 + 7x - 3 = 0$, but she made at least one error. Her work is
shown below.

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{2.5}
\begin{tabular}{c|c}
\begin{tabular}{@{}c@{}}Step\\ 1\end{tabular} &
$\dfrac{-(7) \pm \sqrt{(7)^2 - 4(-2)(-3)}}{2(-2)}$ \\
\hline
\begin{tabular}{@{}c@{}}Step\\ 2\end{tabular} &
$\dfrac{-7 \pm \sqrt{25}}{-4}$ \\
\hline
\begin{tabular}{@{}c@{}}Step\\ 3\end{tabular} &
$\dfrac{-7 \pm 5}{-4}$ \\
\hline
\begin{tabular}{@{}c@{}}Step\\ 4\end{tabular} &
$\dfrac{-7+5}{-4} = -8.25 \quad \text{and} \quad \dfrac{-7-5}{-4} = -5.75$ \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Yasmin recorded her \textbf{first} mistake in

  \begin{itemize}
    \item[$\square$] Step 1
    \item[$\square$] Step 4
    \item[$\square$] Step 2
    \item[$\square$] Step 3
  \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\end{document}

**Note on the math:** Steps 1–3 are correct, since $(7)^2 - 4(-2)(-3) = 49 - 24 = 25$ and $\sqrt{25} = 5$. The first mistake is in **Step 4**. The correct values are:

$$\frac{-7+5}{-4} = \frac{-2}{-4} = 0.5 \qquad \text{and} \qquad \frac{-7-5}{-4} = \frac{-12}{-4} = 3$$
