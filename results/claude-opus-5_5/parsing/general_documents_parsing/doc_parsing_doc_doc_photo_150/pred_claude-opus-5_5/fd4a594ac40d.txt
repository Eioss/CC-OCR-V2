## Notes on the Transcription
- **Header:** The Bengali header characters (অয়োময়, ৪৭, ১৩৭) are kept as in the original.
- **Blanks:** Each blank (\rule{1.6cm}{0.4pt}) stands for the dashed lines in the original.
- **Marks:** The mark notations ($0{\cdot}5\times6 = 3$ and $1\times4=4$) are right-aligned, as in the margin of the paper.
- **Substitution table:** The table keeps the original column structure. The vertically merged cells are approximated with `\multirow` and `\cline` to match the visual layout.
