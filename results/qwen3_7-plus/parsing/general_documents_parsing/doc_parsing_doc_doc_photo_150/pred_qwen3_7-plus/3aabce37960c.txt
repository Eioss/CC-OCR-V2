\section*{Test Your Reading}

\begin{tabular}{ll}
\textbf{Word Power} & \\
Extra & more than what is needed \\
Instead & as an alternative \\
Din & noise \\
Seize & catch by force \\
\end{tabular}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Complete the following statements :}
    \begin{enumerate}[(a)]
        \item They began \underline{heating} the iron and forging it again.
        \item The farmer \underline{led} the lord to the storeroom.
        \item Off rode the \underline{farmer} and the lord to get what he had earned.
        \item The lord \underline{heated} it well and threw it in a tub of water.
        \item The lord \underline{did not} want the coachman to know the shame.
    \end{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{Answer the following questions :}
    \begin{enumerate}[(a)]
        \item What did the lord try to make when he failed to make the plough ?
        \item What did the lord try to make when he could not make the axe ?
        \item What did the lord make at last ?
        \item How did the lord make a sizz ?
        \item Why did the lord take the sack to the farmer's home ?
        \item What did the strongmen do ?
    \end{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{Who said to whom :}

        \item I'll take the sack and go into the storeroom myself. \\

    \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
