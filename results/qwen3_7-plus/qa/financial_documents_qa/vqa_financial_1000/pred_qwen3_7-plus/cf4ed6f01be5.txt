Based on the provided text, **under-reaming of the acetabulum is dependent on two main factors**:

1.  **Bone quality**
2.  **The size of the acetabular component**

The text further elaborates:
*   A **1 mm under-ream** is usually sufficient for **smaller sockets**.
*   A **larger socket** may require **1–2 mm under-ream**.
*   **Soft bone** will more readily accommodate a greater press-fit than **sclerotic (hard/dense) bone**.

Therefore, the surgeon must consider both the physical properties of the patient's bone and the dimensions of the implant being used when determining the appropriate amount of under-reaming.
