The subject of this memorandum is:

**Change of Due Dates for Monthly Payroll Recommendations for December – Academic and Nonacademic**

This is clearly stated in the “SUBJECT” line of the memo. The body explains that due to fewer working days in December, the deadline for submitting payroll recommendations has been moved up to **December 14, 1967**, while paychecks will still be issued on **December 29, 1967, at 2:00 p.m.** (no change).
