This is a **Discipline Review Letter** from the U.S. Department of Health & Human Services, specifically from the Food and Drug Administration (FDA).

It was sent to Takeda Global Research & Development Center, Inc., regarding their supplemental New Drug Application (NDA) for Actos® (pioglitazone HCl) tablets. The letter communicates the FDA’s review findings — particularly that the PROactive trial failed to meet its primary endpoint, and therefore secondary endpoints cannot be considered statistically valid in this context. It also explains why moving certain data to the “Adverse Reactions” section is appropriate.

The letter is dated February 19, 2007, and marked as “Confidential – Subject to Protective Order.”

In short:  
> 📄 **Type**: Discipline Review Letter  
> 🏛️ **Issuer**: FDA (under DHHS)  
> 🎯 **Purpose**: Communicate regulatory feedback on clinical trial data and labeling changes for a drug application  
> ⚠️ **Key Point**: Trial failed primary endpoint → secondary endpoints not acceptable; data moved to Adverse Reactions section
