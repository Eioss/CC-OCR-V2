This is a **Western Union Telegram**.

It’s a formal, typed message sent via the Western Union telegraph service, dated December 30, 1961. The telegram informs Dr. Roger Adams of the death of his father (Mark Edwin Andrews’ father), noting he died quietly on Friday morning and expressing gratitude for Dr. Adams’ support as a trustee of the Welch Foundation.

Key features confirming it’s a telegram:
- “WESTERN UNION TELEGRAM” header
- Service class symbols (DL, NL, LT) in top right
- Timestamp: “1961 DEC 30 AM 10 45”
- Origin/destination codes: “PD=HOUSTON TEX 30 1005A CST=”
- Recipient address format typical of telegrams
- Message body ends with sender’s name followed by “=.” (standard telegram punctuation)

The handwritten notes at the bottom appear to be internal routing or filing marks added after receipt.
