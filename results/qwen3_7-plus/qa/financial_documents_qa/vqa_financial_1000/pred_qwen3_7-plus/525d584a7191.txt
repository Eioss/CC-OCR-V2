This memorandum was written by the **Dean’s Office** of Washington University School of Medicine in Saint Louis.

It is addressed to **Department Heads**, and the subject line indicates it concerns a change in due dates for monthly payroll recommendations for December 1967 — both academic and nonacademic staff.

The memo explains that because there are fewer working days in December, the Payroll Office on the Main Campus has moved up the deadline for submitting payroll recommendations to **no later than December 14, 1967**. It also confirms that paychecks will still be issued at the usual time: **2:00 p.m. on December 29, 1967**.

So while no individual name is signed, the authoritative source is clearly the **Office of the Dean**, as indicated in the “FROM” line and the letterhead.
