Lazlo Toth was arrested for **industrial espionage**.

According to the article, he was accused of taking photographs of a beet-sugar plant in Vrbas, a suburb of Belgrade, while vacationing in Yugoslavia. He worked for the Great Western Sugar Co. in Loveland, Colorado, and was sentenced to seven years in prison after a closed-door trial. He was later pardoned by the Yugoslav collective presidency, though his exact release date was unknown at the time of the report (July 16, 1976).
