Looking at the calendar for **December 2006**:

- The days of the week are labeled across the top: **Sun, Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat**.
- December 1st falls on a **Friday** (as shown in the first row under "Fri").
- We can count forward or look directly at the grid.

Find **28** in the calendar:
