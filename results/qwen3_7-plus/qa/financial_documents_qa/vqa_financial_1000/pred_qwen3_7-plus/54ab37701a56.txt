This is a **QA Sample Submission Form**.

It’s used to document details about a sample submitted for quality assurance review, including:
- Date of submission (9-19-95)
- Country of manufacture (Mexico)
- Facility where used (La Moderna, Monterrey, Mexico)
- Sample identification (Cane Sugar)
- Supplier info (Ingenio Beta San Miguel)
- Contact persons and phone/fax numbers
- Whether the sample is currently used or proposed
- Approval signature and date (10-3-95)

The form also includes fields for QA and SRA comments, and has handwritten annotations like “Reported 12-30” and circled numbers (9567, 9542), likely internal tracking IDs.
