Looking at the calendar for **December 2006**:

- The days of the week are labeled across the top: **Sun, Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat**.
- The first row shows that **Friday, December 1** and **Saturday, December 2** are the first two days of the month.
- That means **Sunday, December 3** is the first Sunday of the month.

✅ So, the first Sunday on December 2006 is **December 3**.
