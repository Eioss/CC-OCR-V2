> "Likewise, soft bone will more readily accommodate a greater press-fit of the acetabular component than sclerotic bone."

**Therefore, soft bone accommodates a greater press-fit of the acetabular component.**

This is because soft bone is less dense and more compliant, allowing it to deform slightly under pressure to accept a larger implant (greater press-fit), whereas sclerotic (hard, dense) bone is rigid and less able to accommodate such deformation without risk of fracture or poor fixation.

---

**Answer: Soft bone**
