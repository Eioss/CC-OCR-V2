This is a **packing slip** (also called a shipping slip or delivery note).

It’s used by Academic Press Inc. to document the details of a shipment sent to Academic Press Ltd in London. Key elements confirming this:

- The large stamped text “PACKING SLIP” diagonally across the document.
- It lists the item shipped: “ROSSER BANKSIAS VI BND ED”, quantity 1, with title code and list price.
- Includes shipping instructions (“SHIP #18 - UPS”, “INSURED - DO NOT CHARGE CUSTOMER”).
- Shows warehouse receipt date (“RECEIVED WAREHOUSE NOV 12 1982”).
- Has fields for picker, packer, checker, cartons, and handling charge — all typical of packing slips.
- Invoice number (129191) and memo reference are included for tracking.

While it references an invoice, its primary purpose here is to accompany the physical goods and confirm what was packed and shipped — hence, it’s a **packing slip**.

*(Note: In some contexts, especially older systems, “packing slip” and “invoice” might be combined on one form — but the prominent stamp and content here clearly label it as a packing slip.)*
