Based on the infographic provided:

Under the section **“FACTS ABOUT INFERTILITY”**, it states:

> **10% OF WOMEN**  
> Percentage of women in the United States between the ages of 15-44 considered infertile (6.1 million)

If **10%** are considered **infertile**, then the remaining percentage are considered **fertile**.

So, to find the percentage of women who are **fertile**:

> **100% - 10% = 90%**

---

✅ **Answer: 90%**

*(Note: The infographic defines infertility as not becoming pregnant after at least one year of unprotected sex — so “fertile” here is used in contrast to that clinical definition.)*
