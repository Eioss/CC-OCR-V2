In the **Decentralised** section of the infographic, there are **4 steps** of app processing shown:

1. **Step 1**: The app broadcasts a random ID and collects the random IDs of other apps it interacts with, plus interaction data (time and distance).
2. **Step 2**: If a user reports they are unwell, they can choose to upload their ID to a central database.
3. **Step 3**: Users’ phones regularly download the most up-to-date version of this database and look for IDs they have interacted with.
4. **Step 4**: If they see any ID(s) they have interacted with, their app calculates whether or not to inform them that they have been in contact with an infected person.

✅ So, the total number of steps given in the **Decentralised** section is **4**.
