The **X-ray machine** was introduced at the 1901 Buffalo Pan-American Exposition.

According to the infographic, when U.S. President William McKinley was shot during the exposition, doctors hesitated to use the X-ray machine on display — which was meant to locate the bullet — due to fear of unknown side effects.

This highlights both the novelty and public skepticism surrounding the technology at the time.
