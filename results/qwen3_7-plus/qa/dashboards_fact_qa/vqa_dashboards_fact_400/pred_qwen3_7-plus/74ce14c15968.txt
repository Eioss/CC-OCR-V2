Based on the infographic, Ban Ki-moon’s **Education First** initiative has three core pillars:

1. **Put every child in school**  
   - Goal: Ensure all children, especially the 61 million primary-aged out of school (32 million girls, 28+ million in conflict zones), access education.
   - Actions needed:
     - Eliminate cost barriers.
     - Provide nutritional, health, and social support.
     - Close gender gaps.
     - Build 4 million more classrooms (especially rural/conflict areas).
     - Double education’s share of humanitarian aid.

2. **Improve the quality of learning**  
   - Problem: 250 million children can’t read, write, or count well — even after 4+ years of school.
   - Actions needed:
     - Train 2 million more teachers with performance measures.
     - Improve learning materials in students’ home languages.
     - Increase early-childhood education by 45%.
     - Better align schooling with workforce needs.

3. **Foster global citizenship**  
   - Goal: Cultivate care for the world and others through education.
   - Actions needed:
     - Promote peace, tolerance, and respect for diversity.
     - Cultivate community participation and environmental stewardship.
     - Train teachers in innovative, participatory methods.

Additionally, to sustain these goals, the initiative calls to **close the financial gap**:
- Generate sustained global funding advocacy.
- Increase global investment by $24 billion/year in poor countries’ schools.
- Invest 5–6% of national GDP in education.
- Ensure accountable use of resources aligned with national plans.

Ban Ki-moon emphasizes that education reduces poverty, ends wasted potential, builds stronger societies, and is the “single best investment” for prosperous, healthy, equitable societies.

> *“When we put Education First, we can reduce poverty and hunger, end wasted potential—and look forward to stronger and better societies for all.”* — Ban Ki-moon
