Based on the infographic, we can calculate the percentage of people who used **only apps on smartphones** by analyzing the device usage data provided.

The infographic states:

- **79%** of learning materials were accessed via Apps by **smartphone**
- **21%** on their **tablets**
- **17%** installed the App on **both tablets and smartphones**

This implies that the 79% who used smartphones includes those who used *only* smartphones AND those who used *both* devices.

Similarly, the 21% who used tablets includes those who used *only* tablets AND those who used *both*.

To find the percentage who used **only smartphones**, we subtract the overlap (those who used both) from the total smartphone users:

> **Only Smartphone Users = Total Smartphone Users - Users of Both Devices**  
> **= 79% - 17% = 62%**

---

✅ **Answer: 62%**

*(Note: The 21% tablet users likely includes the 17% who used both, so only 4% used tablets exclusively — but the question asks for “only apps in smartphones”, which is 62%.)*
