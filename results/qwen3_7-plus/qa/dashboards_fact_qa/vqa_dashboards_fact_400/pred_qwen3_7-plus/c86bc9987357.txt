According to the infographic, **1,100,000 people** were left homeless from the Haiti earthquake.

This figure is listed under the section “Thousands of Lives Devastated,” alongside other key statistics such as estimated deaths (200,000), injuries (196,595), and those needing food (2,000,000).

The data is based on information available as of February 8, 2010 — note that the image mistakenly says “February 8, 2009” in the small print, but given the earthquake occurred on January 12, 2010, this is clearly a typo and should read **2010**.

✅ So, the number of homeless: **1.1 million**.
