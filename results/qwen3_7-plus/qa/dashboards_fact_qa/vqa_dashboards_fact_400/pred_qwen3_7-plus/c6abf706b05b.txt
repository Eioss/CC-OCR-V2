Based on the timeline in the infographic under “A QUICK HISTORY OF SEARCH ENGINE TECHNOLOGY,” the search technologies that existed **before 1995** are:

- **ARCHIE (PRE-WWW)** — Introduced around **1990**, it was the first search engine, indexing FTP servers to help users find and download files. It did not crawl websites as we know them today.

- **LYCOS** — Launched in **1994**, Lycos began with an index of 54,000 documents and grew rapidly to 60 million by 1996.

> Note: While Yahoo! is shown starting in 1994–1995, its initial version (launched in 1994) was a human-edited directory, not a full-text search engine like Archie or later crawlers. However, since it appeared *in* 1994, it technically qualifies as “before 1995.”

So, strictly speaking, the names of search technologies **before 1995** are:

✅ **Archie**  
✅ **Lycos**  
✅ **Yahoo!** (as a directory, launched in 1994)
