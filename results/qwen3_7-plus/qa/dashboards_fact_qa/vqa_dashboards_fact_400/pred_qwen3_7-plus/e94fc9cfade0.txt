Based on the infographic, the three basic approaches modern search engines use to organize websites are:

1.  **Crawling** – Search engines follow links to browse the vast number of pages that make up the web (noted as “60 trillion (and growing)” in the image).
2.  **Sorting** – Results get sorted by a variety of factors (the infographic notes there’s more on this later — referring to algorithms).
3.  **Indexing** – Sorted pages get stored in an index so search engines can match queries with results faster.

These steps form the foundational process all major search engines use to discover, evaluate, and retrieve web content for users.
