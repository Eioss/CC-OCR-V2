According to the infographic, once opened, canned dog food can be refrigerated for **3 days**.

The section "BEST IF USED BY: STORING DOG FOOD" shows:

- For **canned food** (represented by the gold can):
  - Once opened, store at: **<40° F**
  - Once opened, keeps for: **3 DAYS (REFRIGERATED)**

This is contrasted with dry food (the purple bag), which can be stored in a cool, dry place for up to 90 days after opening.

So, the answer is: **3 days**.
