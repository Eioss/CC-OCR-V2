### What is cerebrospinal fluid (CSF)?

Cerebrospinal fluid is a clear, colorless body fluid found in the brain and spinal cord. It acts as a cushion for the brain, provides nutrients, and removes waste. While it *is* mostly water, **it is not what the 85% figure refers to**.

The 85% statistic refers to the **total water content of the human brain tissue itself**, including neurons, glial cells, and interstitial fluid — not just CSF.

In fact, CSF makes up only about **10–15% of total brain volume**, and its composition is ~99% water — but that’s not what the infographic is citing.
