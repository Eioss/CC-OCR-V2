Based on the provided infographic from the UK Department of Health & Social Care, **the Home test (‘Finger-prick’ test taken at home)** is currently considered inaccurate.

Here’s what the image says under the “Can I get tested now?” section for the **Home test**:

> *“These tests are currently not accurate enough and the Government is not offering them at present. We plan to offer this in the future once more accurate tests are available.”*

This clearly indicates that while laboratory-based antibody tests (blood drawn by a medical professional) are being used for specific groups (NHS/social care staff, hospital inpatients, care home residents), the **home finger-prick antibody tests are not yet reliable or approved for public use** due to accuracy concerns.

---

✅ So, to directly answer your question:  
**The Home antibody test (finger-prick test) is the one described as inaccurate.**

*(Note: The Swab test is for detecting active infection — “Do I have it?” — not antibodies, so it’s not relevant to the question about antibody test accuracy.)*
