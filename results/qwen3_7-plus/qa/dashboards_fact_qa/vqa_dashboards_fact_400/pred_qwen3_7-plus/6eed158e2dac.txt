Based on the infographic provided, **dry dog food** can be stored for a longer time once opened.

Here’s the breakdown from the “BEST IF USED BY: STORING DOG FOOD” section:

- **Canned Dog Food (once opened):**
  - Store at: <40°F (refrigerated)
  - Keeps for: **3 days**

- **Dry Dog Food (once opened):**
  - Store at: <80°F (in a cool, dry place)
  - Keeps for: **90 days**

✅ So, **dry dog food lasts 90 days after opening**, while **canned dog food only lasts 3 days after opening** (if refrigerated).

Therefore, **dry dog food has a much longer shelf life once opened** compared to canned dog food.

*(Note: This assumes proper storage conditions as specified — refrigeration for canned, and cool/dry for dry.)*
