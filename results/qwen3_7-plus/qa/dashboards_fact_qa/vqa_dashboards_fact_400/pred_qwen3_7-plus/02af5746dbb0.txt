Based on the provided infographic from the UK Department of Health & Social Care, the test that indicates if a person **needs to continue to self-isolate** or **can return to work** is the:

> **Swab test — “Do I have it?”**

### Here’s why:
- The **Swab test** (which involves taking a sample from the throat and nose) is designed to detect whether you **currently have** the virus.
- Under the section **“What does it mean?”**, for the Swab test, it explicitly states:
  > *Indicates if a person:*
  > - *needs to continue to self isolate*
  > - *can return to work*
  > - *needs to be re-tested*

This makes sense because knowing if you are **currently infected** determines whether you should isolate (to avoid spreading it) or can safely return to work (if you’re not infectious).

---

In contrast:
- The **Antibody tests** (“Have I had it?”) tell you if you’ve been infected in the past, but **not** if you’re currently contagious — so they don’t directly determine isolation status or return-to-work eligibility based on current infection risk.

✅ So, the correct answer is: **Swab test**.
