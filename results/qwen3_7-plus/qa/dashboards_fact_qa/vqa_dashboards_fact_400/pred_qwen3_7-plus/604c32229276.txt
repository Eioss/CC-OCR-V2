**Summary for Dry Dog Food:**

*   **Unopened:** 12–15 months
*   **Opened:** Up to 90 days (if stored properly in a cool, dry place below 80°F)

*(Note: The canned food has different storage guidelines — unopened 18–24 months, opened refrigerated <40°F and keeps for 3 days.)*
