Looking at the map of Australia in the infographic under “AUSTRALIA’S Wine Regions”, the largest section by area is **Western Australia**, which is colored **green**.

- **Western Australia** (13 regions) → **Green**
- **South Australia** (11 regions) → **Yellow/Orange**
- **Victoria** (12 regions) → **Blue**
- **Tasmania** (7 regions) → **Purple/Lavender**
- **New South Wales** (7 regions) → **Brown/Tan**

Although Victoria has 12 regions and South Australia has 11, **Western Australia covers the largest geographic area on the map** — it’s the entire western third of the continent. The color used to fill that largest section is **green**.

✅ So, the answer is: **green**.
