Based on the infographic, under the **Trade and Investment** section, it states:

> **25,000**  
> Tourism to Ireland from India is growing steadily. Approx. 25,000 Indian visitors are expected this year, up from 18,000 in 2012.

To find how many *more* Indian tourists are expected this year compared to 2012:

**25,000 (expected this year) – 18,000 (in 2012) = 7,000**

✅ So, **7,000 more** Indian tourists are expected this year compared to 2012.
