To find the **percentage of free apps in the iPhone and Android stores taken together**, we need to combine the data from both stores.

From the infographic:

- **Apple App Store for iPhone**:
  - Paid apps: **71%**
  - Free apps: **29%**

- **Google Android Market**:
  - Paid apps: **41%**
  - Free apps: **60%**

But note — these percentages are **within each store**, not across a combined total. To compute the **overall percentage of free apps across both stores combined**, we need to know the **total number of apps** in each store, because the stores have different sizes.

From the “Top Mobile App Stores” section:

- **iPhone (Apple App Store)**: **375,566 apps**
- **Android (Google Android Market)**: **224,808 apps**

---

### Step 1: Calculate number of free apps in each store

- **iPhone free apps** = 29% of 375,566  
  → 0.29 × 375,566 ≈ **108,914**

- **Android free apps** = 60% of 224,808  
  → 0.60 × 224,808 ≈ **134,885**

---

### Step 2: Total apps across both stores

Total apps = 375,566 + 224,808 = **600,374**

Total free apps = 108,914 + 134,885 = **243,799**

---

### Step 3: Percentage of free apps overall

→ (243,799 / 600,374) × 100 ≈ **40.6%**

---

✅ **Final Answer: Approximately 40.6%**

*(Note: The infographic does not explicitly ask for this weighted average, but since it says “taken together”, this is the correct interpretation — combining the actual app counts.)*

If you were to naively average the two percentages (29% + 60%) / 2 = 44.5%, that would be incorrect because it ignores the different sizes of the stores.

---

**Answer: \boxed{40.6\%}**
