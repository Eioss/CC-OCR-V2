The **Louisiana Purchase** is regarded as one of the greatest real estate deals in U.S. history.

In 1803, President Thomas Jefferson acquired approximately **828,000 square miles** of land from France for just **$15 million** — effectively doubling the size of the United States overnight. The territory stretched from the Mississippi River to the Rocky Mountains and from the Gulf of Mexico to the Canadian border.

This acquisition included the strategically vital **Port of New Orleans**, which controlled access to the Mississippi River and was crucial for trade and economic expansion. France initially agreed to sell only New Orleans for $10 million but ultimately sold the entire Louisiana Territory for an additional $5 million.

Today, that $15 million would be worth about **$284 million**, making it an extraordinary bargain — especially since the land now forms part of **15 different U.S. states**.
