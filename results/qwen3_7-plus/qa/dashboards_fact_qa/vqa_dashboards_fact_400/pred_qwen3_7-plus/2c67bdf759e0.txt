The **Battle of Blore Heath** took place in **1459**.

It was a significant engagement during the **Wars of the Roses**, fought between the **Lancastrians** (led by Baron Audley and Baron Dudley) and the **Yorkists** (led by the Earl of Salisbury). The Yorkists, though outnumbered, used clever tactics — including feigning defeat to lure the Lancastrian cavalry into a trap — and ultimately won the battle.

Casualties were heavy on the Lancastrian side: approximately **2,000 killed**, compared to about **1,000 Yorkist casualties**.

This battle is often noted for its tactical brilliance and is one of the earliest major battles of the Wars of the Roses.
