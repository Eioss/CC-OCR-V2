To find the **percentage of paid apps on the iPhone and Android stores, taken together**, we need to combine the data from both stores.

From the infographic:

- **Apple App Store for iPhone**:  
  - Paid apps = **71%**  
  - Free apps = 29%

- **Google Android Market**:  
  - Paid apps = **41%**  
  - Free apps = 60%

But note: these percentages are **within each store individually** — they don’t represent absolute numbers of apps. To compute a *combined* percentage of paid apps across both stores, we need the **total number of apps in each store**.

From the “Top Mobile App Stores” section:

- **iPhone (Apple App Store)**: **375,566 apps**
- **Android (Google Android Market)**: **224,808 apps**

---

### Step 1: Calculate number of paid apps in each store

- **iPhone paid apps** = 71% of 375,566  
  → 0.71 × 375,566 ≈ **266,652**

- **Android paid apps** = 41% of 224,808  
  → 0.41 × 224,808 ≈ **92,171**

---

### Step 2: Total apps across both stores

Total apps = 375,566 + 224,808 = **600,374**

Total paid apps = 266,652 + 92,171 = **358,823**

---

### Step 3: Combined percentage of paid apps

→ (358,823 / 600,374) × 100 ≈ **59.77%**

---

### ✅ Final Answer:
**Approximately 59.8%** of apps across the iPhone and Android stores combined are paid apps.

*(Rounded to one decimal place as typical for such infographics.)*
