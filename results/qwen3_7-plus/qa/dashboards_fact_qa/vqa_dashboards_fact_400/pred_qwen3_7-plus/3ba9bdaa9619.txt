You can request a swab test through the **NHS website**.

The infographic states:  
> “Anyone experiencing symptoms can ask for a test through the NHS website, and priority testing is available to essential workers.”

For more details, it also provides this link:  
🔗 [https://www.gov.uk/guidance/coronavirus-covid-19-getting-tested#self-referral](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/coronavirus-covid-19-getting-tested#self-referral)

So, go to the official **NHS website** (or the GOV.UK page linked above) to self-refer for a swab test if you’re having symptoms.
