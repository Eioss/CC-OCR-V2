According to the 2012 survey by Columinate on **Internet Banking in South Africa**, **26%** of consumers used internet banking via a **cellphone or smartphone app**.

This is shown under the section **“DEVICES USED”**, where:

- **Personal laptop or computer**: 97%
- **Cellphone or smartphone app**: **26%**
- Public computer: 14%
- Tablet: 10%

> Note: The percentages for devices are not mutually exclusive — users may use multiple devices, which is why they add up to more than 100%.

---

✅ So, the answer is: **26%**
