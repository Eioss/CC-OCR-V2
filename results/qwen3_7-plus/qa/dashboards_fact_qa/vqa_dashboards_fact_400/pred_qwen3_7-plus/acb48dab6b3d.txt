The infographic mentions **one** “Biologically Appropriate Raw Food” (BARF) diet for dogs.

It was proposed in 1993 by Australian veterinarian Ian Billinghurst and includes:
- Muscle meat (on bone)
- Bones (whole or ground)
- Organ meats (liver, kidney)
- Raw eggs
- Veggies (broccoli, spinach, celery)
- Apples or other fruits
- Some dairy, such as yogurt
