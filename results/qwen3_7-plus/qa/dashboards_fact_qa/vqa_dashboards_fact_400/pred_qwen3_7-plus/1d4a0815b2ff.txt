Based on the infographic, the two specific dog food nutrient profiles defined by AAFCO standards are designed for:

1.  **Canine growth and reproduction** — This profile is intended for puppies, pregnant or nursing dogs, and growing dogs who have higher nutritional needs to support development and reproduction.

2.  **Adult dog maintenance** — This profile is formulated to meet the nutritional needs of healthy adult dogs who are not growing, pregnant, or nursing — essentially maintaining their current health and weight.
