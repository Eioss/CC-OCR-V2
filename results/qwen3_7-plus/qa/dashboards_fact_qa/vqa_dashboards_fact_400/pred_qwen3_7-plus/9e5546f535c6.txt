Based on the provided infographic from the UK Department of Health & Social Care, **antibody testing** (which answers the question “Have I had it?”) can be done in two ways:

1.  **Laboratory test**:  
    - A blood sample is taken by a medical professional (typically via a vein in the arm).  
    - This test is currently being offered to NHS and social care staff, NHS hospital inpatients, and care home residents based on their clinician’s advice.

2.  **Home test**:  
    - A ‘finger-prick’ blood sample is taken at home by the individual themselves.  
    - *However*, the infographic notes that these tests are **currently not accurate enough**, and the Government is **not offering them at present**. They plan to offer them in the future once more accurate versions are available.

---

✅ So, the two ways antibody testing can be done are:  
> **1. Laboratory test (blood draw by a professional)**  
> **2. Home test (finger-prick self-test — currently unavailable due to accuracy concerns)**
