The Irish poet whose bust will be unveiled in New Delhi is **W.B. Yeats**.

According to the infographic, W.B. Yeats and the celebrated Indian poet Rabindranath Tagore were friends. While busts of Tagore already stand in St. Stephen’s Green in Dublin and in Sligo, a bust of Yeats will be unveiled in New Delhi “this year” (relative to when the infographic was published).

This information is found under the **“Our Peoples”** section, next to the icon of Rabindranath Tagore.
