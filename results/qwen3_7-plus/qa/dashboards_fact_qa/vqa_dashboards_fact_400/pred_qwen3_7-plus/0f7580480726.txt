Based on the provided infographic from the Department of Health & Social Care, the test that can be requested for through the NHS website is the **Swab test**.

Here's the evidence from the image:

*   Under the section **"Can I get tested now?"** for the **Swab test** (the black column on the left), it states:
    > "Anyone experiencing symptoms can ask for a test through the NHS website, and priority testing is available to essential workers"

The other tests listed (Antibody tests - Laboratory and Home) have different eligibility criteria:
*   The **Laboratory antibody test** is offered to NHS/social care staff and hospital/care home residents based on clinician advice.
*   The **Home antibody test** is not currently being offered by the Government as they are not accurate enough yet.

Therefore, the only test explicitly mentioned as being requestable via the NHS website is the **Swab test**.
