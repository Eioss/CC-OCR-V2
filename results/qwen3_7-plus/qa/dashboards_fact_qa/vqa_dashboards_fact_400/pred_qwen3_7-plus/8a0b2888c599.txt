Actually, **no appliances suck up electricity when they are *not plugged in***.

The infographic you’re referring to is discussing **“Vampire Appliances”** — which are devices that **continue to draw power even when they are switched off but still plugged in**. This is also known as “phantom load” or “standby power.”

So, the correct interpretation is:

> **Appliances that suck up electricity while plugged in — even though switched off.**

The infographic lists 10 such “vampire appliances at home”:

1. Flat Screen TVs  
2. Gaming Console  
3. Computers  
4. DVRs / Cable Boxes  
5. DVD / VCR  
6. Space Heater  
7. Phones (chargers/base stations)  
8. Printer  
9. Router / Modem  
10. Kitchen Appliances (e.g., microwaves, coffee makers with clocks)

✅ So, the answer to your question — if interpreted literally — is:  
**Zero appliances suck up electricity when NOT plugged in.**

But if you meant:  
**“How many vampire appliances are listed that suck power when plugged in but turned off?”**  
→ Then the answer is: **10**
