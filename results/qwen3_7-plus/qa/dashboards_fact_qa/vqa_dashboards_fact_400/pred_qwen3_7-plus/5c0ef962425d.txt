Based on the infographic, the three inventions introduced at the **1904 St. Louis World’s Fair** were:

1.  **The Ice Cream Cone** – Debuted as the first edible, walk-away ice cream container, made from a waffle rolled into a conical shape.
2.  **Electricity (for the masses)** – Celebrated with “The Palace of Electricity,” where Thomas Edison oversaw its public presentation.
3.  **The Hot Dog** – Born when a sausage seller, Anton Feuchtwanger, asked his brother-in-law to create a long bun to hold sausages so customers wouldn’t need gloves.
