✅ **Answer: Carthage had more infantry during the Battle of Zama — 50,000 vs. Rome’s 34,000.**

*(Note: While Carthage had more infantry and also 80 war elephants, Rome’s superior cavalry tactics — especially the Numidian cavalry flanking maneuver — ultimately led to their victory.)*
