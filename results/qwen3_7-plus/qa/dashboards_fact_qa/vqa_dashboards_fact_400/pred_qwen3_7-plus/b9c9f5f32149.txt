Based on the provided infographic from the UK Department of Health & Social Care, the two main types of Coronavirus (COVID-19) tests are:

1.  **Swab test** – This test answers the question: **“Do I have it?”**
    *   It involves taking a swab sample from the throat and nose.
    *   It indicates if a person currently has the virus and needs to self-isolate, can return to work, or needs re-testing.

2.  **Antibody test** – This test answers the question: **“Have I had it?”**
    *   It detects antibodies in the blood, indicating past infection.
    *   It is further divided into:
        *   **Laboratory test**: Blood taken by a medical professional (currently offered to NHS/social care staff and hospital/care home residents based on clinician advice).
        *   **Home test**: ‘Finger-prick’ test taken at home (not currently available as tests are not accurate enough; planned for future release).

In summary, the two primary categories are **Swab tests** (for current infection) and **Antibody tests** (for past infection).
