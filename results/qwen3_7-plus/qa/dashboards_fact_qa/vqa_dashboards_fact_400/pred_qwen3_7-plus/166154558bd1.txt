The coffee beverage reportedly developed in the 1980s in Sydney cafes is the **Flat White**.

It’s made by pouring microfoam over a double shot of espresso and is typically served in a 170–220 ml mug — smaller than a latte, making it milkier. While some claim it was invented in New Zealand, Australia (particularly Sydney) is widely credited with its development and popularization.

☕ *Fun fact: It’s now a global café staple!*
