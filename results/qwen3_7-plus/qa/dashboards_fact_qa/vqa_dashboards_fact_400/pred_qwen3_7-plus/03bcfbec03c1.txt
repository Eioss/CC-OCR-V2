The **Laboratory test** is the antibody test that involves blood testing by a medical professional.

As shown in the infographic:
- Under “Antibody test → Have I had it?”, there are two subtypes: **Laboratory test** and **Home test**.
- The **Laboratory test** column states:  
  > *“Blood test taken by a medical professional”*  
  and includes an illustration of a healthcare worker drawing blood from a patient’s arm.

In contrast, the **Home test** uses a ‘finger-prick’ sample taken at home by the individual themselves.

✅ So, the correct answer is: **Laboratory test**.
