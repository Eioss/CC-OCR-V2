Based on the infographic, the ideal dog food should be **free from artificial flavors, colors, hormones and preservatives**.

This is listed as one of the five key characteristics of the ideal food for today’s dog, under the section “Since dog physiology hasn’t changed much in 100s of years, the ideal food for today’s dog should be:”.

The icon associated with this point is a red octagon with a syringe crossed out, visually reinforcing the idea of avoiding artificial or synthetic additives.

---

**Summary of Ideal Dog Food Characteristics (from the infographic):**

1.  **Free from** artificial flavors, colors, hormones and preservatives
2.  **High in meat-based protein** from a known animal source
3.  **Low in carbs**
4.  **High in natural fats & oils**
5.  **Complete in vitamins & minerals**

These guidelines are presented as aligning with a dog’s natural ancestral diet (56% protein, 30% fats, 14% carbs) and their unchanged physiology.
