Based on the infographic, the **long-term benefits** of early childhood education are divided into two main categories:

---

### 1. **Student Success**
- **Higher graduation rates**: Students with early childhood education are **29% more likely to graduate from high school**.
- **Lower grade repetition**: They are **44% less likely to repeat a grade**.
- **Better test performance**: They **pass standardized tests more frequently**.

---

### 2. **Adult Responsibility**
- **Higher employment likelihood**: Adults age 40 who had early childhood education are **more likely to be employed**.
- **Higher income**: These same adults have a **33% higher average income**.
- **Lower crime rates**: 18-year-olds **without** early childhood education are **70% more likely to be arrested for a violent crime**.

---

In summary, early childhood education leads to better academic outcomes in school and greater economic stability and social responsibility in adulthood — including higher employment, higher income, and lower involvement in violent crime.

*(Source: Infographic provided by Campbell Christian Schools, citing data from Early Childhood Alliance, KUEDucation, and HighScope.)*
