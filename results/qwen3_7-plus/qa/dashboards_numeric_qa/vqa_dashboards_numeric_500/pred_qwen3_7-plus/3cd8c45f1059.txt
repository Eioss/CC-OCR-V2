Based on the infographic, shoppers from **Generation Z** and **Millennials** are trying out new online retailers.

Here’s the breakdown from the section “Shoppers are trying out new online retailers”:

- **Overall**, 25% of shoppers tried new online retailers due to lockdown and intend to continue.
- This rises to:
  - **32% for Gen Z**
  - **35% for Millennials**

In contrast:
- **43% of Baby Boomers** said they did *not* try new online retailers.
- **48% of the Silent Generation** also said they did *not*.

So, while some individuals across all generations may have tried new retailers, the data clearly shows that **Gen Z and Millennials** are the generations most actively experimenting with and adopting new online retailers during the pandemic — and planning to stick with them.
