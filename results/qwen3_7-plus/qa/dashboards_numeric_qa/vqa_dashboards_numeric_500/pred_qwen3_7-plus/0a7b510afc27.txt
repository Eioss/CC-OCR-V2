According to the 2013 survey presented in the infographic, **23%** of online students considered hybrid programs.

This is shown in the bar chart titled **“Consideration of Hybrid/Classroom Programs”** under the section “DEDICATED TO ONLINE LEARNING”. The blue bar labeled “Considered hybrid programs” clearly indicates 23%.

---

**Breakdown from the chart:**
- **44%** — Only considered fully online programs
- **23%** — Considered hybrid programs ✅
