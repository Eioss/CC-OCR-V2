Based on the infographic, under the section **“Shoppers are trying out new online retailers:”**, we see the following data:

- **Gen Z**: 32% said **YES** (they tried new online retailers and intend to continue)
- **Millennials**: 35% said **YES**

---

### Difference in percentage:
**35% (Millennials) - 32% (Gen Z) = 3%**

---

✅ So, the difference in percentage of shoppers who are trying out new online retailers between Gen Z and Millennials is **3 percentage points**, with Millennials slightly higher.

*(Note: The “NO” percentages — 43% for Baby Boomers and 48% for Silent Gen — are not relevant to this specific comparison.)*
