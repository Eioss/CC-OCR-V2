According to the **Hotels.com Romance Survey (United States 2012)**, for respondents aged **18–29**, the most ideal Valentine’s Day scenarios are:

1. **Exploring a new city** — **33%**  
   *(Highest preference in this age group)*

2. **Snuggling by the fire** — **33%**  
   *(Tied for first place with exploring a new city)*

3. **Spending the night in a hotel suite** — **22%**

4. **Dinner at a romantic restaurant** — **11%**  
   *(Lowest preference among the four options for this age group)*

---

### Key Insight:
Younger respondents (18–29) favor **adventure and intimacy** — either exploring somewhere new or cozying up by a fire — over traditional dinners or luxury hotel stays. This suggests they value **experiences and emotional connection** more than formal romance tropes.

> 💡 *Note: The survey allowed multiple choices per respondent, so percentages don’t sum to 100%.*
