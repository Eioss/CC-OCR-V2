The Dubai Sevens tournament was founded in **1970**.

> **1970**  
> THE YEAR THE TOURNAMENT STARTED IN DUBAI

Additionally, the infographic notes that Emirates Airline first sponsored the competition in **1987**, and that New Zealand teams have been champions 7 times since 1996 (including the invitation side of 1997), which further contextualizes the tournament’s long history starting from 1970.

So, the founding year of the Dubai Rugby 7s is **1970**.
