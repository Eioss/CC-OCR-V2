Based on the infographic provided, **providing a disposable surgical mask** is part of:

> **1. ISOLATE**

Under this step, the text says:
> *“Isolate the person from others and provide a disposable surgical mask, if available, for the person to wear.”*

This applies specifically when **the suspected or confirmed case of COVID-19 is at work**.

---
