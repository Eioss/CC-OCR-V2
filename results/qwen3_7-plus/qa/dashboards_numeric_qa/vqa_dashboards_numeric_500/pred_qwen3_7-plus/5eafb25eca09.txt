According to the 2012 Hotels.com Romance Survey for respondents aged **40–49**, the most ideal Valentine’s Day scenario is:

> **Spending the night in a hotel suite** — chosen by **45%** of respondents in that age group.

This is higher than any other option for that demographic:
- Dinner at a romantic restaurant: 16%
- Exploring a new city: 11%
- Snuggling by the fire: 25%
- Spending the night in a hotel suite: **45%** ✅

So, for the 40–49 age group, **a hotel suite overnight stay** is the clear favorite romantic getaway scenario.

---

*Source: Hotels.com Romance Survey, United States 2012*
