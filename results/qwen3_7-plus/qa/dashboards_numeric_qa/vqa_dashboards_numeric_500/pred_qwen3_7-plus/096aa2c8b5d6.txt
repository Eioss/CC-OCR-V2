The English Football Association (The FA) was founded in **1863**.

This is clearly indicated at the bottom left of the infographic, which states:

> **THE FA**  
> THE ENGLISH FOOTBALL ASSOCIATION CELEBRATES ITS 150TH ANNIVERSARY THIS YEAR  
> **1863 — 150 YEARS — 2013**

Since the infographic was created for the 150th anniversary in 2013, subtracting 150 years from 2013 gives us **1863** as the founding year.

✅ So, the answer is: **1863**.
