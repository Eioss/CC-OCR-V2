Based on the infographic, under the section **“Consumers are experimenting, purchasing items they would not usually purchase online”**, we see:

- **Overall**: 39% of respondents said lockdown encouraged them to try and buy goods online they hadn’t considered before.
- **Millennials**: 52% said the same.

To find the **increase in percentage from overall consumers to millennials**:

> **52% (Millennials) – 39% (Overall) = 13%**

---

✅ **Answer: 13%**

This means Millennials are **13 percentage points more likely** than the overall average to have tried new things while shopping online during lockdown.
