Based on the infographic “Halloween Freaky-Nomics,” here are the key characteristics and facts about Halloween pumpkins:

### 1. **Massive Production Volume**
- **900 million pounds** of pumpkins are produced in the U.S. specifically for Halloween.
- This is enough to make approximately **60 million pumpkin pies**.

### 2. **Seasonal Availability**
- **80% of the total pumpkin supply in the U.S.** is available during **October**, aligning with the Halloween season.

### 3. **Physical Description (Implied by Title & Imagery)**
- The section titled **“Big, Round, and Orange All Over”** suggests that the classic Halloween pumpkin is:
  - **Large in size** (“Big”)
  - **Spherical or rounded shape** (“Round”)
  - **Bright orange color** (“Orange All Over”) — though the image also shows a grayish-brown pumpkin at the top, indicating variety, the traditional carving pumpkin is orange.

### 4. **Cultural Role**
- Pumpkins are central to Halloween traditions:
  - **48% of people carve pumpkins** as part of their celebration (per “How People Celebrate” section).
  - They’re also used as **costume inspiration** — more furry monsters dress up as pumpkins than any other costume type (per “Cuddly Critter Costumes”).

### 5. **Economic Impact**
- While not directly stated as a dollar value for pumpkins alone, they contribute significantly to the overall **$8 billion Halloween spending** (2012 estimate), especially through decorations, costumes, and food (like pumpkin pie).

✅ In summary:  
**Halloween pumpkins are big, round, orange gourds produced in massive quantities (900M lbs) in the U.S., mostly available in October, used primarily for carving (by nearly half of celebrants), and culturally iconic — even inspiring costumes and contributing to billions in seasonal spending.**

*(Note: The infographic doesn’t specify botanical traits like species — but commercially, these are typically *Cucurbita pepo* varieties bred for carving, not eating.)*
