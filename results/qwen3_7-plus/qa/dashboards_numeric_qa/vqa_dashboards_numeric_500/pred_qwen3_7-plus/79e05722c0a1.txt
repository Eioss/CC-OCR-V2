According to the 2011 survey shown in the infographic, **82%** of Australians aged **60–64 years** use the internet.

This is displayed in the curved bar chart on the right side of the image, labeled “Internet usage in over 50s,” where each age group has a corresponding percentage:

- 50–55 year olds: 85%
- 55–59 year olds: 90%
- **60–64 year olds: 82%**
- 65–69 year olds: 76%
- 70–74 year olds: 65%
- 75+ year olds: 41%

The source note confirms this data comes from Apia’s research based on an independent telephone survey conducted by Newspoll of 2060 Australians aged 50 and over across all states and territories in 2011.

✅ So, the answer is **82%**.
