Based on the infographic, the two types of entrepreneurs are:

1.  **Opportunity-Driven**
    *   These are women who start businesses in markets with **strong enabling conditions** (like access to finance, education, good governance, etc.).
    *   Examples of countries that tend to foster more opportunity-driven entrepreneurs: **New Zealand, Canada, United Kingdom, UAE, and Portugal**.

2.  **Necessity-Driven**
    *   These are women who start businesses out of necessity, often because they have **fewer supporting conditions** or limited alternatives for income.
    *   Examples of countries with more necessity-driven entrepreneurs: **Ghana, Brazil, Bangladesh, Vietnam, and Mexico**.

The infographic visually distinguishes them with icons: a smiling woman in a suit for "Opportunity-Driven" and a woman looking concerned while working at a counter for "Necessity-Driven".
