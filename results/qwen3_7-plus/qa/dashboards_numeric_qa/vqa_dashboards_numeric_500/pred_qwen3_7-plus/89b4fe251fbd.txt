The first tweet was made by **Jack Dorsey**, co-founder of Twitter.

His tweet, posted on March 21, 2006, read:  
> “just setting up my twttr”

This is confirmed in the infographic under the section titled **“THE FIRST TWEET”**, which states:
> “WAS BY CO-FOUNDER JACK DORSEY”  
> “03.21.06”

So, the name of the person who made the first tweet is **Jack Dorsey**.
