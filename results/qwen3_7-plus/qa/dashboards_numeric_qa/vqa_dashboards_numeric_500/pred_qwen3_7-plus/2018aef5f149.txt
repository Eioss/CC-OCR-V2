Based on the provided **Community Sport and Active Recreation COVID-19 Factsheet** from the Department of Local Government, Sport and Cultural Industries (Western Australia), the required area for social distancing is:

> **4 square metres per person**

This is explicitly stated in two sections:

1.  **For outdoor activity (including bootcamps):**
    > “...with social distancing (ensuring **4 square metres of space per person** and maintaining a 1.5 metre distance from others).”

2.  **For indoor yoga or Pilates classes within a private residence:**
    > “...permitted if the **four square metres per person rule** is adhered to...”

Additionally, while not an *area*, the factsheet also specifies a **minimum physical distance** of **1.5 metres** between persons for outdoor activities.

---

✅ **Final Answer: 4 square metres per person** (and additionally, maintain 1.5 metres distance where specified).
