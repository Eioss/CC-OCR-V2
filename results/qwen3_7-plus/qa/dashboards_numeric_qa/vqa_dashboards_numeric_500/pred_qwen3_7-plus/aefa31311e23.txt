Based on the infographic, the majority of millennials in the U.S. choose online viewing for the following reasons:

1.  **Immediate Access (56%)**: This is the top reason cited. Millennials value the ability to watch content whenever they want, without being tied to a broadcast schedule.
2.  **Multitasking (49%)**: Online viewing allows them to easily switch between apps or devices, enabling them to do other things (like browsing social media, texting, or working) while watching video content.
3.  **Convenience of Viewing Location (44%)**: They can watch content anywhere – on their phone during a commute, on a tablet in bed, or on a laptop at a coffee shop – not just in front of a traditional TV set.

These factors highlight that millennials prioritize **control, flexibility, and integration** with their digital lifestyles over passive, scheduled television consumption. The data shows these three reasons are all selected by nearly half or more of the millennial population, making them the primary drivers for choosing online viewing platforms like YouTube, Netflix, and Hulu.
