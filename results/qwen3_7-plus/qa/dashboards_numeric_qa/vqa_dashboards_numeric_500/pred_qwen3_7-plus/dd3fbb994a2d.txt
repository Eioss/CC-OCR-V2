Based on the comparison table **“VMWARE VSPHERE VS. MICROSOFT HYPER-V”** at the bottom of the infographic, under the row:

> **Max guests per host**

The values for VMware editions are:

- **VMware STANDARD**: 512
- **VMware ENTERPRISE**: 512
- **VMware ENT. PLUS**: 512

✅ So, the **highest number of guest machines (virtual machines) that can be connected per host** in any VMware edition listed is **512**.

This applies to all three VMware editions shown: Standard, Enterprise, and Enterprise Plus.

---

**Note**: The Microsoft Hyper-V editions show lower limits (384 for Standard/Enterprise, no limit for Datacenter — but “No limit” likely refers to licensing, not technical capacity; technically, Hyper-V also has practical limits based on hardware).

But since your question specifically asks about **VMware**, the answer is:

> **512 guests per host**

*(across VMware Standard, Enterprise, and Enterprise Plus editions)*
