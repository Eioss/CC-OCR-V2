According to the infographic:

> **A 60-WATT BULB can use up to 5 GALLONS OF WATER for every hour it’s left on.**

So, if a 60-watt bulb is left on for **1 hour**, it uses **up to 5 gallons** of water.

This refers to “hidden water” — the water used in electricity generation (likely thermoelectric power, which accounts for 49% of U.S. water use) to produce the energy that powers the bulb.
