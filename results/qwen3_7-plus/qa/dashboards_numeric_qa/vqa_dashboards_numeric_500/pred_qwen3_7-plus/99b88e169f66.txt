Based on the infographic, **Generation Z (Gen Z)** is the age group most concerned about reducing spending on luxury items.

Here’s the breakdown from the section “Consumers are more concerned with essential everyday items”:

- **27% of Gen Z** stated that their spend on luxury products had reduced.
- **23% of Millennials** said the same.
- **18% of Gen X** also reported a decrease in luxury spending.

While the overall figure for all respondents is 19%, **Gen Z leads at 27%**, indicating they are the most likely to have cut back on luxury purchases during the pandemic.

Additionally, this aligns with other findings in the report — such as Gen Z being more experimental in online shopping (61%) and having higher expectations for fast delivery (15% expect goods within 24 hours) — suggesting they are highly responsive to changing circumstances and prioritizing essentials or value-driven purchases.
