According to the 2011 survey data presented in the infographic, **71%** of Australians aged over 50 do **online shopping**.

This statistic is shown in the top-right section under the heading “What over 50s do on the internet,” alongside other activities like online banking (71%), Skype use (40%), and social media use (35%).

The source note at the bottom confirms the data comes from Apia’s research based on an independent telephone survey conducted by Newspoll of 2060 Australians aged 50 years and over across all states and territories in 2011.
