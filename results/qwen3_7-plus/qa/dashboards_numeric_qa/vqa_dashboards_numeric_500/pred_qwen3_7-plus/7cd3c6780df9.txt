Based on the infographic, **Generation Z is more likely to try new things while shopping online** than Millennials.

> **“Consumers are experimenting, purchasing items they would not usually purchase online”**

- **Overall**: 39% of respondents said lockdown encouraged them to try and buy goods online they hadn’t considered before.

This clearly shows that **Gen Z (61%)** is more experimental in trying new products online compared to **Millennials (52%)**.
