Based on the infographic titled **“COVID-19: Canada’s Economic Response Plan”**, the emergency benefits provided to **Working Canadians** are:

---

### 1. **Emergency Care Benefit**
- **Purpose**: For individuals who must stay home due to COVID-19 and don’t have paid sick leave.
- **Eligible individuals may include**:
  - Those who can’t access Employment Insurance (EI)
  - Those who are sick
  - Those taking care of a child or someone who is sick
- **Benefit amount**: Up to **$900 bi-weekly** for up to **15 weeks**

---

### 2. **Emergency Support Benefit**
- **Purpose**: To support workers who are **not eligible for EI** and are facing unemployment or reduced hours.
- **Total funding**: Up to **$5 billion** in support

---

Additionally, under the broader “Working Canadians” category, the plan includes:

- **$27 Billion** — Direct support for Canadian Workers and Businesses
- **$55 Billion** — Support in business liquidity through tax deferrals

---

✅ So, the direct *emergency benefits* for working Canadians are the **Emergency Care Benefit** ($900 bi-weekly for up to 15 weeks) and the **Emergency Support Benefit** (up to $5 billion total for ineligible workers).

The other figures ($27B and $55B) represent broader economic supports, not individual cash benefits.

---

**Summary Answer:**

> Working Canadians are eligible for two key emergency benefits:
> - **Emergency Care Benefit**: Up to $900 every two weeks for up to 15 weeks, for those unable to work due to illness, caregiving, or lack of paid sick leave.
> - **Emergency Support Benefit**: A $5 billion fund for workers not eligible for EI who face job loss or reduced hours.

These are part of Canada’s larger $27 billion direct support package for workers and businesses, plus $55 billion in business liquidity via tax deferrals.
