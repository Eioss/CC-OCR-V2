According to the **Foodbank Hunger Report 2017**, **33%** of food insecure Australians feel **ashamed** when seeking assistance from a charity.

This is shown in the section titled **“How Many People Are Receiving Food Assistance?”**, under the subheading *“This is because they feel:”*, where three emotional barriers are listed with corresponding percentages:

- **42%** — Others need help more than they do  
- **36%** — Embarrassed  
- **33%** — **Ashamed**

So, the percentage of food insecure Australians who are **ashamed** of seeking charitable food assistance is **33%**.
