According to the **Foodbank Hunger Report 2017**, **36%** of food insecure Australians feel **embarrassed** about seeking assistance from a charity.

This is shown in the section titled *“How Many People Are Receiving Food Assistance?”*, under the subheading *“This is because they feel:”*, where three emotions are listed with corresponding percentages:

- **42%** — Others need help more than they do  
- **36%** — Embarrassed 😳  
- **33%** — Ashamed 😔

So, the answer is **36%**.
