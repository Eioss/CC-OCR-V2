Based on the infographic, under the section **“WHO PROVIDED CHILDCARE IN 2011?”**, it states:

> **40% of female indigenous young people** provided childcare.

To find the percentage who **did not** provide childcare, subtract this from 100%:

**100% - 40% = 60%**
