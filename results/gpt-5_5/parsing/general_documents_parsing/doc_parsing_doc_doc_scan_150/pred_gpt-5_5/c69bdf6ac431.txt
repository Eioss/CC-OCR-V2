\[
\text{III. CALCULATIONS:}
\]

Calculating the volume of the given examined bodies based on the formula:

\[
V_b = a \cdot b \cdot c
\]

\[
V_b \text{ - volume of examined bodies } [\mathrm{cm}^3],\quad
a \text{ - width of examined bodies } [\mathrm{cm}],\quad
b \text{ - length of}
\]
\[
\text{examined bodies } [\mathrm{cm}],\quad
c \text{ - height of examined bodies } [\mathrm{cm}].
\]

\[
V_b = 2{,}5\ [\mathrm{cm}] \cdot 4{,}2\ [\mathrm{cm}] \cdot 1{,}5\ [\mathrm{cm}]
= 15{,}75\ [\mathrm{cm}^3]
\]

Calculating the material density of the given examined bodies based on the formula:

\[
\rho_b = \frac{m_b}{V_b}
\]

\[
\rho_b \text{ - material density of given body } [\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3],\quad
m_b \text{ - mass of given body } [\mathrm{g}],\quad
V_b \text{ - volume of}
\]
\[
\text{examined body.}
\]

\[
\rho_b\ (\text{ebonite}) =
\frac{22{,}7}{15{,}75}\ [\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 1{,}44127\ [\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 1441{,}27\ [\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m}^3]
\]

\[
\rho_b\ (\text{bakelite}) =
\frac{19{,}8}{15{,}75}\ [\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 1{,}25714\ [\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]

\]

\[

\frac{42{,}6}{15{,}75}\ [\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]
= 2{,}70476\ [\mathrm{g}/\mathrm{cm}^3]

Calculating the material density using areometers based on the formula:

\[

\[

\[
