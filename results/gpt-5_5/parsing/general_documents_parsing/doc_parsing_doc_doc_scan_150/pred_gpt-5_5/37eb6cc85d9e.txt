\textbf{\underline{Laws of Probability}}

If A and B are mutually exclusive, then:
\[
P(A\ \mathrm{or}\ B)=P(A)+P(B)
\]

If A and B are independent, then:
\[
P(A\ \mathrm{and}\ B)=P(A)\times P(B)
\]

\textbf{\underline{Hardy-Weinberg Equations}}

\[
p^2+2pq+q^2=1
\qquad
\begin{array}{l}
p=\text{frequency of the dominant allele}\\
\text{in a population}
\end{array}
\]

\[
p+q=1
\qquad
\begin{array}{l}
q=\text{frequency of the recessive allele}\\
\text{in a population}
\end{array}
\]
