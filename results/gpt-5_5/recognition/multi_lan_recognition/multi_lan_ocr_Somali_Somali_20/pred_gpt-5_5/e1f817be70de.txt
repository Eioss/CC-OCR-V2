Qorqodaha camuugada afshama, calafba hay siinnin.

VII

Cosoobida midkaa aagan een, ceeb ka komhaynin.
Ceelkuu istaagaba mikli, gabadh cayansiya
Caloolyoma kugu beera iyo, Cillo kugu raagta
Cadardi xishoodiyo marwaba, daba carabaaya
Caskra iyo dhurki ilma, cago budhlynaya
Kii dumarka cill bada Rabboow, calafba hay siinnin.

VIII

Mid caqligu naxasoobyoo, cudur ka guuxayo
Cuyub iyo magan liito oon, xoogga kugu caawin
Canadnimo mid ay hayso oo, habaray cirdridnsa
Ciinsilir mid ay dhanto meel, ciyow ka yeedhayo
Cumug-cumug-midkii lagu-silliday-caynin garan waayo
‘Cakudaan’ inna kugu hayo, canoyo jiidhkaaga
Cawad kii la yuurura Rabboow, calafba hay-siinnin.
