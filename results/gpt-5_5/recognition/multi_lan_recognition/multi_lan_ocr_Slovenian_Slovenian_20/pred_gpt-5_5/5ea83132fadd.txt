Ke o boleletse.

Gore nna ke nna kgosi

Ga ke tlhakakerepe mothwanyana (tes.120).

Ga a ipolele a fedisa boik. Le Jona tota ga a a bo fedisa. Bo fediditswe ke Kereke e Galalelang. Ke Kereke; Kerekepete o Kepolotse Nthile, mme Gabankitse o boile Merementi le Lerithile. Ke phoso gore Ntšime a mo sireletse, e tswe a diriditse jaaka le polao go bologa Kelifepete. Makoko a gagwe ga a na bopaki. Ga go na bosupi jwa gore pelo e a e tshwanetseng ya ga Neo le gore e goutsewe ke Kelifepete.

Thanolo eno ya Moanelwa Letlapa e supa gore ga se a tshwanela go ikanya makoko a moteraana a mo kivang ka ona. A ka nna le maitlhomo a a rileng ka moanelwa jaaka maano a go rara-bolola kgothang kgotsa go ntsha molatlha.
