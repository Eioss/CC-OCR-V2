A **Data & Analytics Manager** is responsible for **managing a team of data analysts and data scientists**.

In a company, they typically:

- Lead analytics and data science projects  
- Coordinate the work of analysts, engineers, and stakeholders  
- Help turn business questions into data-driven solutions  
- Oversee data analysis, reporting, predictive modeling, and insights delivery  
- Ensure the team’s work supports business goals and decision-making  

In short, they act as the **team leader for data and analytics**, connecting technical data work with business strategy.
