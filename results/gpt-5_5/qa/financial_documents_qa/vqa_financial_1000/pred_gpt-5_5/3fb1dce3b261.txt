The document is an **Audit Agenda** for a **Production & Process Controls audit** at **DePuy Orthopaedics, Inc. in Warsaw, Indiana**, dated **13–17 July 2009**.

Its purpose is to assess compliance with **21 CFR 820** and **ISO 13485:2003** for selected parts of the company’s **Quality Management System**. The audit scope focuses on whether the **Production and Process Controls subsystem** has been effectively implemented, including review of prior internal-audit corrective actions where appropriate. It notes that the audit is **not a full assessment** of the entire quality system unless related issues are identified during the review.
