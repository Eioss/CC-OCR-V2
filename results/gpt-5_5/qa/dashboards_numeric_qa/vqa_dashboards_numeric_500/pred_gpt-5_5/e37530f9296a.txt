The fatality rate is calculated as deaths per 100,000 full-time-equivalent workers.

BLS formula:

\[
\text{Fatality rate}=\frac{\text{Number of fatal work injuries}}{\text{Total hours worked by all workers}} \times 200{,}000{,}000
\]

Where:

- **Number of fatal work injuries** = work-related deaths in that occupation/industry during the year  
- **Total hours worked** = total annual hours worked by workers in that group  
- **200,000,000** = the hours worked by **100,000 full-time workers** working 40 hours/week for 50 weeks/year

So a fatality rate of **135.9** means about **135.9 deaths per 100,000 full-time-equivalent workers per year**.
