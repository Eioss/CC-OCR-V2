The emergency benefits for working Canadians shown are:

- **Emergency Care Benefit**: Up to **$900 bi-weekly for up to 15 weeks** for people who must stay home due to COVID-19 and do not have paid sick leave, including those who are sick, caring for someone sick, or caring for a child.

- **Emergency Support Benefit**: Up to **$5 billion** in support for workers who are **not eligible for EI** and are facing unemployment or reduced hours.
