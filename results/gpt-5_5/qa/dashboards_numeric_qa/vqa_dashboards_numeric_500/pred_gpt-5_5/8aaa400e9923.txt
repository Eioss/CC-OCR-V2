The developmental benefits of early childhood education include:

- **Social and emotional development**
  - Children develop relationships with adults and peers.
  - They gain a sense of security.
  - This can lead to greater participation and cooperation.

- **Cognitive development**
  - Age-appropriate learning supports development.
  - Children build on existing skills and increase their knowledge.
  - Teachers help students reach important developmental milestones.

- **Language development**
  - Language develops quickly between ages 1 and 5.
  - Regular interactions with teachers and peers help build language skills.
  - More advanced skills emerge as vocabulary and conversation abilities grow.
