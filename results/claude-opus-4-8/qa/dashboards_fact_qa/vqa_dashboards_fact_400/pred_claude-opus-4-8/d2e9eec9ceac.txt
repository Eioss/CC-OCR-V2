Based on the infographic, it states that **1/3 of all mobile phone users will access Instagram at least once a month in 2016.**

To find the percentage who are **not** using Instagram about once a month:

- 1/3 access Instagram = approximately 33.3%
- Therefore, those **not** accessing it = 2/3 = approximately **66.7%**

So about **66.7% (two-thirds)** of all mobile phone users are not accessing Instagram about once a month in 2016.
