Based on the infographic, **Education First** is the United Nations Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon's initiative, which has **three main priorities/initiatives**:

## 1. Put Every Child in School
- Get every child enrolled in education
- Address that 61 million primary-aged children are out of school
- Eliminate cost barriers and close the gender gap
- Build more classrooms in rural and conflict-affected areas

## 2. Improve the Quality of Learning
- Ensure children gain basic literacy and numeracy skills
- Address that 250 million children cannot read, write, or count well
- Provide more well-trained teachers and improved learning materials
- Increase early-childhood education programs

## 3. Foster Global Citizenship
- Promote peace, tolerance, and respect for diversity
- Cultivate a sense of community and environmental stewardship
- Train teachers in innovative teaching methods

---

The infographic also highlights a supporting goal to **Close the Financial Gap** — increasing investments in education to maintain progress and meet the goals.

As Ban Ki-moon states: *"When we put Education First, we can reduce poverty and hunger, end wasted potential—and look forward to stronger and better societies for all."*
