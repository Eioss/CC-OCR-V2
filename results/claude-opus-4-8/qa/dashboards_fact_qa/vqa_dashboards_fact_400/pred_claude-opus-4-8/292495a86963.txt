Based on the infographic, during **Operation Phantom Fury (2004)**, the **Coalition** had significantly more strength:

- **Coalition:** 10,500 U.S. / 2,000 Iraq / 850 U.K. = **13,350 total men**
  - Commanders: U.S. Natonski/Mattis, U.K. Cowan

- **Insurgents:** **4,000 men**
  - Commanders: Al-Janabi/Hadid

So the **Coalition** had a major numerical advantage, outnumbering the insurgents by more than 3 to 1.
