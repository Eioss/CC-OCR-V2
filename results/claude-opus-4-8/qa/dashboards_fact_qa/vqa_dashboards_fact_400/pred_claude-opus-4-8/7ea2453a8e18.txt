Based on the infographic, **Damper** is the bread that is cooked over a damped fire's ashes.

According to the "Damping" section: "A fire is damped to allow the bread to be cooked in the fire's ashes – hence the name."

Damper is described as a Traditional Australian soda bread, based on Irish Soda Bread, which dates back to the early white settlement of Australia. It was traditionally prepared by swagmen, drovers, and stockmen, with its two traditional ingredients being flour and water.
