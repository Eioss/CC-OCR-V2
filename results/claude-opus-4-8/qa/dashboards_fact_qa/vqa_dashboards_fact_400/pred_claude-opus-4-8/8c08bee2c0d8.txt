Based on the infographic, **57%** of Grade 5 children in South Africa were able to read at the basic reading level.

This is calculated from the statistic stating that **43%** of Grade 5 children were *unable* to read at even the basic reading level (according to the Progress in International Reading Literacy Study/PIRLS, 2011). Therefore, 100% − 43% = **57%** were able to read at the basic level.
