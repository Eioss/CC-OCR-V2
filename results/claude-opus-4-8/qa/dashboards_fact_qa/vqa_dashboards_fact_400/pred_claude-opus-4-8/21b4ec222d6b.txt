According to the infographic, **NOT-SO-CLOSE READERS** are the ones who "Let their thinking wander."

This appears in the section **"WHAT CLOSE READERS DO DIFFERENTLY,"** which contrasts the habits of close readers versus not-so-close readers:

| Close Readers... | Not-So-Close Readers... |
|---|---|
| Reread | Read the text once |
| **Focus on the text** | **Let their thinking wander** |
| Ask questions | Take the text at face value |
| Pay attention to language | Ignore syntax clues |
| Uncover deeper meaning | Understand only at surface level |

So while close readers **focus on the text**, not-so-close readers let their thinking wander.
