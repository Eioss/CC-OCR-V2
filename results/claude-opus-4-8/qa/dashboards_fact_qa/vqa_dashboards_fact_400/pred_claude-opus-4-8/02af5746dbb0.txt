According to the infographic, the **Swab test** ("Do I have it?") indicates if a person needs to be isolated or can return to work.

Under the "What does it mean?" section, the swab test indicates if a person:
- needs to continue to self isolate
- can return to work
- needs to be re-tested

The swab test uses a sample taken from the throat and nose to determine whether someone **currently has** the virus.
