Looking at this infographic, I need to clarify an important distinction:

**Pedestrian accidents are listed as 2.9%** of the leading causes of death for 15-24 year olds.

However, it's important to note that pedestrian accidents do **not cause suicides**. These are separate, distinct categories in the chart:

- Suicide: 23.3%
- Car accidents: 14%
- Drugs and poisoning: 4.2%
- Motorcycle accidents: 4.2%
- Pedestrian accidents: 2.9%
- Cancer: 2.4%

These percentages represent different **leading causes of death** among young people—they are independent categories, not subcategories of suicide. So pedestrian accidents account for 2.9% of deaths in this age group, but they are unrelated to the suicide statistics.
