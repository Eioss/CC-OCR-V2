Based on the infographic, **infrared** is more durable than resistive.

According to the durability comparison:

- **Resistive**: "Fair Durability. Screens are vulnerable to scratches and other minor damage. The screen will wear over time and need replacing."

- **Infrared**: "Good Durability. Glass shatters upon major impact only."

So infrared has **good durability** (only vulnerable to major impacts), while resistive has only **fair durability** (vulnerable to scratches and general wear over time). The glass screen of infrared technology makes it more resistant to everyday damage compared to the flexible polyester film used in resistive screens.
