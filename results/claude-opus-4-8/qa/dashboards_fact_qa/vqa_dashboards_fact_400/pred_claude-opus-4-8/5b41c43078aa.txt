Based on the infographic, under "THE GOOD NEWS" section, the two good practices highlighted are:

1. **Read to your children** – A significant factor influencing a child's early educational success is an introduction to books and being read to at home prior to beginning school. *(National Commission on Reading, 1985)*

2. **Be a reading role model** – Children who know adults who read for pleasure take it for granted that reading is a valuable and worthwhile activity. *(Csikszentmihalyi, 1991)*
