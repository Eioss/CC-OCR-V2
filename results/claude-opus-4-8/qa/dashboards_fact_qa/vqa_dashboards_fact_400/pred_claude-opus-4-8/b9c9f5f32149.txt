Based on the infographic, the two types of coronavirus (COVID-19) tests are:

1. **Swab test** — "Do I have it?" — This test determines whether a person currently has the virus.

2. **Antibody test** — "Have I had it?" — This test determines whether a person has previously had the virus. The antibody test comes in two forms:
   - **Laboratory test** (blood test taken by a medical professional)
   - **Home test** ('finger-prick' test taken at home)
