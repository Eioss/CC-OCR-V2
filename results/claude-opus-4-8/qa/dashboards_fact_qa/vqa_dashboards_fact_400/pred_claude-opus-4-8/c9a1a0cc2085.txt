Based on the infographic, the **Louisiana Purchase** is regarded as one of the greatest real estate deals in history.

In 1803, President Thomas Jefferson led the acquisition of the Louisiana Territory (approximately 828,000 square miles of land owned by the French) for a mere **$15 million**. This deal doubled the size of the United States overnight, making it one of the greatest real estate bargains in history—especially considering that the land is now part of 15 different states.
