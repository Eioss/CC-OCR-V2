Based on the infographic, the **Swab test** ("Do I have it?") can be requested through the NHS website.

The infographic states under the "Can I get tested now?" section for the swab test:

*"Anyone experiencing symptoms can ask for a test through the NHS website, and priority testing is available to essential workers."*

The swab test involves taking a sample from the throat and nose to determine if a person currently has the virus.
