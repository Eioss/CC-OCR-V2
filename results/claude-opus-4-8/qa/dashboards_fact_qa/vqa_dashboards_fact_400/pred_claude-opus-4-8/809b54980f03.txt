Based on the infographic, **canned dog food** has a longer shelf life than dry dog food.

According to the "Best If Used By: Storing Dog Food" section:

- **Canned dog food** (left): Shelf life (unopened) is **18–24 months** from date packaged
- **Dry dog food** (right, shown in the bag): Shelf life (unopened) is **12–15 months** from date packaged

So canned food lasts longer when unopened. However, it's worth noting that once **opened**, the situation reverses:
- Canned food (refrigerated, <40°F) keeps for only **3 days**
- Dry food (stored <80°F in a cool, dry place) keeps for **90 days**
