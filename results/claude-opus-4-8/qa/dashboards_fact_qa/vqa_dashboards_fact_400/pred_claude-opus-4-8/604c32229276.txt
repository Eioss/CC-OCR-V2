According to the infographic, the shelf life for dry dog food (unopened) is **12-15 months** from the date packaged.

Additional storage information for dry dog food shown in the infographic:
- **Once opened, store at:** <80° F
- **Once opened, keeps for:** 90 days (store in a cool, dry place)

This is compared to canned dog food, which has a longer unopened shelf life of 18-24 months but a much shorter post-opening lifespan of only 3 days (refrigerated).
