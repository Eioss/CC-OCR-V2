According to this infographic, **10%** of people want to go to a restaurant as the first thing they'd do "when this is all over."

However, I should clarify an important point: this is **not** based on the world population. The infographic states at the bottom that it's based on a survey of **N = 500** people (from a 2020 Pureprofile survey). So this 10% represents the responses from that specific sample of 500 survey participants, not the global population.

The infographic also shows that **32%** of respondents said hugging family & friends would be the first thing they'd want to do.
