2. **Electricity** - showcased at the Palace of Electricity, with Thomas Edison overseeing its presentation to the public
3. **The Hot Dog** - created when Bavarian sausage seller Anton Feuchtwanger ran out of gloves and had his brother-in-law make a sausage-sized bun
