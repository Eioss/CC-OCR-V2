According to the infographic, **Anzac biscuits** were the biscuits used in WWI soldier rations.

The infographic explains under the "ANZAC BISCUITS" section that "The original biscuits used in WWI soldier rations were hard and savoury." Anzac biscuits are closely associated with the Australian and New Zealand Army Corps (ANZAC), and they were made eggless because eggs were scarce during the war, with syrup used as the binding agent instead.
