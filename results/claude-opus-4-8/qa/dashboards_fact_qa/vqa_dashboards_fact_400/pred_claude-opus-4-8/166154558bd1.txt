The coffee beverage you're referring to is the **Flat White**.

According to the infographic, the Flat White:
- Was reportedly developed in the **1980s** in **Sydney cafes**
- Is a coffee beverage made by pouring microfoam over a double shot of espresso
- Has some competing claims from New Zealand, where some say it was invented by Kiwis rather than Aussies
- Is typically served at 170-220 ml (smaller than a latte, which is served in a 225 ml mug, making the latte milkier)
