Based on the infographic, the **Business Analyst** role requires only one language skill:

**SQL**

This is notably the most limited language requirement among all the data science roles shown in the infographic. The Business Analyst role focuses more on business-oriented skills like:
- Basic tools (e.g. MS Office)
- Data visualization tools (e.g. Tableau)
- Conscious listening and storytelling
- Business Intelligence understanding
- Data modeling

This reflects the Business Analyst's position as a "Change Agent" that acts as an intermediary between business and IT, requiring less technical coding depth than roles like Data Engineer or Data Scientist.
