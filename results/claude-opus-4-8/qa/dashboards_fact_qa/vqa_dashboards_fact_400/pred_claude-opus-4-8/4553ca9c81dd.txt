Based on the infographic, the two Thomas Edison inventions introduced at World Expos were:

1. **The Phonograph** - introduced at the **1878 Paris World Expo**. Edison used tinfoil to record and reproduce sound in his newly invented phonograph.

2. **Electricity** - showcased at the **1904 St. Louis World's Fair**, where the Palace of Electricity was a main attraction, and Edison oversaw its presentation to the public.
