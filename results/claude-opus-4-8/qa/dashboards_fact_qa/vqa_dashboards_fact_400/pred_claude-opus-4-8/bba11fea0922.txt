Based on the infographic, there are **8 top tools and apps** listed in the "Top Tools (And Apps) To Use" section:

1. **Draft** - Allows you to invite people to review your work and provide feedback
2. **Sharelist** - Allows you to capture and save any type of content
3. **Grammarly** - Your very own Spell Check, personal Editor and Grammar tool
4. **Unstuck** - Helps you organize your work and break things down
5. **Zen Writer** - Takes up your entire computer screen and eliminates distractions
6. **Mindnode** - Allows you to create graphical mind maps or tables of contents
7. **Dragon Dictation** - Voice-to-text translation tool
8. **Readability Score** - A website that shows what grade level your writing targets
