Based on the infographic, **dry dog food** can be stored longer once opened.

According to the "Best If Used By: Storing Dog Food" section:

- **Canned dog food** (left): Once opened, keeps for **3 days** (refrigerated)
- **Dry dog food** (right): Once opened, keeps for **90 days** (stored in a cool, dry place)

So dry dog food lasts significantly longer (90 days) compared to canned dog food (only 3 days) after opening.
