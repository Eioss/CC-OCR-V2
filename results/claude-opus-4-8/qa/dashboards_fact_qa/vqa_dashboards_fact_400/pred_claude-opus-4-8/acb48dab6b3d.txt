Based on the infographic, there are **6** foods mentioned in the "Biologically Appropriate Raw Food" (BARF) diet proposed by Australian veterinarian Ian Billinghurst:

1. **Muscle Meat** (on bone)
2. **Bones** (whole or ground)
3. **Organ Meats** (liver, kidney)
4. **Raw Eggs**
5. **Veggies** (broccoli, spinach, celery)
6. **Apples or Other Fruits**
7. **Some Dairy**, such as yogurt

Actually, counting them all, there are **7** foods listed in this diet.
