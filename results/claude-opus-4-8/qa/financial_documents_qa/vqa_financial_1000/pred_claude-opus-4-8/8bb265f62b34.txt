Based on the newspaper article, Lazlo Toth was arrested in Yugoslavia for **"industrial espionage."**

According to the article, he was a Thornton resident and naturalized American citizen who was vacationing in Yugoslavia when he was detained on August 1 and arrested about two weeks later. He was allegedly arrested after it was claimed he took several photographs of a beet-sugar plant in Vrbas, a Belgrade suburb. Toth was charged with industrial espionage and sentenced to seven years after a closed-door trial. The article reports that he was later pardoned by the Yugoslav collective presidency, which included President Tito.
