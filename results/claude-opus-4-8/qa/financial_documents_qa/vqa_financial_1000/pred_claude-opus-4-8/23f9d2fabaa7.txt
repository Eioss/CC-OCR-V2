According to the document, **potassium compounds** are often used as substitutes for sodium compounds.

The relevant text states: "Although potassium compounds are often used as substitutes, four basic sodium compounds may be used in preparing soft drink syrups."

The four basic sodium compounds mentioned that may be used (and for which potassium serves as a substitute) include:
1. **Sodium benzoate** - a food preservative
2. **Sodium chloride (salt)** - for flavoring
3. **Sodium citrate** - an acidity buffer
4. **Sodium saccharin** - a sweetener
