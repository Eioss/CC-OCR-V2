This is a **DISCIPLINE REVIEW LETTER** from the U.S. Department of Health & Human Services, Food and Drug Administration (FDA).

Key details about this letter:

- **Sender:** FDA (Food and Drug Administration), Rockville, MD
- **Recipient:** Mary Jo Pritza, MPH, PharmD, Manager of Regulatory Affairs at Takeda Global Research & Development Center, Inc.
- **Reference number:** NDA 21-073/S-026
- **Received date:** February 19, 2007
- **Subject:** It concerns a supplemental New Drug Application (NDA) for Actos® (pioglitazone HCl) tablets in 15 mg, 30 mg, and 45 mg doses

The letter discusses the FDA's review of the clinical section of the company's response to proposed labeling revisions, specifically addressing the PROactive trial outcomes and the rationale for the FDA's proposed changes to the drug label.
