This is a **MEMORANDUM** (memo).

Key details from the document:
- **From:** Department of State, Agency for International Development, Washington, D.C.
- **To:** Members of Ad Hoc Technical and Scientific Advisory Group
- **From (sender):** Martin J. Forman, WOH/NCF
- **Subject:** "Toward an Effective Program to Combat Malnutrition"

The memorandum discusses a meeting held on June 15, 1967, where a selected group of professionals met in Washington, D.C. to assist the Office of War on Hunger, Nutrition and Child Feeding Service in developing guidelines and program content to combat malnutrition in less developed countries. It outlines four major problem areas:
1. Lack of nutrients in diets
2. Lack of local resources (manpower and material)
3. Lack of awareness and concern
4. Lack of knowledge about the problem and potential solutions
