According to the newspaper article, **Yugoslavia pardons Lazlo Toth**, a Thornton resident who was jailed for espionage.

Specifically, Toth was a naturalized American citizen who had been arrested in Yugoslavia for "industrial espionage" and sentenced to seven years after a closed-door trial. He was accused of taking several photographs of a beet-sugar plant in Vrbas, a Belgrade suburb, while vacationing in Yugoslavia. He worked as Director of research and development for Great Western Sugar Co. in Loveland, Colorado.

The article (from The Denver Post, dated Friday, July 16, 1976) reports that the Yugoslav collective presidency pardoned Toth, though an exact release date had not yet been announced.
