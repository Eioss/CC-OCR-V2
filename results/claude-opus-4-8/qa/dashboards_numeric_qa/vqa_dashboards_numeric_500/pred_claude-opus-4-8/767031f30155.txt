Based on the infographic, the emergency benefits provided to working Canadians under Canada's COVID-19 Economic Response Plan are:

## 1. Emergency Care Benefit
- **For:** Those who must stay home due to COVID-19 and don't have paid sick leave
- **Eligible individuals include:**
  - Those who can't access EI and are sick
  - Those taking care of a child or someone who is sick
- **Amount:** Up to **$900 bi-weekly** for up to **15 weeks**

## 2. Emergency Support Benefit
- **For:** Workers who are **not eligible for EI** and who are facing unemployment and reduced hours
- **Total funding:** Up to **$5 billion** in support

These benefits fall under the broader plan, which includes **$27 billion** in direct support for Canadian workers and businesses, and **$55 billion** to support business liquidity through tax deferrals.
