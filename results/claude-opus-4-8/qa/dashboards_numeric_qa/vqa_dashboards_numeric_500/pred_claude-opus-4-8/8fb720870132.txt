Based on the infographic, the two aspects that should be at their **highest levels during PreMatch** are:

1. **Preparedness** (blue line) - reaches its peak at 100
2. **Fitness** (yellow/tan line) - reaches its peak near the top as well

These two metrics peak right before the match, which represents the goal of the training periodization process. Meanwhile, **Fatigue** (red line) and **Training load** (gray line) drop to their lowest levels during the 2-Day Taper leading into PreMatch, ensuring the athlete is well-rested and optimally prepared to perform.
