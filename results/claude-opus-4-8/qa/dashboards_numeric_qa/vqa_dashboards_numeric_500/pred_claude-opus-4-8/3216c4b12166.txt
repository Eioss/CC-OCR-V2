Based on the infographic:

- In **1969**, 42% of kids biked/walked to school
- In **2001**, 16% of kids biked/walked to school

The reduction is: **42% - 16% = 26 percentage points**

So kids walking or cycling to school decreased by **26 percentage points** from 1969 to 2001.

(If measured as a relative percentage decrease, that would be approximately a **62% drop**: 26 ÷ 42 ≈ 0.619.)
