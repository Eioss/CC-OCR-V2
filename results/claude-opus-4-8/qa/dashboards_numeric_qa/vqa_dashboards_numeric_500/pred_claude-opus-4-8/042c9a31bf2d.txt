Based on the infographic, early childhood education provides two main categories of **long-term benefits**:

## 1. Student Success
- **29% more likely** to graduate from high school
- **44% less likely** to repeat a grade
- **Pass standardized tests more frequently**

## 2. Adult Responsibility
- Adults age 40 who had early childhood education are **more likely to be employed**
- These 40-year-olds also have a **33% higher average income**
- 18-year-olds **without** early childhood education are **70% more likely to be arrested for a violent crime**

In summary, the long-term benefits show that early childhood education leads to better academic achievement during school years and translates into greater employment, higher income, and reduced criminal behavior later in adulthood.
