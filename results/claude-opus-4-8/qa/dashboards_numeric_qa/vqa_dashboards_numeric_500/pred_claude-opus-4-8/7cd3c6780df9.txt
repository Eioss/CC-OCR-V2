Based on the infographic, **Generation Z** is more likely to try new things while shopping online than Millennials.

This is shown in the section "Consumers are experimenting, purchasing items they would not usually purchase online":

- **Gen Z: 61%**
- **Millennials: 52%**
- Overall: 39%

Gen Z had the highest percentage (61%) of respondents who said the lockdown encouraged them to try and buy goods online they had not considered before, compared to 52% of Millennials.
