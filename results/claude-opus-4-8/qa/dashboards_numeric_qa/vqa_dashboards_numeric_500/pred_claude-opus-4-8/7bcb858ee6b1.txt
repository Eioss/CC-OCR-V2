Based on the infographic shown in this COVID-19 situation report from Barbados, the hand washing and sanitizing guidance indicates **20 seconds**.

You can see the "20s" symbol appears in the handwashing section (in the bottom right of the wash your hands steps) and again in the hand sanitizer section, indicating you should:
- **Wash your hands** with soap and running water for **20 seconds**
- When using a 60% or higher alcohol-based sanitizer gel, also rub for **20 seconds**

This 20-second guideline is the standard recommendation for proper hand hygiene to effectively remove germs and viruses.
