## Pumpkin Characteristics & Facts:

1. **Big, Round, and Orange** — These are the defining physical characteristics highlighted in the section title.

2. **Production Volume:** **900 million pounds** of pumpkins are produced in the US for Halloween.

3. **Pie Potential:** That amount could make about **60 million pumpkin pies!**

4. **Seasonal Availability:** **80%** of the total pumpkin supply in the US is available during October.
