The brand being discussed in this infographic is **Starbucks**.

The infographic provides various facts about Starbucks, including:

- **Logo**: A twin-tailed mermaid or siren from Greek mythology
- **Origin of name**: From a character in Herman Melville's classic *Moby Dick*
- **History**: First store opened in Seattle, Washington on March 30, 1971, by Jerry Baldwin, Zev Siegl, and Gordon Bowker; later sold to Howard Schultz in 1988
- **Products**: Whole bean coffee, boxed tea, beverages, baked goods, merchandise, Frappuccinos, and smoothies
- **Cup sizes**: Short, Tall, Grande, Venti, Trenta, and the secret "Demi" size
- **Statistics**: Over 19,555 stores in 58 countries, 87,000 flavor combinations, uses 93 million gallons of milk and 2.3 billion paper cups per year, and over 13,700 employees
- **Expansion**: Plans to open in India in 2013 through a tie-up with the Tata group

It's described as **"the world's largest coffee company and coffeehouse chain."**
