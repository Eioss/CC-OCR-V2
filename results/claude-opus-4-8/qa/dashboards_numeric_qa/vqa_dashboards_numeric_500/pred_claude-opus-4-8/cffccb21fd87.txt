Based on the infographic, there are **3 developmental benefits** of early childhood education:

1. **Social and emotional** - Students develop relationships, gain a sense of security, and show greater participation and cooperation.

2. **Cognitive** - Age-appropriate learning, building on existing skills/knowledge, and reaching important developmental milestones.

3. **Language** - Rapid language development between ages 1-5, developing language skills through interactions, and advancing vocabulary and conversation skills.
