Based on the cricket field shown in the infographic, the area between the wickets (the two sets of stumps) is called the **pitch**.

The pitch is the central strip of the cricket field where most of the action takes place—it's where the bowler delivers the ball to the batsman. In the infographic, this is the lighter-colored rectangular strip running between the two sets of wooden stumps (wickets), and it's referenced in the statistic noting that "A good Test match pitch starts with 32% moisture."
