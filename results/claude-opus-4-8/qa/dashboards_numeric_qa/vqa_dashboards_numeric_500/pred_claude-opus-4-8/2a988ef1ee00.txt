According to the infographic, **Asia** is the most populated continent in the world, with **60%** of the global population living there.

This is shown in the "Where do we all live?" section, where the pie chart breaks down population distribution by continent:
- **Asia: 60%**
- Africa: 14%
- Europe: 11%
- North America: 8%
- South America: 5.3%
- Australia: 0.3%

Note: The infographic shows the most *populated* continent (by total population share), which is Asia. However, the term "densely populated" technically refers to population per unit area, which would be a different measure—but based on the data presented in this infographic, Asia holds the largest share of the world's population.
