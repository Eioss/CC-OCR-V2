Based on the infographic, **Generation Z (Gen Z)** is the age group most concerned about reducing spending on luxury items.

According to the section "Consumers are more concerned with essential everyday items":
- **Over a quarter of Gen Z (27%)** stated their spend on luxury products had reduced
- This compares to **23% of Millennials**
- And **18% of Gen X**
