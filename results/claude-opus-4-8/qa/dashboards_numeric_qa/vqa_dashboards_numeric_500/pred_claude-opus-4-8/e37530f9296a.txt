Based on the infographic, the fatality rate is calculated using the **Bureau of Labor Statistics' formula**. As noted in the fine print at the bottom of the image:

**The fatality rate is per 100,000 full-time workers (or equivalent).**

The formula is:
- The **number of fatal injuries** divided by the **number of hours worked by all employees** during the calendar year, then **multiplied by 200,000,000**.

This standardized approach allows for fair comparison of how dangerous different occupations are, regardless of the total number of workers in each field. The 200,000,000 figure represents the equivalent of 100,000 full-time workers working 2,000 hours per year.
