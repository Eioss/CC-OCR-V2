Based on the infographic, **Coca-Cola** received an honorable mention for upgrading to a plant-based bottle.

Under the heading "CHANGING THE WAY THE WORLD THINKS ABOUT PLASTIC BOTTLES," it explains that Coca-Cola reduces its carbon footprint by using a plant-based bottle composed of sugarcane converted into monoethylene glycol (MEG). This alternative packaging is lighter than the average recyclable bottle but functions just like traditional plastic.
