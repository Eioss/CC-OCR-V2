Based on the infographic, Pinterest can help businesses in **5 unique ways**:

1. **Creates Backlinks To Your Site** - Backlinks are created when you upload pins and when users repin your content, improving search engine page rankings.

2. **Boosts Your Keyword Targeting** - Search engines scan Pinterest for keywords, helping improve your page ranking when keywords are being searched.

3. **Boosts Your Social Signals** - Pins give users the opportunity to generate new social signals that factor into Google and other search engine rankings.

4. **Opportunity for New Content** - Pinterest provides a platform to share fresh, original content that search engines reward.

5. **SEO Image Boost** - Optimizing photos with keyword-rich names and descriptions helps utilize keywords since search engines look for images, not just webpages.
