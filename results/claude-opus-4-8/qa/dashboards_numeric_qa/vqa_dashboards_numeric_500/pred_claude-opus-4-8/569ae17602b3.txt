According to the infographic, **Acupuncture** is the treatment where practitioners use thin needles to change the flow of *qi*, the energy in the body.

The infographic also notes that:
- Acupuncture is thousands of years old and comes from China and other Asian nations
- Needles are inserted into more than 400 specific points
- More than 3 million people use acupuncture in the U.S.
