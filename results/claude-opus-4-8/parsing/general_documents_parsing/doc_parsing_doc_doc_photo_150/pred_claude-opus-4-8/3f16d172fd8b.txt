**Note:** I've used `\ulcorner` and `\urcorner` to represent the half-bracket epigraphic notation marks (⌜ ⌝) used to indicate partially damaged signs. These require the `amssymb` package, which should be added to the preamble:

```latex
\usepackage{amssymb}
```

The transcription preserves the original cuneiform studies notation, including superscript determinatives (like the divine determinative ⁠ᵈ), subscript numbers in transliterations, and the catalog formatting conventions.
