# NCERT Solutions for Class 10 Maths
## Chapter 1 – Real Number Ex-1.1

**1. Express each number as a product of its prime factors:**

**(i) 140**

$$\text{Prime factors of } 140 = 2, 2, 5, 7$$
$$= 2^2 \times 5 \times 7$$

**(ii) 156**

$$\text{Prime factors of } 156 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 13$$
$$= 2^2 \times 3 \times 13$$

**(iii) 3825**

$$\text{Prime factors of } 3825 = 3 \times 3 \times 5 \times 5 \times 17$$
$$= 3^2 \times 5^2 \times 17$$

**(iv) 5005**

$$\text{Prime factors of } 5005 = 5 \times 7 \times 11 \times 13$$

**(v) 7429**

$$\text{Prime factors of } 7429 = 17 \times 19 \times 23$$

---

**2. Find the LCM and HCF of the following pairs of integers and verify that LCM × HCF = product of the two numbers**

**(i) 26 and 91**

$$\text{Prime factors of } 26 = 2 \times 13$$
$$\text{Prime factors of } 91 = 7 \times 13$$

$$\text{HCF of } 26 \text{ and } 91 = 13$$
$$\text{LCM of } 26 \text{ and } 91 = 2 \times 7 \times 13$$
$$= 182$$

$$\text{Product of two numbers} = 26 \times 91 = 2366$$
$$\text{LCM} \times \text{HCF} = 182 \times 13 = 2366$$

$$\text{So, Product of two numbers} = \text{LCM} \times \text{HCF}$$
