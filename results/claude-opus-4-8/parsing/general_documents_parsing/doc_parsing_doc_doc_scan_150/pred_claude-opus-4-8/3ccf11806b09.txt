Smartphone Camera Flash

%==================== GENERAL DESCRIPTION ====================
\section*{\textcolor{blue}{GENERAL DESCRIPTION}}

The AW36526 is a inductorless triple channel flash
\& Indicator 2in1 LED driver that provides a high
level of adjustability within a ultra-small solution
size. The AW36526 includes two current sources
and a current sink which is controlled
independently by I\textsuperscript{2}C-compatible interface. The
AW36526 provides IVFM protection mode to
prevent system reset or shutdown under low
battery condition.

\vspace{0.5em}

The AW36526 is controlled via an I\textsuperscript{2}C-compatible
interface. The main features of the AW36526
include: flash / torch / IR / Indicator current, flash
timeout duration, IVFM and breath features. The
AW36526 also provides hardware flash pin
(STROBE) to control flash event.

\vspace{0.5em}

The device operates over a $-40^\circ$C to $+85^\circ$C
ambient temperature range.

\vspace{0.5em}

The AW36526 is available in small 0.4mm pitch
1.6mm$\times$1.2mm FCQFN-10L package.
