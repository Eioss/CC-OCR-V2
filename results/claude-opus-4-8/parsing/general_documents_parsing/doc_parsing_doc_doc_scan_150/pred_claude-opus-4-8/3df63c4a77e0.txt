# Physics Formula Sheet

## Mechanics

$$x = x_0 + v_{x0}t + \frac{1}{2}a_xt^2 \qquad a_c = \frac{v^2}{r} \qquad |\vec{F}_{spring}| = k\,|\vec{x}|$$

$$v = v_0 + at \qquad \theta = \theta_0 + \omega_0 t + \frac{1}{2}\alpha t^2 \qquad PE_{spring} = \frac{1}{2}kx^2$$

$$v_x^2 - v_{x0}^2 = 2a(x - x_0) \qquad \omega = \omega_0 + \alpha t \qquad T_{spring} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}$$

$$\vec{a} = \frac{\Sigma \vec{F}}{m} = \frac{\vec{F}_{net}}{m} \qquad T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega} = \frac{1}{f} \qquad T_{pendulum} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\ell}{g}}$$

$$|\vec{F}_{friction}| \leq \mu\,|\vec{F}_{Normal}| \qquad v = f\lambda$$

$$\vec{p} = m\vec{v} \qquad x = A\cos(2\pi ft) \qquad |\vec{F}_{gravity}| = G\frac{m_1m_2}{r^2}$$

$$\Delta\vec{p} = \vec{F}\Delta t \qquad \vec{\alpha} = \frac{\Sigma\vec{\tau}}{I} = \frac{\vec{\tau}_{net}}{I} \qquad |\vec{F}_{gravity}| = m\vec{g}$$

$$KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 \qquad \vec{\tau} = r \times F \qquad PE_{gravity} = -G\frac{m_1m_2}{r}$$

$$\Delta PE = mg\Delta y \qquad L = I\,\omega \qquad \rho = \frac{m}{V}$$

$$\Delta E = W = Fd\cos\theta \qquad \Delta L = \tau\Delta t \qquad KE = \frac{1}{2}I\,\omega^2$$

## Electricity

$$|\vec{F}_E| = k\left|\frac{q_1q_2}{r^2}\right| \qquad \Delta V = IR \qquad R = \frac{\rho\,\ell}{A}$$

$$I = \frac{\Delta q}{\Delta t} \qquad\qquad P = I\Delta V$$

$$R_{series} = R_1 + R_2 + \ldots + R_n \qquad \frac{1}{R_{Parallel}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \ldots + \frac{1}{R_n}$$

## Trigonometry

$$c^2 = a^2 + b^2 \qquad \sin\theta = \frac{a}{c} \qquad \cos\theta = \frac{b}{c} \qquad \tan\theta = \frac{a}{b}$$

*(Right triangle with hypotenuse $c$, vertical side $a$, base $b$, and angle $\theta$)*

| $\theta$ | $0°$ | $30°$ | $37°$ | $45°$ | $53°$ | $60°$ | $90°$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $\sin\theta$ | $0$ | $1/2$ | $3/5$ | $\sqrt{2}/2$ | $4/5$ | $\sqrt{3}/2$ | $1$ |
| $\cos\theta$ | $1$ | $\sqrt{3}/2$ | $4/5$ | $\sqrt{2}/2$ | $3/5$ | $1/2$ | $0$ |
| $\tan\theta$ | $0$ | $\sqrt{3}/3$ | $3/4$ | $1$ | $4/3$ | $\sqrt{3}$ | $\infty$ |
