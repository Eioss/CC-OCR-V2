$$\int_0^\infty r^{N-1} \sup_{0<s\leq r} \int_{S^{N-1}} |u(s\sigma)|^p \, d\sigma \, dr$$

$$\leq \int_0^\infty r^{N-1} \sup_{0<s\leq r} \left(\int_{S^{N-1}} \frac{|u(s\sigma)|^p}{r^p} \, d\sigma\right)^{\frac{1}{p}} \left(\int_{S^{N-1}} r^{\frac{p}{p-1}} |u(s\sigma)|^p \, d\sigma\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p}} \, dr$$

$$\leq \int_0^\infty r^{N-1} \sup_{0<s\leq r} \left(\int_{S^{N-1}} \frac{|u(s\sigma)|^p}{r^p} \, d\sigma\right)^{\frac{1}{p}} \sup_{0<s\leq r} \left(\int_{S^{N-1}} r^{\frac{p}{p-1}} |u(s\sigma)|^p \, d\sigma\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p}} \, dr$$

$$\leq \left(\int_0^\infty r^{N-1} \sup_{0<s\leq r} \int_{S^{N-1}} \frac{|u(s\sigma)|^p}{r^p} \, d\sigma \, dr\right)^{\frac{1}{p}} \times$$
